羽生结弦（日语：羽生 結弦／はにゅう ゆづる，罗马化：Ha'nyū Yuzuru，1994年12月7日—），日本职业花样滑冰运动员、前日本男子单人滑代表，曾为全日空集团旗下运动员（2013－2022）。。他于2013年至2018年间连续五年位居世界排名第一，是目前唯一达成男子花样滑冰“超级大满贯”的选手。 羽生结弦拥有两届冬季奥运（2014、2018）金牌、两届世界花样滑冰锦标赛（2014、2017）金牌、四届国际滑冰联盟花样滑冰大奖赛总决赛（2013－2016）金牌、一届四大洲花样滑冰锦标赛（2020）金牌、六届全日本花样滑冰锦标赛（2012－2015，2020－2021）金牌、一届世界青少年花样滑冰锦标赛（2010）金牌、一届世界青少年花样滑冰大奖赛总决赛（2009-10赛季）金牌。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「2020年2月9日：羽生結弦」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

Yuzuru Hanyu (羽生 結弦, Ha'nyū Yuzuru; Hiragana: はにゅう ゆづる; born December 7, 1994) is a Japanese figure skater and ice show producer. Widely recognized as one of the greatest figure skaters in history for his well-rounded skills, achievements, innovations, longevity, popularity, and impact on the sport, he started skating at four years old and competed in the men's singles discipline from 2004 to 2022. Hanyu is the first men's singles skater in 66 years since Dick Button to win back-to-back Olympic titles (2014, 2018), and the first Asian Olympic champion in that discipline. He is a two-time World champion (2014, 2017), six-time Japanese national champion (2012–2015, 2020–2021), and the first singles skater to win four consecutive Grand Prix Finals (2013–2016).

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

羽生结弦被国际奥林匹克委员会与国际滑冰联盟公认为史上最伟大的花样滑冰选手之一，是花样滑冰史上首位、也是截至目前唯一在男子单人滑青年组与成年组均达成“主要赛事超级大满贯（Super Slam）”成就的运动员。。十九次打破世界纪录。 羽生是国际滑冰联盟裁判系统（ISU Judging System）于2018/19赛季前，成年组短节目、自由滑及总分的历史世界纪录保持者，也是首位于国际比赛中短节目破100分、自由滑破200分及总分破300分的选手。 2014年，年仅19岁的羽生结弦成为首位夺得花样滑冰男子单人滑奥运金牌的亚洲选手，同时亦是自1948年迪克·巴顿以来最年轻的该项目奥运冠军。2018年，他再次夺金，成为继迪克·巴顿于1948年与1952年连霸后，首位成功卫冕冬季奥运男子单人滑冠军的花样滑冰选手。

At 16 years old, Hanyu experienced the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami in his hometown of Sendai, which fundamentally shaped his life and career, having participated in different charity events and dedicated various performances to the victims of the disaster. He is also the chairperson of Nippon TV's annual 3.11 commemoration ice show Yuzuru Hanyu Notte Stellata, inaugurated in 2023. Since the earthquake, Hanyu has donated a cumulative total of more than $3 million USD for reconstruction, disaster prevention, and other humanitarian efforts. The donations include the full prize money of his Olympic wins and all royalties from his best-selling autobiography series Blue Flames I–IV. In recognition of his achievements, Hanyu became the youngest recipient of the People's Honor Award (2018), bestowed by the Prime Minister of Japan, received Japan's Medal of Honor with purple ribbon twice (2014, 2018) and was awarded the Kikuchi Kan Prize (2022). He is the first figure skater to be nominated for the Laureus World Sports Award (2019) and was named the Most Valuable Skater at the inaugural ISU Skating Awards (2020). He was also featured in prestigious lists, such as Forbes' 30 Under 30 Asia (2018), ESPN's top 25 of greatest Olympians of the 21st century (#10), and the International Sports Press Association's list of most impactful male athletes of the last 100 years (#6) (both 2024). In 2022, he was ranked sixth in the list of most-searched athletes on Google Search worldwide.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

他也在2013-14赛季－2017-18赛季连续54个月积分排名世界第一。羽生是首位在国际比赛中成功跳出后外四周结环跳（4Lo）的选手。 2022年7月19日，羽生正式宣布转为职业运动员，继续从事花样滑冰活动，不再参加竞技比赛。纽约时报称之为“花滑运动一个时代的终结”。 此后羽生在2022-2025年举办了21场个人冰演，而其中2023年2月的"Yuzuru Hanyu ICE STORY 2023 “GIFT”在东京巨蛋举办，成为史上第一位成功在东京巨蛋举行个人单独冰演的花样滑冰运动员。2025年8月宣布为了提升自己的技术暂时停止举办冰演。

At the age of two, Hanyu was diagnosed with asthma, a condition that gradually improved with time yet negatively affected his stamina, especially during his junior career. He began skating at the age of four at Ice Rink Sendai (formerly Konami Sports Club) in Izumi, after coach Mami Yamada had suggested he try the sport instead of being a nuisance during his sister's training. Yamada noted Hanyu's impatience when he first got onto the ice, but also praised him for his sincerity. Coaching him until the end of his second grade in elementary school, Yamada had to move to another prefecture and asked Shōichirō Tsuzuki, former coach of Japan's first World medalist, Minoru Sano, to train Hanyu and "not put his talent to waste". Hanyu described Tsuzuki's practice sessions as particularly strict and exhausting, tempting him to skip lessons at times, but he appreciated Tsuzuki's approach to build a solid foundation of skills and focus on basic training, noting: "He placed so much emphasis on skating and the Axel jump. Perhaps that made me confident to this day that the Axel is my forte." Novice and junior career (2004–2010)

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

羽生在2023年8月4日日本时间23:11分于自己的官方Twitter宣布结婚。同年11月17日晚间宣布因媒体跟踪骚扰压力而决定离婚。 羽生结弦于1994年12月出生于日本宫城县仙台市泉区，2岁确诊患有哮喘，4岁时受姐姐沙绫影响开始接触滑冰。自幼目标在奥运会上夺得金牌。2004年第一次参加全国性比赛获得金牌。2011年日本东北大地震发生的时候他正在仙台冰场训练，在未及脱下冰鞋的情况下逃出冰场避离，因家里受到破坏和家人在避难所待了四天。地震后因仙台冰场需要关闭整修而失去训练基地，曾参与60场慈善冰演并借此当作训练机会。同年接受访问时表示希望将来能取得两面奥运金牌，以拥有足够影响力去支援灾区。翌年2012年3月首次参加世锦赛获得铜牌。同年4月开始在加拿大多伦多训练并更换教练为布莱恩·奥瑟。

Hanyu competed for the first time in the 2004–05 season, winning gold at the Japan Championships in the Novice B category, the lower of the two novice level categories. His home rink then closed due to financial issues, forcing him to switch to the Katsuyama Skating Club in Aoba ward, Sendai. The same year, Shōichirō Tsuzuki moved to Yokohama, and Nanami Abe became Hanyu's main coach and choreographer, guiding him until 2012. On weekends, Hanyu travelled three hours from Sendai to Yokohama for additional lessons at Tsuzuki's new skating club. In summer 2006, at 11 years old, Hanyu's confidence showed up when initiating a spin battle against that year's Olympic silver medalist, Stéphane Lambiel, who was known for his world-class spins. Hanyu suffered a disarming defeat, which he remembered as an important career lesson: "After competing against him, I decided to improve my spins as well. You will definitely improve, learning from [the best]." In the 2006–07 season, Hanyu won the bronze medal at the Japan Championships in the Novice A category, which earned him an invitation to the Japan Junior Championships, where he placed seventh. His home rink in Izumi ward eventually reopened in 2007 after being closed for two years. The next season, he placed first at the Japan Championships in the Novice A category and won the bronze medal at the Japan Junior Championships. In 2008–09, Hanyu moved up to junior level and made his international debut in the ISU Junior Grand Prix at the Merano Cup in Italy, where he placed fifth.
