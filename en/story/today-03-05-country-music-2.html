1963年3月5日：乡村音乐March 5, 1963: Country music

乡村音乐（英语：Country music），也称为乡村与西部（英语：Country and Western）或简称为乡村（英语：Country），是一种当代的流行音乐，起源于美国南部与阿巴拉契亚山脉山区。乡村音乐的根源可追溯至1920年代，融合了传统民谣音乐、凯尔特音乐、福音音乐、蓝调及美国民间音乐。1940年代，当乡巴佬音乐地位渐衰时，乡村音乐开始兴起，人们还以统一的术语“乡村音乐”来称呼这种音乐。1970年代，这一称呼更大大普及，并在世界各地取代了“乡村与西部”这一称呼。 乡村音乐这个术语有时还被用来描述许多风格和其他子流派音乐风格。乡村音乐的起源是美国工人阶级的民间音乐，融合了流行歌曲、爱尔兰和凯尔特的小提琴曲、传统英国民谣和美国西部牛仔歌曲，以及来自欧洲移民社区的各种音乐传统。历史纪录上，蓝调音乐模式已被广泛应用于乡村音乐。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「1963年3月5日：乡村音乐」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

Country music, also known as country and western or simply country, is a music genre originating from the United States. It is known for its ballads and dance tunes, identifiable by both traditional lyrics and harmonies accompanied by banjos, mandolins, fiddles, harmonicas, and many types of guitar; either acoustic, electric, steel, or resonator guitars. Once called hillbilly music, the term country music was popularized in the 1940s. Country music was first developed in the South, and then spread throughout the Piedmont, from Louisiana along the Appalachian Mountains to New York. The music is believed to be derived from British folk music, brought to the United States during early waves of immigration. Rooted in American folk music, such as old-time and Southern Appalachian music, many traditions blended to form country music.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

乡村音乐最早出现在1920 年代。当时，融合了阿巴拉契亚音乐、凯尔特民谣和蓝调的“乡巴佬”（hillbilly）音乐开始成形。此时的艺人有被称为“乡村音乐之父”的吉米·罗杰斯、卡特家族等。1930年代到1940年代，广播成为一种流行的娱乐来源，以乡村音乐为特色的谷仓舞蹈节目在南方各地开始流行，同时乡村音乐受到美国南方文化、西部牛仔文化影响渐深。此时的音乐流派包括酒馆乡村乐、重视弦乐器的蓝草音乐，和受爵士乐影响的西部摇摆乐等。知名艺人包括“歌唱牛仔”吉恩·奥特里和汉克·威廉斯等。 1950年代到1960年代，主流乡村音乐产业以田纳西州纳什维尔为中心蓬勃发展。1950年代乡村音乐受到早期摇滚影响形成了洛卡比里，塑造了包括约翰尼·卡什和猫王在内的巨星。1960年代英伦入侵后，逐渐发展成熟的摇滚乐也和乡村音乐融合形成乡村摇滚，影响了老鹰乐队和克利尔沃特合唱团等乐团。 1970年代至1980年代的乡村音乐大约可以分成两支：源自纳什维尔、民间音乐和软摇滚的乡村流行音乐，以源自贝克斯菲尔德音乐、带着对主流乡村音乐反叛意味的叛道乡村。1970年代初期，歌手兼吉他手约翰·丹佛发行了一系列融合乡村和民谣摇滚音乐风格的歌曲，并取得了巨大成功。随侯乡村艺人们的唱片继续在流行音乐排行榜上得到出色表现，而受到叛道乡村的影响，亦衍生出了得州乡村等流派。

Country music has remained an integral part of the American music scene, with a recent revitalization in interest since the early 2020s. In 2023, 45% of Americans reported listening to country music, an increase in the genre's popularity. The main components of the modern country music style date back to music traditions throughout the Southern United States and Southwestern United States, while its place in American popular music was established in the 1930s during the early days of music recording. According to country historian Bill C. Malone, country music was "introduced to the world as a Southern phenomenon." Migration into the southern Appalachian Mountains, of the Southeastern United States, brought the folk music and instruments of Europe and the Mediterranean Basin along with it for nearly 300 years, which developed into Appalachian music. As the country expanded westward, the Mississippi River and Louisiana became a crossroads for country music, giving rise to Cajun music. In the Southwestern United States, it was the Rocky Mountains, American frontier, and Rio Grande that acted as a similar backdrop for Native American, Mexican, and cowboy ballads, which resulted in New Mexico music and the development of western music, and it is directly related to Red Dirt, Texas country, and Tejano music styles. In Oceania, the steel guitar sound of country music has its provenance in the music of Hawaii.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

1980年代中期，一群新创作者开始出现，他们拒绝了广播和排行榜上流行的更为精致的乡村流行音乐，转而使用更传统的乐器及服装风格，是为新传统乡村。到了1990年代，Uncle Tupelo等受到朋克摇滚及另类摇滚影响的乐团形成了另类乡村音乐。虽然不被主流的广播媒体青睐，但另类乡村及Americana等强调根源性的乡村音乐仍在后续数十年持续发展，如克里斯·斯台普顿等人。 2000年后，主流乡村音乐受到其他音乐类型的影响更为显著，知名乡村歌手包括战前女神、卢克·布莱恩、泰勒·斯威夫特等，此时乡村音乐和摇滚、流行等流派的差异主要反映在表演者的服装和歌曲的文化脉络上，而非音乐本身。其他融合流派如乡村说唱也在此时出现，唱片骑师Avicii亦尝试融合电子音乐和乡村、民谣等音乐风格。

The U.S. Congress has formally recognized Bristol, Tennessee, as the "Birthplace of Country Music", based on the historic Bristol recording sessions of 1927. Nashville, Tennessee has also become a common hotspot for many country artists. Historians have also noted the influence of the less-known Johnson City sessions of 1928 and 1929, and the Knoxville sessions of 1929 and 1930. In addition, the Mountain City Fiddlers Convention, held in 1925, helped to inspire modern country music. Before these, pioneer settlers, in the Great Smoky Mountains region, had developed a rich musical heritage. Country music, still known as hillbilly music at the time, gained an increased audience with the invention of the radio in the 1920s. The largest country music radio show was the Grand Ole Opry, aired starting in 1925 by WSM in Nashville and continuing to present day. Okeh Records, a New York City record label began issuing hillbilly records in 1923, eventually followed by Columbia Records in 1924, and RCA Victor Records in 1927. Outside of New York, Atlanta's country music scene was also important launching many early recording artist's career. The steel guitar entered country music as early as 1922, when Jimmie Tarlton met famed Hawaiian guitarist Frank Ferera on the West Coast. Many hillbilly musicians recorded blues songs throughout the 1920s. The first commercial recordings of what was considered instrumental music in the traditional country style were "Arkansas Traveler" and "Turkey in the Straw" by fiddlers Henry Gilliland & A.C.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

乡村音乐出了两位十分知名的畅销歌手。一位是埃尔维斯·普雷斯利，人称猫王，同时也是新音乐类型“摇滚乐”的代表人物。另一位，当代音乐家加斯·布鲁克斯（Garth Brooks），拥有一亿两千八百万专辑销售量，则是美国史上最畅销的歌手。他个人就拥有六张钻石唱片，和披头士乐团并列为拥有最多钻石唱片的艺人。 瑞典已故电子音乐人、唱片骑师和词曲作家“ Avicii ” 的电子音乐就融合了多种乡村、摇滚、爵士等创新的曲风尝试，如歌曲《Wake me up》，融合了乡村蓝调与电音的元素来创作的。

The first commercial recording of what is widely considered to be the first country song featuring vocals and lyrics was Fiddlin' John Carson with "Little Log Cabin in the Lane" for Okeh Records on June 14, 1923. Vernon Dalhart was considered the first country singer to have a nationwide hit in May 1924 with "Wreck of the Old 97." The flip side of the record was "Lonesome Road Blues", was also popularized. In April 1924, "Aunt" Samantha Bumgarner and Eva Davis became the first female musicians to record and release country songs. Many of the early country musicians, such as the yodeler Cliff Carlisle, recorded blues songs into the 1930s. James Gideon "Gid" Tanner, an American old-time fiddler, was one of country music's earliest stars. With his string band, the Skillet Lickers, in the 1920s and 1930s, many early country songs were written and performed. Jimmie Rodgers and the Carter Family are widely considered to be important early country musicians. From Scott County, Virginia, the Carters had learned sight reading of hymnals and sheet music using solfege. Their songs were first captured at a historic recording session in Bristol, Tennessee, on August 1, 1927, where Ralph Peer was the talent scout and sound recordist. A scene in the movie O Brother, Where Art Thou? depicts a similar occurrence in the same timeframe. Rodgers fused hillbilly country, gospel, jazz, blues, pop, cowboy, and folk, and many of his best songs were his compositions, including "Blue Yodel", which sold over a million records and established Rodgers as the premier singer of early country music.
