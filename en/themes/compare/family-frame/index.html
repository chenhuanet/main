家庭与合影Family and Group Portrait

家庭群像是最合作的图像，也是最暴露权力关系的图像。谁站在中间、谁抱着孩子、谁的手搭在谁肩上、谁被挤到边缘，绘画和摄影都要处理这些安排，只是材料和时间不同。The family group is the most cooperative image and the one that most exposes power. Who stands at the centre, who holds the child, whose hand rests on whose shoulder and who is pushed to the edge: painting and photography both arrange these relations, only material and time differ.

并置Pairings

01把《《圣母与圣婴》》（1495）与《1849年10月7日：艾德加·愛倫·坡》（1849）放在一起：相隔约 354 年，一边用木板油画处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Virgin and Child” (1495) beside “October 7, 1849: Edgar Allan Poe” (1849): about 354 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 木板油画, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
