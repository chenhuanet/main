夜与光Night and Light

夜不是没有光，而是光变得有选择。月光、烛火、电灯、跑道灯和舱外阳光，各自制造不同的夜；谁在光里，谁在暗处，决定了夜是被观看还是被管理。Night is not the absence of light but light becoming selective. Moonlight, candle, electric lamp, runway light and sunlight outside a capsule each manufacture a different night; who is lit and who stays dark decides whether night is watched or managed.

01把《《劫经和德瓦钱德拉的卡拉卡查雅故事中的书页：文字（正面）；布拉曼尚蒂夜叉（背面）》》（1279）与《1893：芝加哥的白色城市》（1893）放在一起：相隔约 614 年，一边用蛋彩处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Folio from a Kalpa-sutra and Story of Kalakacharya of Devachandra: Text (recto); Brahmanshanti Yaksha (verso)” (1279) beside “1893: Chicago's White City” (1893): about 614 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 蛋彩, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
