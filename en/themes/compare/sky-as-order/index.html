天空作为秩序Sky as Order

天空从来不只是对象。它可以是神、日历、王朝档案，也可以是轨道和编号。把月亮、天象记录、冥王星和天文照片放在一起，会看到“看见天空”始终是制度问题。The sky is never only an object. It can be a god, a calendar, a dynastic archive, or an orbit and a number. Moon, omen record, Pluto and astronomical photograph together show that seeing the sky is always an institutional question.

并置Pairings

01把《《月光下小酌》》（1189）与《1902年5月17日：安迪基西拉机械装置》（1902）放在一起：相隔约 713 年，一边用绢本水墨处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Drinking in the Moonlight” (1189) beside “May 17, 1902: Antikythera mechanism” (1902): about 713 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 绢本水墨, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
