工业Industry

工厂、矿山、机器、钢轨与建设。工业图像不只是机器照，它也保存劳动、事故和城市化的代价。Factories, mines, machines, rails and construction. Industrial images are not only machine portraits; they keep labour, accidents and the cost of urbanisation.

论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

工业图像不只是机器照，它也保存劳动、事故和城市化的代价。绘画用工厂、矿山、烟囱、钢轨和建设表现生产力的崇高，也把工人画成秩序的一部分；摄影把机器、厂房、工地、污染和劳动者放进可验证的现场。两种媒介都参与制造“进步”的视觉证据。并置时要看，谁被拍成操作机器的人，谁被拍成被机器伤到的人，以及烟囱和废墟为什么总在画面的边缘或中心。Industrial images are not only machine portraits; they keep labour, accident and the cost of urbanisation. Painting uses factory, mine, chimney, rail and construction to express the sublime of productivity and places workers inside order; photography puts machine, workshop, site, pollution and labourer into a verifiable scene. Both help manufacture visual evidence of progress. The pairing asks who is photographed operating the machine, who is photographed injured by it, and why chimney and ruin so often sit at the centre or the edge of the frame.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《千里江山图》》（1113）与《1901年9月3日：皇家展览馆》（1901）放在一起：相隔约 788 年，一边用绢本设色处理“进步图像保存了谁的代价？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains” (1113) beside “September 3, 1901: Royal Exhibition Building” (1901): about 788 years apart, one works through “Whose cost does the image of progress preserve?” in 绢本设色, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
