论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

材料不是背景条件，它直接决定线条和光怎么走。绘画的金箔、矿物颜料、木板、绢纸、湿壁画和版画，各自规定颜色的厚度、笔触的速度和保存的方式；摄影的银版、盐纸、蛋白、胶片、彩色负片和传感器，各自规定曝光、颗粒、色偏和可复制性。两种媒介都在用材料回答“什么可以被看见”。并置时要看，技术选择怎样变成美学选择，又怎样被展览、市场和修复重新解释。Material is not a background condition; it decides how line and light travel. Gold leaf, mineral pigment, panel, silk, fresco and print each prescribe the thickness of colour, speed of brush and mode of survival; daguerreotype, salt print, albumen, film, colour negative and sensor each prescribe exposure, grain, colour shift and reproducibility. Both media answer what can be seen through material. The pairing asks how technical choice becomes aesthetic choice, and how exhibition, market and conservation reinterpret it.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《带受难场景的十字架》》（1225）与《1888年10月14日：路易·普林斯》（1888）放在一起：相隔约 663 年，一边用蛋彩处理“材料如何变成美学与价值？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Crucifix with Scenes of the Passion” (1225) beside “October 14, 1888: Louis Le Prince” (1888): about 663 years apart, one works through “How does material become aesthetics and value?” in 蛋彩, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
