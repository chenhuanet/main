山路与朝圣Mountain Roads and Pilgrimage

山路不是空的风景，它被朝圣者、商队、士兵和游客反复走过。绘画把山画成可望不可即的远方，摄影把路、鞋、客栈和到达时刻变成可证的现场。A mountain road is not empty scenery; pilgrims, caravans, soldiers and tourists walk it again and again. Painting makes the mountain a distant object of desire; photography makes road, shoe, inn and moment of arrival verifiable.
