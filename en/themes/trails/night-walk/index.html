夜行与灯Night Walk and Lamp

从一条被烛光照亮的街，走到有路灯、橱窗和霓虹的城市。夜行路线让绘画的阴影与摄影的曝光交替，看谁在夜里被看见、谁被留在黑暗里。From a street lit by candle to a city of street lamps, shop windows and neon. This night walk alternates painted shadow with photographic exposure, asking who is seen at night and who remains in darkness.
