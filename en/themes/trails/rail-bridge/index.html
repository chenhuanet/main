铁路与桥梁Rail and Bridge

铁路必须过河，桥梁因此成为现代性最集中的形象。钢铁、铆钉、脚手架、火车和乘客把工程、劳动和速度压进同一个画面。A railway must cross water, which makes the bridge the most concentrated image of modernity. Steel, rivet, scaffold, train and passenger press engineering, labour and speed into one frame.
