河流与港口Rivers and Ports

从河口到港口，再沿着码头和仓库走。水把城市连接起来，也把货物、劳工、移民和污染留在岸边。这条路线让绘画的港湾与摄影的港口交替出现。From river mouth to harbour, then along quay and warehouse. Water connects cities and leaves cargo, labour, migrants and pollution on the shore. This route alternates painted harbours with photographed ports.
