战争与大众War and the Masses

战争、宣传、新闻和大众印刷把图像变成动员与见证的工具。绘画用碎裂、抽象和表现主义处理创伤，摄影把前线、废墟、阅兵和难民放进报纸与档案。两种媒介都必须在国家宣传和个人见证之间选择位置，也都可能被审查、摆拍和重新配文。War, propaganda, news and mass print turn images into tools of mobilisation and witness. Painting handles trauma through fracture, abstraction and expressionism; photography puts frontline, ruin, parade and refugee into newspaper and archive. Both media must choose a position between national propaganda and personal witness, and both can be censored, staged and recaptioned.

01把《《罗得斯纪念堂》》（1916）与《1927年1月11日：路易·B·梅耶》（1927）放在一起：相隔约 11 年，一边用油画处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Rhodes Memorial” (1916) beside “January 11, 1927: Louis B. Mayer” (1927): about 11 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 油画, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
