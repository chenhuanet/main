数字与全球Digital and Global

数字文件、卫星、网络和全球展览让图像可以复制、传输、再编辑。绘画面对扫描、打印、屏幕和算法，摄影面对手机、社交平台、卫星和生成式图像。两种媒介都不再能靠材料边界定义自己；真正的问题是，谁控制文件、谁拥有平台、谁还能确认一张图像来自哪里。Digital file, satellite, network and global exhibition let images be copied, transmitted and re-edited. Painting faces scan, print, screen and algorithm; photography faces phone, social platform, satellite and generated image. Neither medium can define itself by material boundary any more; the real question is who controls the file, who owns the platform and who can still confirm where an image came from.

并置Pairings2

01把《《有太阳的抽象风景》》（1990）与《2003年1月23日：国际空间站》（2003）放在一起：相隔约 13 年，一边用布面丙烯处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Abstract Landscape with Sun” (1990) beside “January 23, 2003: International Space Station” (2003): about 13 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 布面丙烯, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
