时代问题Period question

信仰与手抄本Faith and the Manuscript

在摄影出现之前，图像如何把不可见的世界放进可携带的页面？这一期从宗教图像、写本、壁画和朝圣物出发，看绘画怎样用金色、光线和身体姿势制造神圣感。这里没有摄影，也不硬造摄影；要问的是，当图像是信仰的主要载体时，谁有权生产它、复制它、携带它。Before photography, how did images put the invisible world onto a portable page? This period starts from religious image, manuscript, mural and pilgrimage object, and asks how painting made the sacred through gold, light and posture. There is no photography here and none is invented; the question is who had the right to produce, copy and carry images when they were the main vehicle of faith.
