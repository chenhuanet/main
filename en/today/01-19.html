《创造亚当》The Creation of Adam

《创造亚当》，米开朗基罗，1511–1512，盛期文艺复兴，梵蒂冈·西斯廷礼拜堂。Michelangelo compresses the act of creation into two hands about to touch. Adam lies half-awake on green earth, his body only just kindled; God rushes forward through cloud, his mantle swelling like a sail, angels and wind packed around him. A tiny gap remains between the fingertips, and that gap is what charges the whole ceiling.
