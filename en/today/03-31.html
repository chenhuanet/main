《巴利经藏与帕玛莱故事：抄本插图》Extracts from the Pali canon (Tipitaka) and Story of Phra Malai

《巴利经藏与帕玛莱故事：抄本插图》，1795年。东南亚。纸本、墨与颜料；写本；馆藏未著录尺寸。Thai folding book (samut khoi) containing extracts from the Pali canon (Tipitaka) and Story of Phra Malai, the text illustrated with scenes from the jataka tales and the life of the Buddha, with ink, colours and gold on paper, dating to 1795. Side 1 is comprised of openings 1-40 while side 2 contains openings 41-79.
