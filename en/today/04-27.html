《拾穗者》The Gleaners

《拾穗者》展示了三个农妇在收割后弯腰捡拾散落的谷粒。她们的身体几乎折成直角，手触地面；她们身后是干草堆、收割工人和淡金色的天空。画面没有故事，只有反复的劳作和沉重的地平线。The Gleaners shows three peasant women bending to pick up scattered grain after the harvest. Their bodies fold almost to the right angle, hands reaching the ground; behind them stand haystacks, harvesters and a pale gold sky. There is no story, only repeated labour and a heavy horizon.
