《释迦三尊：文殊与普贤》Shakyamuni Triad: Buddha Attended by Manjushri and Samantabhadra (Buddha)

《释迦三尊：文殊与普贤》，1350s，中国绘画，克利夫兰艺术博物馆。Shakyamuni Triad: Buddha Attended by Manjushri and Samantabhadra (Buddha) (釋迦三尊：釋迦摩尼與文殊菩薩普賢菩薩), late 1300s. China, Fuzhou, Fujian Province, Yuan dynasty (1271-1368). Hanging scroll; ink and color on silk; painting: 107 x 46.6 cm (42 1/8 x 18 3/8 in.); overall with knobs: 192.4 x 66.7 cm (75 3/4 x 26 1/4 in.). The Cleveland Museum of Art, Purchase from the J. H. Wade Fund, 2009.342.2
