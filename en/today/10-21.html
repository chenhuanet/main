《水月观音》Water-Moon Kannon

《水月观音》，岳翁蔵丘，c. 1500，室町时代绘画，克利夫兰艺术博物馆。This painting of Kannon, a Buddhist deity of mercy and compassion with many forms. is attributed to Gakuō Zōkyū, a well-known Zen monk who specialized in ink painting.
