1969-07-20 · Sea of Tranquility, Moon1969-07-20 · Sea of Tranquility, Moon

1969 年 7 月 20 日：月面20 July 1969: the lunar surface

人类第一次站到另一个天体上The first time humans stood on another world

1969 年 7 月 20 日（UTC），阿波罗 11 号的鹰号登月舱降落在静海基地。阿姆斯特朗和奥尔德林走上月面，柯林斯留在月球轨道。

这个主题按“出发—月面—痕迹—回望”的顺序，区分登月、月面活动、留下的物证和返回地球后的回望。日期采用 UTC；月面活动的部分照片拍摄于 7 月 21 日 UTC。

On 20 July 1969 (UTC), Apollo 11's Eagle lunar module landed at Tranquility Base. Armstrong and Aldrin walked on the Moon; Collins stayed in lunar orbit.

This story follows departure, the surface, traces and the look back, separating the landing, surface activity, the objects left behind and the return. Dates use UTC; some surface photographs were taken on 21 July UTC.

第 1 章Chapter 1

出发Departure

1969 年 7 月 16 日，土星五号从肯尼迪航天中心发射。尼尔·阿姆斯特朗、巴兹·奥尔德林和迈克尔·柯林斯飞向月球。7 月 20 日，鹰号登月舱降落在静海基地。

On 16 July 1969, Saturn V lifted off from Kennedy Space Center. Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin and Michael Collins flew toward the Moon. On 20 July, Eagle landed at Tranquility Base.

第 2 章Chapter 2

月面The surface

阿姆斯特朗先踏上月面，说出“这是个人的一小步，却是人类的一大步”。奥尔德林随后出舱。两人在月面活动约两个半小时，布置仪器、采集岩石和土壤样本。

Armstrong stepped out first: “That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind.” Aldrin followed. They spent about two and a half hours outside, setting up instruments and collecting rock and soil samples.

第 3 章Chapter 3

痕迹Traces

月面没有风，脚印和装置会长时间保留。两人留下了一块铭牌、一面旗帜、一台地震仪和激光反射器。这些物件成为人类到达另一个天体的物证。

There is no wind on the Moon; footprints and equipment remain for a long time. They left a plaque, a flag, a seismometer and a laser reflector — physical evidence that humans had reached another world.

第 4 章Chapter 4

回望Looking back

柯林斯留在月球轨道上的哥伦比亚号指令舱。阿波罗 11 号返回地球后，人类第一次有了从另一个天体回望地球的完整影像。

Collins stayed in the Columbia command module in lunar orbit. After Apollo 11 returned, humans had for the first time a complete image of Earth seen from another world.
