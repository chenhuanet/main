给宇宙的一封信A letter to the universe

1974 年 11 月 16 日，人类把 1679 位电报发向 M1316 November 1974: 1,679 bits sent toward M13

1974 年 11 月 16 日，波多黎各的阿雷西博望远镜向 M13 方向发出一次 1679 位的无线电信息。它携带的不是照片，而是一组用二进制写成的自我介绍：数字、DNA、人类的身形、太阳系和望远镜本身。信息以光速前进，抵达 M13 需要约 2 万多年。

这不是一次真正的星际对话，更像一次技术演示。但它把人类第一次认真写下的“我们是谁”，交给了宇宙。

这个主题用同一时期从太空回望地球的影像，讲述这封电报所处的时代。阿雷西博望远镜本身不在本站收藏中，图片与事件的关系在图注里逐张说明。

On 16 November 1974, the Arecibo telescope in Puerto Rico sent a 1,679-bit radio message toward M13. It carried not photographs but a binary self-introduction: numbers, DNA, the human figure, the solar system and the telescope itself. Travelling at the speed of light, it will take about 20,000 years to reach M13.

This was less a real interstellar conversation than a technical demonstration. But it handed the universe the first carefully written answer to “who we are”.

This story uses images of Earth seen from space in the same period to place the message in its time. The Arecibo telescope itself is not in this archive; the relationship between each image and the event is explained in its caption.

第 1 章Chapter 1

先离开地表Leaving the surface

1957 年，斯普特尼克把第一颗人造卫星送上天；1961 年，加加林和谢泼德先后进入太空；1965 年，怀特走出双子座 4 号舱门。人类第一次不再只是从地面仰望天空，而是开始从外面看自己。

In 1957 Sputnik put the first artificial satellite in orbit; in 1961 Gagarin and Shepard entered space; in 1965 White opened the hatch of Gemini 4. For the first time humans were no longer only looking up from the ground — they had begun to see themselves from outside.

第 2 章Chapter 2

第一次看见地球Seeing Earth for the first time

1968 年，阿波罗 8 号在绕月时拍到“地出”；1969 年，阿波罗 11 号把人送上月面；1972 年，阿波罗 17 号拍下完整的“蓝色弹珠”。地球第一次成为一张可以被装进画框的肖像。

In 1968 Apollo 8 photographed “Earthrise” while orbiting the Moon; in 1969 Apollo 11 put people on the lunar surface; in 1972 Apollo 17 took the full “Blue Marble”. Earth had become a portrait that could be framed.

第 3 章Chapter 3

1974：把地球写成电报1974: Earth written as a telegram

1974 年 11 月 16 日，波多黎各的阿雷西博望远镜向球状星团 M13 发出一次 1679 位的无线电信息。它用二进制写出数字、DNA 的元素组成、人类的身形、太阳系和望远镜本身。信息以光速前进，抵达 M13 需要约 2 万多年。它更像一次技术演示，而不是一场真正的星际对话。本站没有阿雷西博望远镜的影像；这一章用同一时期从太空回望地球的照片，交代这封电报所处的时代。

On 16 November 1974, the Arecibo telescope in Puerto Rico sent a 1,679-bit radio message toward the globular cluster M13. In binary it described numbers, the elemental makeup of DNA, the human figure, the solar system and the telescope itself. At the speed of light, the message needs about 20,000 years to arrive. It was more a technical demonstration than a true interstellar dialogue. This archive holds no image of Arecibo itself; this chapter uses photographs of Earth from space taken in the same period to set the scene.

第 4 章Chapter 4

信发出之后After the message

1977 年，旅行者 1 号和 2 号带着镀金唱片升空。唱片里有人类的问候、音乐和地球的声音。阿雷西博的信息只有一次，旅行者的唱片则飞得更远。它们都不知道会不会有人收到，但都把“我们在这里”写进了宇宙。

In 1977 Voyager 1 and 2 lifted off carrying the Golden Record: greetings, music and the sounds of Earth. Arecibo's message was sent once; the Voyager records are travelling farther. No one knows whether anyone will receive them, but both wrote “we are here” into the universe.
