1918-11-11 · EuropeNovember 11, 1918 · Europe

1918 年 11 月 11 日：十一点11 November 1918: eleven o'clock

西线停火，城市开始庆祝The Western Front falls silent; cities begin to celebrate

1918 年 11 月 11 日上午 11 点，西线停火生效。第一次世界大战的主要战斗结束，欧洲城市开始庆祝。

这个主题把“停战—城市—军队”放在一起：停战协定结束了战斗，但没有立刻结束战争带来的伤亡、流离和重建。正式和平还要等到 1919 年的凡尔赛条约。

At 11 a.m. on 11 November 1918, the armistice on the Western Front took effect. The main fighting of the First World War ended, and cities across Europe began to celebrate.

This story sets armistice, city and army side by side: the agreement ended the fighting, but did not immediately end the casualties, displacement and reconstruction. Formal peace had to wait for the Treaty of Versailles in 1919.

第 1 章Chapter 1

十一点Eleven o'clock

1918 年 11 月 11 日上午 11 点，西线停火生效。德国与协约国签署停战协定，第一次世界大战的主要战斗结束。停战不是正式和平；正式和约要到 1919 年的凡尔赛条约才签署。

At 11 a.m. on 11 November 1918, the armistice on the Western Front took effect. Germany and the Allies signed the agreement, and the main fighting of the First World War ended. The armistice was not formal peace; the peace treaty was not signed until Versailles in 1919.

第 2 章Chapter 2

城市The city

消息传开后，伦敦、巴黎、纽约、布里斯班都出现了庆祝人群。人们涌上街头，士兵、平民和车辆混在一起。照片里的城市并不轻松，更像是在长期消耗之后突然松开的一口气。

When the news spread, crowds appeared in London, Paris, New York and Brisbane. People poured into the streets; soldiers, civilians and vehicles mixed together. The cities in these photographs do not look carefree so much as suddenly released after a long exhaustion.

第 3 章Chapter 3

军队The army

停火生效后，部分德军开始撤离占领区，美军团也在庆祝。前线的沉默并不意味着一切立刻恢复；伤亡、伤残、饥饿和战后秩序重建，仍将持续很多年。

After the ceasefire, some German units began to withdraw from occupied territory, while American regiments celebrated. Silence at the front did not mean everything immediately returned to normal: casualties, disability, hunger and the rebuilding of order would continue for years.
