1948：柏林空运1948: the Berlin Airlift

6 月 24 日，城市被封锁，飞机开始降落24 June: the city is blockaded, aircraft begin to land

1948 年 6 月 24 日，苏联封锁西柏林。西方盟国用飞机运入食物、煤炭和药品，维持城市运转。1949 年 5 月，苏联解除封锁；空运又持续到 9 月，以确保西柏林有足够的储备。

这个主题按“封锁—空运—冬天”的顺序，讲清柏林空运不是一次飞行，而是一整套持续数月、按分钟计算的后勤系统。

On 24 June 1948, the Soviet Union blockaded West Berlin. The Western Allies flew in food, coal and medicine to keep the city running. In May 1949 the blockade was lifted; the airlift continued until September to ensure West Berlin had enough reserves.

This story follows blockade, airlift and winter, showing that the Berlin Airlift was not a single flight but a logistics system run for months, measured in minutes.

第 1 章Chapter 1

封锁Blockade

1948 年 6 月 24 日，苏联封锁了通往西柏林的水陆通道。西柏林被包围在苏占区内部，粮食、煤炭和药品都依赖外部输入。西方盟国决定用飞机维持城市运转。

On 24 June 1948, the Soviet Union cut the land and water routes into West Berlin. The city was surrounded inside the Soviet zone, dependent on outside supplies of food, coal and medicine. The Western Allies decided to keep the city running by air.

第 2 章Chapter 2

空运Airlift

从 6 月下旬开始，英美等国的运输机不断飞往西柏林。飞机运来面粉、煤、药品和机器零件。最繁忙的时候，每隔几分钟就有一架飞机降落。

From late June, British, American and other transport aircraft flew into West Berlin continuously, carrying flour, coal, medicine and machine parts. At its busiest, an aircraft landed every few minutes.

第 3 章Chapter 3

冬天Winter

1948–1949 年的冬天尤其艰难。煤炭、食物和电力都要靠空运和严格配给维持。西柏林人看着运输机在头顶降落，称它们为“葡萄干轰炸机”。

The winter of 1948–49 was especially hard. Coal, food and electricity depended on the airlift and strict rationing. Berliners watched the transports come in overhead and called them “raisin bombers”.
