1883：布鲁克林大桥通车1883: the Brooklyn Bridge opens

5 月 24 日，两座城市被一座桥连起来24 May: two cities joined by one bridge

1883 年 5 月 24 日，布鲁克林大桥正式通车，纽约与布鲁克林之间第一次有了固定的陆路通道。工程从约翰·罗布林的设计开始，由他的家人和一批工程师接力完成。

这个主题按“设想—建造—通车—之后”的顺序，讲清这座桥是怎样建成的，也记录通车六天后发生的踩踏事件。那些图版和照片今天看起来并不只是工程记录，也是十九世纪城市生活的现场。

On 24 May 1883, the Brooklyn Bridge officially opened, giving New York and Brooklyn a fixed land route for the first time. The project began with John Roebling's design and was carried through by his family and a succession of engineers.

This story follows idea, construction, opening and aftermath, including the crush six days after the opening. The plates and photographs are not only engineering records but scenes of nineteenth-century city life.

第 1 章Chapter 1

一座桥的设想The idea of a bridge

19 世纪初，纽约与布鲁克林隔着东河相望，往来依赖渡船。约翰·罗布林提出用悬索桥连接两岸：两座花岗岩桥塔，钢缆悬吊桥面，主跨约 486 米。这个跨度在当时的桥梁工程里没有先例。

At the start of the nineteenth century, New York and Brooklyn faced each other across the East River and depended on ferries. John Roebling proposed a suspension bridge: two granite towers, steel cables carrying the deck, a main span of about 486 metres. No bridge of that span had been built.

第 2 章Chapter 2

建造Construction

工程在 1869 年前后开工。约翰·罗布林在勘察时脚部受伤，不久后去世；他的儿子华盛顿·罗布林接手，又因沉箱作业患上减压病，长期卧床。此后，艾米莉·沃伦·罗布林代替丈夫往返工地，传递指令。桥塔、钢缆和桥面在十多年里一点点接起来。

Work began around 1869. John Roebling injured his foot during a survey and died soon after; his son Washington took over, then suffered decompression sickness from caisson work and was bedridden. Emily Warren Roebling relayed instructions between her husband and the site. Towers, cables and deck were joined piece by piece over more than a decade.

第 3 章Chapter 3

通车Opening

1883 年 5 月 24 日，布鲁克林大桥正式开放。美国总统切斯特·阿瑟和纽约市长富兰克林·埃德森走过桥面；艾米莉·沃伦·罗布林也在这天过桥。夜间燃放烟花，两岸的船只鸣笛。此后，东河两岸的通勤和货运不再只靠渡船。

On 24 May 1883 the bridge opened. President Chester Arthur and New York mayor Franklin Edson walked across; Emily Warren Roebling crossed that day too. Fireworks were set off at night and ships sounded their horns. Commuting and freight across the East River no longer depended only on ferries.

第 4 章Chapter 4

之后Afterward

通车六天后的 5 月 30 日，桥面发生踩踏。有人在阶梯上跌倒，人群中传出“桥要塌了”的喊声，拥挤导致 12 人死亡、数十人受伤。第二年起，马戏团老板巴纳姆带着象群过桥，用这种方式证明桥的承重。布鲁克林大桥此后一直是纽约的城市象征。

Six days later, on 30 May, a crush occurred on the bridge. Someone fell on the stairs, a shout went up that the bridge was collapsing, and 12 people died and dozens were injured. The following year P.T. Barnum walked a herd of elephants across to prove the bridge could bear the load. The Brooklyn Bridge remained a symbol of New York.
