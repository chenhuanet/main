从地球到月面From Earth to the lunar surface

离开地表之后，我们第一次回头看After leaving the ground, we looked back for the first time

太空影像最动人的地方，不是我们走得多远，而是我们第一次有机会回头看地球。

从 1961 年的一次亚轨道飞行，到 2026 年猎户座上的“地落”，人类在六十五年里把相机带出了大气层，也把“地球”从一个地面词汇变成了一个可以看见的圆面。这个专题按“离开地表 / 第一次回望 / 更远的近处”三章，选 11 帧影像；它们既是科学记录，也是我们重新理解“家”的方式。

The most moving thing about space photography is not how far we went, but that we had a chance to look back at Earth.

From a suborbital flight in 1961 to the “Earthset” seen from Orion in 2026, humans carried cameras out of the atmosphere over sixty-five years, turning “Earth” from a word on the ground into a visible sphere. This story has three chapters — leaving the surface, the first look back, the far nearby — and eleven images. They are scientific records, and also ways of understanding “home” again.

第 1 章Chapter 1

先离开地表Leaving the surface

1961 年，Ham 从太空回来，伸手去拿一个苹果；同年，谢泼德在自由 7 号里等待发射。1965 年，怀特走出舱外。人类先学会了在真空里活下来，才学会从远处看自己。

In 1961 Ham returned from space and reached for an apple; the same year Shepard waited in Freedom 7 for launch. In 1965 White stepped outside the capsule. Humans first learned to survive in vacuum, then learned to see themselves from a distance.

第 2 章Chapter 2

第一次回望The first look back

1968 年，阿波罗 8 号绕过月球背面，地球从月平线上升起。此后一年，人类第一次站到另一颗天体上；再往后，整颗地球被装进一张照片。太空竞赛的镜头，最后拍到的却是“家”。

In 1968 Apollo 8 rounded the far side of the Moon and Earth rose above the lunar horizon. A year later humans stood on another world; later still, the whole Earth was fitted into a single photograph. The camera of the space race ended up photographing “home”.

第 3 章Chapter 3

更远的近处The far nearby

空间站让“离开地表”变成日常，望远镜把视线推得更远。我们看见的星云和星系，是几十亿年前发出的光；而每一次回望地球，都是当下。远处和近处，在太空影像里第一次靠得这么近。

Space stations made “leaving the surface” ordinary; telescopes pushed the view farther. The nebulae and galaxies we see are light emitted billions of years ago; every look back at Earth is the present. In space photography, the far and the near came this close for the first time.
