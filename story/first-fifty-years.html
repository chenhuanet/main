摄影术的前五十年The first fifty years of photography

从银版到快照，光如何学会留下细节From daguerreotype to snapshot: how light learned to keep detail

摄影术的前五十年，真正改变的不是技术，而是谁有资格被看见。

1839 年，达盖尔公布银版法；1889 年，埃菲尔铁塔下的世界博览会成为摄影与城市现代性的展示场。这五十年里，摄影从一门需要化学家、木匠和长时间摆拍的技艺，变成了城市里可以消费的影像。它先让脸留下来，再把世界装进纸里，最后为“随手一拍”准备好了条件。

这不是一部发明史。它更关心：谁走进了照相馆，谁被带到镜头前，谁站在镜头后，以及一张照片在五十年后还能不能回到它的来源。

In the first fifty years of photography, what really changed was not the technology but who was allowed to be seen.

In 1839 Daguerre announced the daguerreotype; in 1889 the world's fair beneath the Eiffel Tower became a showcase of photography and urban modernity. In those fifty years photography moved from a craft requiring chemists, carpenters and long sittings to an image that could be consumed in the city. First it let faces stay; then it put the world on paper; finally it prepared the conditions for “just taking a picture”.

This is not a history of inventions. It asks who entered the studio, who was brought before the lens, who stood behind it, and whether a photograph can still find its way back to its source fifty years later.

第 1 章Chapter 1

先让脸留下来Letting the face stay

1839 年之后，银版照相馆在欧洲和美国的城市里出现。曝光要几十秒，被摄者必须把呼吸放轻，把眼神固定在一个并不存在的点上。最早走进照相馆的，不只是王公和作家，也有神父、士兵、普通女人，以及被殖民者带到镜头前的人。

After 1839 daguerreotype studios appeared in cities across Europe and America. Exposure took tens of seconds; the sitter had to quiet their breath and fix their eyes on a point that did not exist. The first people to enter the studio were not only princes and writers but priests, soldiers, ordinary women, and people brought before the lens by colonial power.

第 2 章Chapter 2

把世界装进纸里Putting the world on paper

银版只有一份，纸负片却可以复制。盐纸和蛋白印相让摄影走出照相馆，进入考古、工程、战争和殖民地的现场。相机不是中立的：谁站在镜头前，谁站在镜头后，往往由当时的权力关系决定。

A daguerreotype existed in one copy; a paper negative could be reproduced. Salt prints and albumen prints took photography out of the studio and into archaeology, engineering, war and colonial sites. The camera was not neutral: who stood before it and who stood behind it was often decided by the power relations of the time.

第 3 章Chapter 3

在快照之前Before the snapshot

1880 年代，干版、轻便相机和工业化印相让摄影更快、更便宜。城市街头、铁路、工地和殖民地港口都进入了镜头。1888 年，柯达推出装好胶卷的便携相机，摄影开始从照相馆走向业余者；快照时代还没有真正到来，但“随手一拍”的条件已经齐了。

In the 1880s dry plates, portable cameras and industrial printing made photography faster and cheaper. City streets, railways, construction sites and colonial ports all entered the frame. In 1888 Kodak introduced a portable camera loaded with film, and photography began to move from the studio to the amateur; the snapshot age had not quite arrived, but the conditions for “just taking a picture” were ready.
