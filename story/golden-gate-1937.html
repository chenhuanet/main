1937：金门大桥通车1937: the Golden Gate Bridge opens

5 月 27 日，渡轮时代结束27 May: the ferry era ends

1937 年 5 月 27 日，金门大桥通车，旧金山与马林县之间第一次有了直接的道路连接。渡轮逐渐退出，桥成为新的城市入口。

这个主题按“海峡—建造—通车”的顺序，讲清这座桥的工程规模，也记录施工中的事故和通车当天的安排。

On 27 May 1937 the Golden Gate Bridge opened, giving San Francisco and Marin County a direct road link for the first time. Ferries faded, and the bridge became the city's new entrance.

This story follows strait, construction and opening, covering the scale of the work, the accidents during construction and the arrangements on opening day.

第 1 章Chapter 1

海峡The strait

金门海峡把旧金山半岛和马林县隔开。在桥建成之前，两岸往来主要靠渡轮。1933 年 1 月，金门大桥开工。工程师约瑟夫·斯特劳斯负责整体工程，欧文·莫罗确定装饰艺术风格的桥塔造型。

The Golden Gate separates the San Francisco peninsula from Marin County. Before the bridge, the two shores depended mainly on ferries. Work began in January 1933. Engineer Joseph Strauss led the project; Irving Morrow shaped the Art Deco towers.

第 2 章Chapter 2

建造Construction

桥塔建在海峡两侧，主跨约 1280 米。施工人员在强风和浓雾中作业，钢缆一点点从一岸拉到另一岸。1937 年 2 月，一段脚手架坠入海峡，十名工人遇难；整项工程共有十一人死亡。桥下设置的安全网救下了十九个人。

The towers stood on either side of the strait, with a main span of about 1,280 metres. Workers laboured in strong wind and thick fog as the cables were drawn from shore to shore. In February 1937 a section of scaffolding fell into the strait, killing ten workers; eleven died on the project in all. A safety net below the bridge saved nineteen lives.

第 3 章Chapter 3

通车Opening

1937 年 5 月 27 日，金门大桥开放。这一天先让行人上桥，第二天机动车通行。罗斯福总统在华盛顿按下电键，作为通车信号。桥身涂的是“国际橙”，在旧金山的雾里更容易被看见。

On 27 May 1937 the bridge opened. Pedestrians crossed first, motor vehicles the next day. President Roosevelt pressed a key in Washington as the opening signal. The bridge was painted “International Orange” so it would be easier to see in San Francisco's fog.
