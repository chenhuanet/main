手上的工作Hands at work

照片留下了动作，却常常没有留下名字Photographs kept the gesture but often not the name

劳动是历史里最容易被漏掉的部分。照片留下了动作：搬运、纺织、采摘、挖掘、浇铸；却常常没有留下名字。

这个专题选 12 帧，从 1851 年的瓷器厂到 1949 年的钢厂。它不打算替劳动者写传记，只想把“工作”本身放回画面中央。

Labour is the part of history most easily left out. Photographs kept the gestures — carrying, weaving, picking, digging, pouring — but often not the names.

This story selects twelve images, from a porcelain works in 1851 to a steel mill in 1949. It does not try to write biographies of workers; it only wants to put “work” itself back at the centre of the frame.

第 1 章Chapter 1

工场与田野Workshop and field

1850 年代，摄影已经能进入工场和田野，但曝光时间仍然要求人停下来。于是最早的劳动影像总带着一种奇怪的静止：手在动，身体却被固定在机器旁。

By the 1850s photography could enter workshops and fields, but exposure times still required people to stop. The earliest images of labour therefore carry a strange stillness: the hands are working, the body is fixed beside the machine.

第 2 章Chapter 2

孩子的手Children's hands

童工照片不是“可怜”的素材，它们是调查证据。Lewis Hine 带着相机进厂，把机器旁的孩子拍下来，让“没有这回事”变得难以说出口。

Photographs of child labour were not “poor thing” material; they were investigative evidence. Lewis Hine took a camera into factories and photographed children beside machines, making “it doesn't happen” hard to say.

第 3 章Chapter 3

钢铁与流水线Steel and the assembly line

1910 年代到 1940 年代，劳动被组织成更大的系统：矿井、运河、兵工厂、钢厂。照片里的动作越来越小，机器越来越大。

From the 1910s to the 1940s, labour was organised into ever larger systems: mines, canals, arsenals, steelworks. In the photographs the gestures grow smaller and the machines larger.
