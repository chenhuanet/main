1998：国际空间站的第一块1998: the first piece of the space station

11 月 20 日，曙光号升空20 November: Zarya lifts off

1998 年 11 月 20 日，曙光号从拜科努尔升空，国际空间站开始组装。三周后，奋进号把团结号送上去，两个舱段在轨道上对接。

这个主题按“第一个舱段—团结号—组装—之后”的顺序，讲清空间站最初是怎样从两个舱段开始的。

On 20 November 1998 Zarya lifted off from Baikonur and assembly of the International Space Station began. Three weeks later Endeavour delivered Unity, and the two modules docked in orbit.

This story follows the first module, Unity, assembly and aftermath, showing how the station began with just two pieces.

第 1 章Chapter 1

第一个舱段The first module

1998 年 11 月 20 日，曙光号功能货舱从拜科努尔发射场升空。它是国际空间站的第一个舱段，由俄罗斯制造、美国出资，负责早期的电源、推进、导航和通信。没有它，后续舱段没有地方对接，也没有电可用。

On 20 November 1998 the Zarya functional cargo block lifted off from Baikonur. It was the first module of the station, built by Russia with American funding, providing early power, propulsion, guidance and communications. Without it, later modules had nothing to dock to and no power.

第 2 章Chapter 2

团结号Unity

1998 年 12 月 4 日，奋进号航天飞机执行 STS-88 任务升空，货舱里装着“团结号”节点舱。乘组包括罗伯特·卡巴纳、弗雷德里克·斯图尔科、杰里·罗斯、詹姆斯·纽曼、南希·柯里和谢尔盖·克里卡列夫。

On 4 December 1998 Endeavour lifted off on STS-88 carrying the Unity node in its payload bay. The crew was Robert Cabana, Frederick Sturckow, Jerry Ross, James Newman, Nancy Currie and Sergei Krikalev.

第 3 章Chapter 3

在轨组装Assembly in orbit

12 月 6 日，航天员用机械臂把团结号与曙光号对接。随后，罗斯和纽曼进行三次舱外活动，把电源线、数据线连接起来。空间站第一次有了两个舱段，也开始有了“组装”这件事。

On 6 December the crew used the robotic arm to dock Unity with Zarya. Ross and Newman then made three spacewalks to connect power and data lines. For the first time the station had two modules, and “assembly” became a thing.

第 4 章Chapter 4

之后Afterward

2000 年 7 月，星辰号服务舱加入；同年 11 月，第一支长期考察组进驻，空间站开始持续有人居住。此后二十多年，来自不同国家的舱段和实验设备陆续对接，国际空间站成为人类在近地轨道上最大的人造结构。

In July 2000 the Zvezda service module joined; in November the first long-duration crew arrived and the station became continuously inhabited. Over the next two decades modules and experiments from many countries docked, and the ISS became the largest human-made structure in low Earth orbit.
