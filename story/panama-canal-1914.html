1914：巴拿马运河通航1914: the Panama Canal opens

8 月 15 日，两片海洋被一条水道连接15 August: two oceans joined by a waterway

1914 年 8 月 15 日，巴拿马运河正式通航。船只从大西洋进入船闸，被抬升到加通湖，再降入太平洋，不再需要绕行合恩角。

这个主题按“两条洋流之间—加通船闸—1914 通航—之后”的顺序，讲清运河的工程逻辑，也说明法国失败、美国接手和巴拿马主权的长期争议。

On 15 August 1914 the Panama Canal officially opened. Ships entered the locks from the Atlantic, were lifted to Gatun Lake and lowered into the Pacific, no longer needing to sail around Cape Horn.

This story follows the two currents, the Gatun Locks, the 1914 opening and aftermath, explaining the engineering and the long disputes over the French failure, the American takeover and Panama's sovereignty.

第 1 章Chapter 1

两条洋流之间Between two currents

巴拿马运河连接大西洋和太平洋，船只不必再绕行南美洲最南端的合恩角。法国在 1880 年代尝试开凿，因疾病、工程困难和资金问题失败；美国在 1904 年接手。

The Panama Canal connects the Atlantic and Pacific, sparing ships the voyage around Cape Horn at the southern tip of South America. France attempted the cutting in the 1880s and failed through disease, engineering difficulties and money; the United States took over in 1904.

第 2 章Chapter 2

加通船闸The Gatun Locks

运河通过一系列船闸把船只抬升到加通湖，再降回海平面。加通船闸是工程的核心部分，混凝土、闸门和机械系统在当时都是巨型工程。

The canal raises ships through a series of locks to Gatun Lake and lowers them back to sea level. The Gatun Locks were the core of the work; the concrete, gates and machinery were gigantic engineering for the time.

第 3 章Chapter 3

1914 通航The 1914 opening

1914 年 8 月 15 日，巴拿马运河正式通航。第一艘正式通过运河的船是 SS Ancon。运河把纽约到旧金山的航程缩短了数千海里，也改变了全球航运的路线。

On 15 August 1914 the canal officially opened. The first ship formally through was SS Ancon. The canal cut thousands of nautical miles from the New York–San Francisco route and changed global shipping lanes.

第 4 章Chapter 4

之后Afterward

运河在 1914 年通航，第一次世界大战也在同年爆发。此后，运河成为全球贸易和军事运输的关键通道，也成为巴拿马与美国关系中长期争议的焦点。

The canal opened in 1914, the year the First World War began. It became a key route for global trade and military transport, and a long-running point of dispute between Panama and the United States.
