1906：旧金山的地动与火1906: San Francisco shakes and burns

4 月 18 日清晨，城市在几十秒内改变18 April, early morning: the city changes in seconds

1906 年 4 月 18 日清晨，旧金山发生强烈地震。水管破裂、建筑倒塌，随后的大火燃烧数日，摧毁了市中心大片街区。

这个主题按“清晨—火—废墟—空中与地面”的顺序，区分地震本身和随后的大火，也把早期航拍、地面救援和城市重建放在同一条时间线上。灾难的死亡人数至今仍有不同估计，正文只采用“约”数，具体以来源页为准。

In the early morning of 18 April 1906, a powerful earthquake struck San Francisco. Water mains broke, buildings collapsed, and fires burned for days, destroying much of the city centre.

This story follows morning, fire, ruins and the view from air and ground, separating the earthquake from the fires that followed and placing early aerial photography, rescue work and reconstruction on one timeline. Estimates of the death toll still differ; the text uses “about” and leaves exact figures to the sources.

第 1 章Chapter 1

清晨Early morning

1906 年 4 月 18 日清晨 5 点 12 分，旧金山发生强烈地震。现代估计震级约为 7.9。城市的水管、街道和建筑在几十秒内被破坏，随后多处起火。

At 5:12 a.m. on 18 April 1906, a powerful earthquake struck San Francisco. Modern estimates put it at about magnitude 7.9. Water mains, streets and buildings were destroyed within seconds, and fires broke out in several places.

第 2 章Chapter 2

火Fire

地震破坏了供水系统，消防队难以控制火势。大火燃烧了数日，吞掉了市中心的大片街区。很多建筑不是被地震震倒，而是被随后的大火烧毁。

The earthquake wrecked the water system, making it hard for firefighters to control the flames. The fires burned for days and consumed large parts of the city centre. Many buildings were destroyed not by the quake but by the fire that followed.

第 3 章Chapter 3

废墟Ruins

市政厅、法院和商业区都遭到严重破坏。照片里的废墟成为这场灾难最直接的证词；同时，关于死亡人数和损失规模的记录，也在之后很多年里被反复修订。

City Hall, the courts and the commercial district were badly damaged. The ruins in these photographs are the most direct testimony of the disaster; at the same time, records of the death toll and losses were revised for years afterward.

第 4 章Chapter 4

空中与地面Air and ground

这场灾难也留下了早期的航拍影像。有人从系留飞艇上拍下整片废墟；地面上，士兵和救援人员在瓦砾间维持秩序、分发物资。城市随后开始重建。

The disaster also left early aerial images. Someone photographed the ruins from a tethered airship; on the ground, soldiers and relief workers kept order and distributed supplies among the rubble. The city then began to rebuild.
