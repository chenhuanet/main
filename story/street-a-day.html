街角的一天A day on the street corner

同一条街，可以是集市、城市、战场和舞台The same street can be a market, a city, a battlefield and a stage

一条街不是背景。它是历史最先把人聚在一起的地方：买卖在这里发生，陌生人在这里经过，庆典和冲突也在这里留下痕迹。

这个专题选 11 帧，从 1860 年代的广州、上海，到 1930 年代的南京路，再到 1989 年的柏林墙。它们不是一部街道史，而是同一条街在不同时间里的几种样子。

A street is not a backdrop. It is where history first gathered people together: buying and selling happened here, strangers passed here, and celebrations and conflicts left their marks here.

This story selects eleven images, from Guangzhou and Shanghai in the 1860s to Nanjing Road in the 1930s and the Berlin Wall in 1989. They are not a history of streets, but several versions of the same street at different times.

第 1 章Chapter 1

街是集市The street as market

在成为“城市”之前，街道先是交换发生的地方。买卖、寒暄、等待、看人，都在同一条路边完成。1860 年代的广州和上海，镜头已经把这些动作记了下来。

Before it became a “city”, the street was first a place of exchange. Buying, greeting, waiting and watching all happened along the same edge. In Guangzhou and Shanghai in the 1860s, the camera already recorded those gestures.

第 2 章Chapter 2

街是城市The street as city

1880 年代以后，街道开始被电车、邮政、百货和路灯组织起来。城市不再只是房子的集合，而是一套让陌生人互相经过的系统。

After the 1880s, streets were organised by trams, post, department stores and street lighting. The city was no longer just a collection of houses but a system for strangers to pass one another.

第 3 章Chapter 3

街是现场The street as scene

街道也是历史发生的地方。街垒、庆典和墙，都把同一条路临时改写成另一种东西。照片留下的不是“背景”，而是人怎样聚在一起。

The street is also where history happens. Barricades, celebrations and walls temporarily rewrite the same road into something else. What the photographs leave is not a “background” but how people came together.
