1968年1月24日：越南战争January 24, 1968: Vietnam War

越南战争是1955年至1975年越南、老挝和柬埔寨发生的大规模冲突，交战双方为北越（越南民主共和国）与南越（越南共和国）及其各自盟友。北越得到苏联与中国的支持，南越则获得美国及其它反共国家的援助。这场冲突是印度支那战争中的第二场，也是冷战期间的一场代理人战争。越南战争既是后殖民时代争取独立的战争之一，也是冷战的重要战场，同时具备内战性质，从一开始，内部武装对抗就是其显著特征。美国直接军事介入从1965年开始升级，直至1973年撤军。战火蔓延至老挝和柬埔寨，最终三国均于1975年成为共产主义国家。 越南的内战与反殖民斗争于1950年国际化。第一次印度支那战争中法兰西联盟战败后，越南的独立地位在1954年日内瓦会议上得到确认，但国家沿北纬17度线被分割：胡志明领导的越盟控制北越，吴廷琰则领导南越，美国接手了对南越的财政与军事支持。北越向南越境内的越南南方民族解放阵线（VC）提供补给，并最终对其进行指挥，越共自1957年起逐步升级游击战。1958年，北越入侵老挝，开辟“胡志明小道”以支持越共叛乱。至1961年，北越已开始秘密派遣越南人民军（PAVN）士兵南下支援叛军。约翰·F·肯尼迪总统在1960年代初增加了美国的介入力度，包括派遣美军顾问团及援助越南共和国陆军（ARVN）。1963年11月1日-2日，吴廷琰在一场由美国支持的南越陆军政变中被杀，进一步加剧了南越的动荡局势。

The Vietnam War (c. 1955 – 30 April 1975) was an armed conflict in Vietnam, Laos, and Cambodia fought between North Vietnam (Democratic Republic of Vietnam) and South Vietnam (Republic of Vietnam) and their allies. North Vietnam was supported by China and the Soviet Union, while South Vietnam was supported by the United States and other anti-communist nations. The second of the Indochina wars, the Vietnam War was a postcolonial war of national liberation, a theater in the Cold War, and a civil war, with civil warfare a defining feature from the outset. Direct US military involvement escalated from 1965, peaked in 1969, and subsequently declined until the complete withdrawal of US forces in 1973. The fighting spilled into the Laotian and Cambodian civil wars, which ended with all three countries becoming communist in 1975.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

继1964年8月2日“东京湾事件”（又称“北部湾事件”）后，美国国会通过北部湾决议案，授权林登·B·约翰逊总统在不正式宣战的情况下扩大军事存在。约翰逊随即对北越发动滚雷行动并部署作战部队，驻越美军人数在1966年激增至18.4万人，1969年达到53.6万人。美军依赖空中优势和压倒性火力，在农村地区实施“搜索与歼灭”战术。共产主义武装则依托乡村与丛林作为隐蔽基地，开展游击战。 1968年，黎笋发动“新春攻势”。虽然此役在战术上遭遇挫败，却助推了美国国内日益高涨的反战浪潮。约翰逊的继任者理查德·尼克松自1969年起推行“越南化”政策，由扩充后的南越陆军承担主要作战任务，美军则逐步撤离。1970年柬埔寨政变后，北越入侵柬埔寨，美越联军随后展开反攻，令柬埔寨内战进一步升级。 受普遍存在的吸毒问题与士气崩溃影响，美军主力于1972年前基本撤出越南。然而，面对北越发动的大规模“复活节攻势”，美军仍通过“后卫行动”为南越陆军提供了关键的空中支援。随着1973年《巴黎和平协约》签署，最后一批美军撤离。此后北越违反协定，血腥战斗持续至1975年春季攻势。在南越阮文绍政权长期腐败与经济困境的削弱下，西贡最终被北越占领，标志着越南战争的结束。1976年，北越与南越正式统一。

Following the Gulf of Tonkin incident in 1964, the US Congress passed a resolution that gave President Lyndon B. Johnson authority to increase military presence without declaring war. Johnson launched a bombing campaign of the north and deployed combat troops, dramatically increasing deployment to 184,000 by 1966, and 536,000 by 1969. US forces relied on air supremacy and overwhelming firepower to conduct search and destroy operations in rural areas. Communist forces relied on guerrilla tactics, using the countryside and jungle as concealed base areas. In 1968, the communists under Lê Duẩn launched the Tet Offensive, which was a tactical defeat but contributed to growing American opposition to the war. Johnson's successor, Richard Nixon, began "Vietnamization" from 1969, which saw the conflict fought by an expanded ARVN while US forces withdrew. The 1970 Cambodian coup d'état resulted in a PAVN invasion and US–ARVN counter-invasion, escalating its civil war. With its ranks degraded by widespread drug abuse and plummeting morale, US troops had mostly withdrawn from Vietnam by 1972. However, American forces provided crucial air support to ARVN against North Vietnam's massive Easter Offensive with the Linebacker Operations. Following the 1973 Paris Peace Accords, the last American forces left. The accords were subsequently violated by North Vietnam, and bloody fighting continued until the 1975 Spring Offensive. Weakened by years of corruption and the economic troubles of South Vietnam's Thiệu regime, Saigon fell to the PAVN, marking the war's end.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

这场战争代价惨重：越南军民死亡人数估计在97万至300万之间。另有约27.5万至31万柬埔寨人、2万至6.2万老挝人以及58,220名美军人员丧生。战争期间暴行累累，包括大规模屠杀（如戊申顺化屠杀、美莱村屠杀）、恐怖主义、无差别轰炸、强奸、酷刑以及越南对高地民族的迫害。南越约20%的丛林被喷洒有毒的橙剂，造成严重健康问题。 战后政治镇压与失误的经济政策引发了“越南船民”潮及更大规模的印度支那难民危机，数百万人逃离印度支那，其中约25万人葬身大海。红色高棉在柬埔寨实施了红色高棉大屠杀，并于1978年引发柬越战争。作为回应，中国出兵入侵越南，中越边境冲突一直持续到1991年。在美国国内，这场战争催生了“越南综合征”——即公众对海外军事干预的强烈抵触情绪；加之水门事件的影响，共同导致了贯穿整个70年代的“信心危机”。

Political repression and flawed economic policies following the war would precipitate the Vietnamese boat people and Indochina refugee crisis, which saw millions leave Indochina, of which about 250,000 perished at sea. The Khmer Rouge carried out the Cambodian genocide, and the Cambodian–Vietnamese War began in 1978. In response, China invaded Vietnam, with border conflicts lasting until 1991. Within the US, the war gave rise to Vietnam syndrome, an aversion to American overseas military involvement, which, with the Watergate scandal, contributed to the crisis of confidence that affected the US throughout the 1970s. Various names have been applied, though Vietnam War is the most commonly used in English. It has also been called the Second Indochina War, as it spread to Laos and Cambodia, the Vietnam Conflict, and colloquially 'Nam. South Vietnam used terms such as Kháng chiến chống Cộng sản (lit. 'Resistance War against the Communists') and Cuộc chiến bảo vệ tự do (lit. 'Fight to Protect Freedom'). North Vietnam at the time, and official histories produced by the Vietnamese government today, refer to it as Kháng chiến chống Mỹ, cứu nước (lit. 'Resistance War against America to save the nation'), or simply the Resistance War against America. Vietnamese both within the country and overseas occasionally refer to it as Chiến tranh Việt Nam, the Vietnam War.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

这场战争在西方通常被称为“越南战争”，此外也有其他多种称谓：因其蔓延至老挝与柬埔寨，又称“第二次印度支那战争”；亦称“越南冲突”，口语中常简称为“越战”。南越曾称其为“抗共抗战”或“保自由之战”；北越当时及今日越南官方史书则称其为“抗美救国战争”，或“抗美战争”。越南国内及海外侨民偶尔也沿用“越南战争”这一说法。 越南自19世纪80年代起成为法属印度支那的一部分，长期处于法国殖民统治之下。尽管民众对各类改革与革命民族主义事业的支持日益高涨，越南民族主义运动，如越南国民党仍屡遭镇压。胡志明于1930年创立印度支那共产党，这个马克思列宁主义政党旨在推翻法国统治，建立共产主义政权。

Vietnam had been under French control as part of French Indochina since the 1880s. Vietnamese independence movements, such as the Vietnamese Nationalist Party, faced suppression despite growing public support for diverse reformist and revolutionary nationalist causes. Nguyen Sinh Cung established the Indochinese Communist Party (ICP) in 1930; the Marxist–Leninist party aimed to overthrow French rule and establish a communist state. Fractures between nationalists and communists emerged in the late 1920s, as the two groups differed in their visions for postcolonial Vietnam: republicanism for the revolutionary nationalists, and proletarian internationalism for the communists. The communists' radical push for centralized control led to a prolonged civil conflict marked by the suppression of rival nationalists, with the ICP largely responsible for initiating systemic Vietnamese-on-Vietnamese violence.
