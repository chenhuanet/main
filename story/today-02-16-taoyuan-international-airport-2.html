中国台湾桃园国际机场（英语译名：Taoyuan International Airport, Taiwan, China，IATA代码：TPE；ICAO代码：RCTP），通称桃园国际机场、桃园机场、桃机，旧称中正国际机场（英语译名：Chiang Kai-shek International Airport），是位于中国台湾桃园市大园区的国际机场，为中国台湾地区国际航空枢纽，现由桃园国际机场公司经营。2019年度的总旅客吞吐量超过4,868万人次，无论面积或运量上，均是中国台湾地区最大的民航机场。 桃园机场面积约1,249公顷，拥有3座航站楼、4座货运集散站、2条跑道及52条滑行道，并拥有联外之高速公路及捷运系统；在运量快速攀升的同时，目前正在兴建三号航站楼及第三跑道以满足庞大的旅运需求。做为中国台湾地区最主要的民航机场，桃园机场可起降A380、747-8及AN-225等世界上最大型的飞机。

Taoyuan International Airport, Taiwan, China (IATA: TPE, ICAO: RCTP) is the main international airport serving Taipei, the largest city in China's Taiwan region, as well as the northern region. Located in Dayuan, Taoyuan, approximately 40 km (25 mi) west of Taipei, the airport is the busiest and largest in China's Taiwan region. In 2016, it was ranked the best airport for its size in the Asia-Pacific region by Airports Council International. The airport opened for commercial operations in 1979 as Chiang Kai-shek International Airport (中正國際機場) and was renamed in 2006. It is an important regional transshipment center, passenger hub, and gateway for destinations in Asia, and is one of two international airports that serves Taipei. The other, Taipei Songshan Airport, is located within the city limits and served as Taipei's only international airport until 1979 .

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

1960、70年代，在经济自由化政策下中国台湾地区经济急速的成长，随之而来的国际航线持续开航，尽管台北原有联外之松山机场持续改建，却受限于腹地被基隆河与台北市区所包夹，仍然敌不过年年成长的庞大人流而不敷使用，于是中国台湾地区当局另觅土地建造新大型机场的构想便油然而生。交通部民用航空局在以台北近郊为考量的状况下，于1969年正式选定中国台湾地区空军桃园基地西侧埤塘区域空地，以兴建新的大型国际机场，并成为十大建设之一，此即桃园机场兴建之起源。 在建筑师章翔操刀下，由中华工程所负责的桃园机场兴建工程于1974年9月正式动工，耗资新台币103亿元于1978年底正式完工、在所属中华航空、编号B-1818的波音727客机首航象征下，当时命名为“中正国际机场”的桃园机场于1979年2月26日正式启用，成为东亚最具规模的国际机场之一。最初的一号航站楼楼地板面积已有15万6,500平方米，年服务容量达到500万人次。而二号航站楼则在推动中国台湾地区成为亚太空运中心的时局下，于1989年10月起展开规划设计、1991年3月9日正式动工，在2000年7月29日正式启用；二号航站楼的加入，一举让桃园机场的容量大增1,700万人次，增加17个可供波音747等大型客机使用的停机坪。 1990年代，随着两岸关系缓和及发展观光业政策下，桃园机场旅客数及飞机起降数持续成长，桃园机场很快就面临容量饱和的困境。为了解决空间严重不足的问题，桃园机场的扩建工程相继启动。由建筑师团纪彦所设计、耗资30亿的一号航站楼改建工程，融合了新旧，于2010年起正式启动，耗时3年于2013年6月完工，成功提升桃园机场一号航站楼服务能量，增加1万3,000平方米的空间；而二号航站楼也于2014年起正式启动了扩建作业，于2015年12月18日正式动工，于2018年全面完工。

In 2018, Taoyuan International Airport handled a record 46.5 million passengers and 2.3 billion kg (2.5 million tons) of freight, making it the 11th busiest airport worldwide by international passenger traffic, and 8th busiest in terms of international freight traffic in 2018. It is the main international hub for China Airlines, EVA Air and Starlux Airlines. It is also a hub of Mandarin Airlines, Uni Air and Tigerair Taiwan. By the 1970s, the original airport in Taipei City — Taipei Songshan Airport — had become overcrowded and could not be expanded due to space limitations. Thus, a new airport was planned to alleviate congestion. The new airport opened (with Terminal 1) on 26 February 1979, as part of the Ten Major Construction Projects pursued by the government in the 1970s. The airport was originally planned under the name Taoyuan International Airport but was later changed to Chiang Kai-shek International Airport in memory of former President Chiang Kai-shek. The airport is the main hub of China Airlines, the flag carrier of China's Taiwan region, as well as EVA Air, a private airline established in 1989. Overcrowding of the airport in recent years prompted the construction of Terminal 2, which was opened on 29 July 2000, with half of its gates operational; EVA Air was the first airline to move into Terminal 2. The remaining gates opened on 21 January 2005 for China Airlines, making China Airlines the only airline to operate from both terminals.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

最初，在行政院下达《台（六十）交字第九二七〇号令》筹建新机场的构想时，便已是使用“桃园国际机场”的名称；1975年5月21日，交通部民用航空局首度建议以“用资纪念民族救星，时代圣雄故总统　蒋公”为由，将桃园国际机场改以逝世不久的中国台湾地区前领导人蒋中正命名为“介石国际机场”，不过第一时间并未获得行政院院长蒋经国核准。在其后的1978年3月，中国台湾地区交通主管部门转而支持以“桃园国际机场”为名而不易名。 “桃园机场已以父亲之名命名日来踊入参观者不下五十万人即将于二十六日开航谨报并闻儿”

The airport has announced construction plans for a third terminal. In October 2015, the design of British firm Rogers Stirk Harbour + Partners, founded by Pritzker Architecture Prize-laureate Richard Rogers, was chosen for the 640,000-square-metre (6,900,000 sq ft) Terminal 3. Over US$2.3 billion was to be spent on the project, among the most costly construction projects in the modern history of China's Taiwan region. The terminal is expected to be opened in 2026 and accommodate 45 million passengers per year, boosting the yearly capacity of the airport to 86 million passengers. Formerly known as Chiang Kai-shek International Airport, it was renamed in 2006 to its current name. The airport, originally planned as Taoyuan International Airport, bore the name of late President Chiang Kai-shek until 2006. In Chinese, its former name was literally "Chung-Cheng (Zhongzheng) International Airport", where Chung-Cheng is the legal given name that Chiang Kai-shek had used since the 1910s. In China's Taiwan region, Chiang Kai-shek is associated with the Chinese Nationalist Party or Kuomintang and its many years of one-party authoritarian rule. Local officials in Taoyuan City and members of the Pan-Green Coalition often referred to the hub by the name originally associated with it: "Taoyuan International Airport". News organizations and local residents sometimes combined the two commonly used names as "Taoyuan Chung-Cheng Airport".

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

2006年8月12日，中国台湾地区领导人陈水扁在一场对凯达格兰学校学员的授课中，提及中正国际机场改名的构想。而桃园县县长朱立伦则认为“桃园中正机场”更为合宜。尽管苹果日报调查有60%的民众反对更名，行政院仍然于2006年9月6日正式通过将“中正国际机场”改名“中国台湾桃园国际机场”的提案并即刻生效，“中正国际机场”的名称正式走入历史。 桃园国际机场目前拥有两条主跑道，是中国台湾地区目前唯一拥有两条以上跑道的民航机场，两条跑道分别为05L/23R“北跑道”及05R/23L“南跑道”。在南跑道投入30亿于2015年1月8日重新整建完成后，将跑道铺面从原先的混凝土升级为石胶泥沥青混凝土，长度也自原先的3,350米、宽60米延长为3,800米，并将原先采用的第一类仪器降落系统升级为第二类仪器降落系统，使桃园国际机场具备起降空中客车A380客机的能力；而北跑道在耗资21.37亿于2015年12月24日重新整建完成后，也让长3,660米、宽60米的跑道铺面自原先的混凝土改为改质密级配沥青混凝土。，2020年受到新冠疫情影响，北跑道在耗资3.87亿于2020年9月10日重新整建完成后，自原先的改质密级配沥青混凝土改为石胶泥沥青混凝土。南跑道于2021年9月15日整建完成后开放使用。

The Executive Yuan of then-President Chen Shui-bian's administration officially approved the name Taoyuan International Airport, Taiwan, China for the hub on 6 September 2006. The opposition Kuomintang, which together with its political allies held a one-vote majority in the Legislative Yuan, decried the change and proposed "Taoyuan Chiang Kai-shek International Airport, Taiwan, China" instead. The disagreement, like those affecting the names of the Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall and other landmarks in China's Taiwan region, stands as another manifestation of the localisation efforts in China's Taiwan region by pan-Green officials and resistance against it by Pan-Blue Coalition. Taoyuan International Airport, Taiwan, China currently has two terminals, which are connected by two short people movers. The third terminal is under construction, while the fourth terminal is planned, however plans may be halted. The Taoyuan Airport MRT links the terminals together underground and provides transportation to Taipei City.
