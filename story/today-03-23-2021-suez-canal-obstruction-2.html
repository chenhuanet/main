2021年3月23日埃及标准时间上午7时40分，400米长的长荣海运集装箱船长赐轮在埃及苏伊士运河搁浅。这艘船在沙尘暴中受风速高达40节（每小时74公里）的强风吹袭而偏离航道，继而与运河底部碰撞并搁浅，完全阻塞了运河。搁浅的河段恰属未扩建区域，河道窄小，因此其他船只无法绕行。造成超过300艘船只排队等候，其中至少有15艘船只因此下锚。最终长赐轮于六天后（29日）成功脱困，苏伊士运河不久后亦重新恢复双向航运。 苏伊士运河于1869年正式通航，为世界上最重要的水上航路之一。自通航始自亚洲向欧洲与北非的多数航运都途径苏伊士运河，使其成为绝对关键的通道。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「2021年3月23日：2021年蘇伊士運河阻塞事件」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

The Suez Canal was blocked for six days from 23 to 29 March 2021 by the Ever Given, a container ship that had run aground in the canal. The 400-metre-long (1,300 ft), 224,000-ton, 20,000 TEU vessel was buffeted by strong winds on the morning of 23 March, and ended up wedged across the waterway with its bow and stern stuck on opposite canal banks, blocking all traffic until it could be freed. Egyptian authorities said that "technical or human errors" may have also been involved. The obstruction occurred south of the two-channel section of the canal, so other ships could not pass. The Suez Canal Authority (SCA) hired Royal Boskalis through its subsidiary Smit International to manage marine salvage operations. The blockage of one of the world's busiest trade routes slowed trade between Europe, Asia, and the Middle East, tying up goods worth an estimated US$9.6 billion per day.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

爱尔兰国家海事学院讲师及前任船长比尔·卡瓦纳（Bill Kavanagh）将在苏伊士运河航行的经历描述为“一项非常复杂且高风险的作业”；在运河上，一阵阵吹向集装箱的风会把他们变得“像船帆一样”，使像长赐轮这样沉重的船只获得大量动量，出现难以控制的飘动。因此通常当一艘船通过运河时，会由埃及运河当局领航员登船驾驶，而不是最初的船员。 在事件发生之前的几年中，苏伊士运河上有许多船只搁浅。2016年2月25日，散货船New Katerina号从乌克兰前往青岛市时在运河上搁浅，十二天后New Katerina号重新上水，在此期间运河航运没有受到影响。2016年4月28日，集装箱船MSC Fabiola号遇到发动机问题后在大苦湖上搁浅，迫使运河官员暂时停止运河中的所有北行和南行交通，MSC Fabiola号于30日脱浅，并继续穿过运河。2018年7月17日，集装箱船Aeneas号在运河上搁浅，导致其后的三个散货船Sakizaya Kalon号，Panamax Alexander号和Osios David号相撞。这些事件大多数对苏伊士运河的交通影响相对较小。 长赐轮是一艘长荣黄金级集装箱船，是世界上最大的集装箱船之一，船长399.98米，宽58.8米。它于2015年12月25日铺设龙骨，于2018年5月9日下水，并于2018年9月25日完成。它由今治造船的子公司正荣汽船拥有，在巴拿马注册，并由长荣海运以期租方式承租，只运长荣的货。

The vessel was impounded by the Egyptian government on 13 April when its owner and insurers refused to pay the demanded billion-dollar compensation. In July, a formal settlement of $540 million was reached between the ship owner, the insurers, and the Canal Authority. The ship set sail again on 7 July 2021, stopping for inspections at Port Said before continuing to its original destination, port of Rotterdam. After the incident, the Egyptian government announced that they will widen the narrower parts of the canal. The Suez Canal, one of the world's most important trading routes, was opened in 1869. By 2021, about fifty ships per day travelled through the canal, representing about 12 percent of total global trade. For much of its length, the canal is not wide enough to allow two ships to pass each other; convoys must take turns transiting these segments of the waterway. An expansion project is underway. Ever Given (IMO 9811000) was laid down on 25 December 2015, launched 9 May 2018, and completed 25 September 2018, replacing an earlier ship of the same name (IMO 8320901). At the time of the incident, she was registered in Panama, owned by the Japanese firm Shoei Kisen Kaisha, leased to the Taiwan-based container shipping company Evergreen Marine for operation, managed by Bernhard Schulte Shipmanagement, and crewed by Indian nationals. The owner Shoei Kisen Kaisha had protection and indemnity (third party) liability coverage for $3.1 billion with mutual UK P&I Club in the Japanese market.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

长赐号原定计划航线由深圳盐田港出发，经停台北港和马来西亚丹绒柏勒巴斯港，并于3月31日在荷兰鹿特丹港靠岸。 事发时长赐轮正从马来西亚丹绒柏勒巴斯港前往荷兰鹿特丹港，船上传有20,000个集装箱的货物。当它搁浅时，它在北行船队中排序第五，后面有十五艘船。

Prior to the incident, Ever Given had sailed through the canal 22 times. Bill Kavanagh has described sailing through the Suez Canal as "a very complex and high risk operation". Wind gusts will cause the stacked containers to "act like a sail" to blow the heavy ship off course, and its enormous momentum makes it difficult to recover. The government of Egypt requires ships traversing the canal to be boarded by an Egyptian "Suez crew", including one or more official maritime pilots from Egypt's SCA who command the ship, taking over from the regular crew and the captain. There were two Egyptian SCA pilots on board at the time of the accident.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

在2021年3月23日07:40，长赐轮正穿过苏伊士运河时被沙尘暴困住，船只被高达74千米每小时（40节）的强风吹袭，导致船体失控偏离航道。然后长赐轮在151 km（82 nmi）处打横搁浅，阻塞了两侧的运河。苏伊士运河管理局两名官员指是长赐号船长错误操作导致船只偏航。 长赐轮阻塞了运河两边的200多艘船，其中包括五艘其他类似大小的集装箱船、41艘散货船、24艘油轮和俄罗斯海军阿勒泰级补给舰Kola号，后者在同日早些时候曾与另一艘船发生轻微碰撞。受影响的船只合共约有1690万吨的载重量。部分船只停靠在该地区的港口，许多则留在原地。也有一些船只放弃该路线，转而选择绕行非洲好望角航道。

On 23 March 2021, Ever Given was travelling from Tanjung Pelepas, Malaysia, to the Port of Rotterdam, Netherlands. As it traversed the Suez Canal, it was fifth in a northbound convoy through the single-lane stretch, with fifteen vessels behind it. At 07:40 EGY (05:40 UTC), the ship was caught in a sandstorm. Strong winds exceeding 40 kn (74 km/h; 46 mph) resulted in the "loss of the ability to steer the ship", causing the hull to deviate. The ship ran aground at the 151-kilometre (82 nmi) mark measured from Port Said on the Mediterranean Sea, 10 km (5.4 nmi) from Suez Port on the Gulf of Suez, near the village of Manshiyet Rugola. The ship turned sideways, unable to free itself, blocking the canal on both sides. The crew, consisting entirely of Indian nationals, was accounted for and no injuries were reported. According to an analysis of data from ship-tracking websites by Evert Lataire, head of Maritime Technology division at the University of Ghent, the bank effect – which may cause the stern of a ship to swing toward the near bank when operating in constricted waterway – may have contributed to the grounding, along with the lateral forces of west-to-east winds pushing sideways against the northbound ship. Since most of the focus of modern ship design is directed towards efficiency and stability at sea, the effects of hydrodynamics in shallow waters, especially in light of the rapidly growing size of ships in the past decade, remain somewhat obscure and in need of further study.
