2023年6月2日：2023年印度奥迪沙邦列车相撞事故June 2, 2023: 2023 Odisha train collision

2023年6月2日晚间7时20分许，三列火车于印度奥迪沙邦巴拉索尔县相撞，造成291人死亡、1175人受伤。此次事故成为印度24年以来最严重的火车事故。On 2 June 2023, three trains collided in Balasore district in the East Indian state of Odisha.

2023年6月2日晚间7时20分许，三列火车于印度奥迪沙邦巴拉索尔县相撞，造成291人死亡、1175人受伤。此次事故成为印度24年以来最严重的火车事故。 事故发生于当地时间2023年6月2日晚间7时20分许，一列从加尔各答开往金奈的客运列车在奥迪沙邦巴拉索尔市巴哈纳贾火车站附近的豪拉-钦奈干线脱轨，导致车厢翻倒，部分车厢落在对向轨道上。随后有一列由班加罗尔开往加尔各答的客运列车从对向轨道驶来，撞上先前脱轨的车厢，造成该列车的几节车厢也脱轨，而先前脱轨的车厢又撞上一列货运列车的车厢。两列客运列车共有17节车厢严重受损。事故造成至少291人死亡、1,175人受伤。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「2023年6月2日：2023年印度奥迪沙邦列车相撞事故」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

### 补记

还需要补上一层：历史写作最难的地方，不是把事件说得热闹，而是把它的尺度说准。一个日期之所以能进入“历史上的今天”，往往因为它同时具备可被记录的动作、可被辨认的现场和可被后来者继续追问的后果。照片会褪色，报纸会变黄，建筑会被改建，但这些问题不会自动消失。它们会在城市更新、技术迭代和公共记忆的争论中一次次返回。也正因如此，重读这一天，应当同时保留两种眼光：一种贴近现场，看见具体的人在具体的时间做出具体的选择；另一种拉远距离，看见这些选择如何被制度、市场和偶然性放大。两种眼光互相校正，历史才不至于变成单纯的赞歌或单纯的清算。我们把这些图像放在一起，是希望读者先看见，再判断；先理解，再纪念。

On 2 June 2023, three trains collided in Balasore district in the East Indian state of Odisha. The accident occurred around 19:00 IST when Coromandel Express, a passenger train, collided with a stationary goods train near Bahanaga Bazar railway station on the Howrah–Chennai main line. Due to the high speed of the passenger train and the heavy tonnage of the goods train, the impact resulted in 21 coaches of the Coromandel Express derailing, three of which collided with the oncoming SMVT Bengaluru–Howrah Superfast Express on the adjacent track. The accident killed 296 people and injured more than 1,200. It was one of the deadliest railway accidents in India. National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) and Odisha Disaster Rapid Action Force (ODRAF) were involved in the search and recovery efforts, assisted by other government agencies and the general public.

缘起Origins

铁路部门宣布向死者家属赔偿100万卢比、重伤者赔偿20万卢比、轻伤者赔偿50,000卢比。此外，总理国家救济基金（PMNRF）将向死者家属提供20万卢比的特惠补偿，向受伤人员提供50,000卢比的特惠补偿。 事故发生后，有58座车站取消停靠、81列火车改道、18列火车停运。原定于6月3日举行的列车“Mumbai CSMT–Madgaon Vande Bharat Express”首航也被取消。奥迪沙邦宣布6月3日为哀悼日。 救援队在翻倒的列车和残骸中并无发现更多幸存者，于6月3日晚间10时许结束搜救行动和开始清理现场。

Preliminary investigation revealed that the Coromandel Express entered a passing loop line instead of the main line at full speed and crashed into the stationary goods train. In the aftermath, Railway Minister stated that a change in electronic interlocking due to an error in electronic signalling, caused the crash. He also said that sabotage was suspected and the railway board had recommended a Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI)-led probe. On 7 July 2023, the CBI arrested three railway officials believed to be responsible for the accident. Despite the statement of the railway authorities that the accident was not a reflection of the safety issues in the system, various questions were raised by journalists, politicians and retired railway employees. The railway lines were not equipped with the Kavach train protection system. It was made aware that a similar signalling error had been reported earlier in February 2023 and a December 2022 report by the Comptroller and Auditor General of India had warned that the safety department of the railways lacked adequate staffing and funding, suffered from misuse of funds and that these could impact the quality of maintenance. On 2 June 2023, the Coromandel Express (Train no:12841) was travelling from Shalimar in Howrah, West Bengal, to Chennai Central in Chennai, Tamil Nadu on the up line of the Howrah-Chennai main line near Bahanaga Bazar railway station. 第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

6月3日，一辆转运脱轨相撞事故伤者的大巴在西孟加拉邦与另一辆中巴车相撞。事故造成车上多人受轻伤。 6月4日，印度铁道部部长表示，火车相撞事故是由于电子联锁发生变化所导致。

At about 19:00 IST, the Coromandel Express, which was intended to pass straight on the up line, was instead wrongly switched to the parallel up loop line, where it collided with a stationary goods train laden with iron ore. Because of the high speed of the passenger train and the weight of the goods train, 21 coaches of the Coromandel Express derailed. The goods train did not derail. Three of the derailed coaches from the Coromandel Express careened into the adjacent track and whip-lashed the tail end of the SMVT Bengaluru–Howrah Express, which was crossing on the down line at the same time. Two unreserved coaches and the brake van of the SMVT Bengaluru–Howrah Express derailed. Indian Railways published the reservation charts for both the passenger trains on its website. The railways and the governments of Odisha, West Bengal and Tamil Nadu issued helpline numbers. According to the Chief Secretary of Odisha, three National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) units, four Odisha Disaster Rapid Action Force (ODRAF) units, more than 15 fire rescue teams, 100 doctors, 200 police personnel and 200 ambulances were mobilised for the rescue operations. Locals assisted in the relief effort, helping the stranded passengers and transporting the injured passengers to the hospital.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

印度总理纳伦德拉·莫迪表示向受影响的人士提供所有可能的援助，称言语无法表达他深深的悲伤，莫迪抵达事故现场和到访巴拉索尔的一家医院后形容事件痛苦，已下达调查指示，有过失之人将被严惩，铁路部门正努力恢复轨道。 美国驻印度大使艾瑞克·贾西迪向受难者家属向事故中丧生人员的家属表示最深切的哀悼会与印度和奥迪沙邦人民站在一起。

The Government of West Bengal sent a team consisting of 40 doctors, nurses and 30 ambulances to assist in the rescue operations. The Government of Tamil Nadu sent a high level delegation comprising two state ministers and three IAS officers to assist the passengers and arranged a treatment facility with 70 beds at Chennai's Rajiv Gandhi Government General Hospital. Search dogs were used to find survivors. The search and recovery operations continued till the afternoon of 3 June. Locals from neighbouring areas helped in donating blood to treat the injured. A total of 296 people died in the crash. Bodies of deceased passengers were taken to a local high school, chosen due to its open spaces and its location close to the crash site. Identification of the bodies was made difficult due to burns and other trauma, leading officials to rely on personal belongings to try to identify the passengers. Most of the casualties were from the first three cars of the Coromandel Express, which included two general category coaches. These unreserved coaches were often the most crowded, as they allowed anyone with the cheapest category ticket to board without a specific seat reserved for them and were frequently used by migrant workers. As the railways had only the names of passengers with reserved seats, this made identification more difficult. There were allegations of bodies being handed over to the wrong families due to difficulties in identification. Subsequently, DNA tests were carried out to try to identify the unclaimed bodies.
