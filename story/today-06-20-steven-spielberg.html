1975年6月20日：斯蒂芬·斯皮尔伯格June 20, 1975: Steven Spielberg

史蒂文·艾伦·斯皮尔伯格，KBE（英语：Steven Allan Spielberg，1946年12月18日—），生于美国辛辛那提，犹太人，美国电影导演、编剧、…Steven Allan Spielberg (; born December 18, 1946) is an American filmmaker.

史蒂文·艾伦·斯皮尔伯格，KBE（英语：Steven Allan Spielberg，1946年12月18日—），生于美国辛辛那提，犹太人，美国电影导演、编剧、电影制作人、慈善家，主要导演作品包括《侏罗纪公园》系列、《夺宝奇兵》（1981年）、《辛德勒的名单》（1993年）和《拯救大兵瑞恩》（1998年）等多部影史经典。除了电影，史蒂文也执导拍摄电视剧，2002年的战争迷你剧《兄弟连》大获好评，该片横扫当年19项艾美奖的提名并夺得第54届黄金时段艾美奖最佳迷你剧集，又在第59届金球奖拿下了最佳迷你剧/电视电影。 他有三部电影，包括《大白鲨》 （1975年） 、《E.T.外星人》 （1982年）与《侏罗纪公园》（1993年），曾打破票房纪录，成为当时最卖座的电影。至今，斯皮尔伯格执导的电影收入在全球粗估逾85亿美元。根据福布斯杂志报导，斯皮尔伯格坐拥净值31亿美元的财产，是目前全世界最富有的电影制作人之一。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「1975年6月20日：斯蒂芬·斯皮尔伯格」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

Steven Allan Spielberg (; born December 18, 1946) is an American filmmaker. A major figure of the New Hollywood era and pioneer of the modern blockbuster, Spielberg is widely regarded as one of the greatest and most influential filmmakers in the history of cinema and is the highest-grossing film director of all time, with his films having earned almost $11 billion worldwide. Among other accolades, he has received three Academy Awards, four Golden Globe Awards, four BAFTA Awards, twelve Emmy Awards, a Tony Award, and a Grammy Award, as well as the AFI Life Achievement Award in 1995, an honorary knighthood in 2001, the Kennedy Center Honor in 2006, the Cecil B. DeMille Award in 2009, the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2015, and the National Medal of Arts in 2023. According to Forbes, he is one of the world's wealthiest celebrities, with a net worth of at least $5.3 billion.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

史蒂文被认为是20世纪美国新浪潮运动的重要人物之一。2006年，首映杂志将斯皮尔伯格列为电影业中最有权威与影响力的人物。《时代》杂志将他列入世纪百大最重要的人物的一员。20世纪末，《生活》杂志将斯皮尔伯格命名为他同代中最有影响力的人物。 1946年12月18日，斯皮尔伯格出生于俄亥俄州辛辛那提市的正统犹太人家庭，祖父来自于乌克兰卡缅涅茨-波多利斯基，祖母来自于苏季尔基夫。。高中时，由于斯皮尔伯格的犹太人外表和血统，他被同学欺负，人们经常对斯皮尔伯格大喊：“肮脏的犹太人”。 少时他已对电影产生兴趣。1965年中学毕业后，他进入加州州立大学长滩分校，修读电影制作艺术学士。然而，在修读期间，斯皮尔伯格在一些片场担任兼职，由剪辑做起一路转为正式导演，进而辍学，因此直到成名多年后，于2002年才完成学位。另外，在1994年，他获得了南加大的荣誉学位。

Spielberg directed the historical war epics Schindler's List (1993)—which has often been listed as one of the greatest films ever made—and Saving Private Ryan (1998), both of which won him the Academy Award for Best Director with the former also earning him Best Picture. He has also directed the science fiction films Jurassic Park (1993)—which became the highest-grossing film at the time—A.I. Artificial Intelligence (2001), Minority Report (2002), War of the Worlds (2005), Ready Player One (2018) and Disclosure Day (2026); the historical dramas Amistad (1997), Munich (2005), War Horse (2011), Lincoln (2012), Bridge of Spies (2015) and The Post (2017); the comedies Catch Me If You Can (2002) and The Terminal (2004); the animated film The Adventures of Tintin (2011); the musical West Side Story (2021); and the family drama The Fabelmans (2022). Spielberg co-founded Amblin Entertainment and DreamWorks Pictures, and he has served as a producer for many successful films and television series, among them Poltergeist (1982), Gremlins (1984), Back to the Future (1985), An American Tail (1986), Who Framed Roger Rabbit (1988), Animaniacs (1993), Pinky and the Brain (1995), Twister (1996), Band of Brothers (2001) and Transformers (2007). Several of Spielberg's works are considered among the greatest films in history, and some are among the highest-grossing ever. Seven of his films have been inducted into the National Film Registry by the Library of Congress as being "culturally, historically or aesthetically significant".

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

在四十年的电影生涯中，斯皮尔伯格曾触及多种主题与类型。斯皮尔伯格早期以拍摄科幻小说与冒险电影为主，其有时聚焦于儿童，被视为现代好莱坞大成本（Blockbuster）电影制作的典型。此时代表作有：《大白鲨》、《夺宝奇兵》、《E.T.外星人》、《侏罗纪公园》等。 斯皮尔伯格首部成名作是1975年的《大白鲨》，这部惊悚片改编自彼得·本奇利的畅销小说。影片讲述了一头大白鲨在夏季度假小镇袭击海滩游客，促使警察局长马丁·布罗迪（罗伊·施奈德饰）在一位海洋生物学家（理查德·德莱弗斯饰）和一位经验丰富的鲨鱼猎人（罗伯特·肖饰）的帮助下展开追捕。《大白鲨》是第一部在公海拍摄的电影，因此拍摄过程十分艰难，尤其是当机械鲨鱼发生故障时。拍摄计划延误了一百天，环球影业甚至威胁要取消制作。出乎意料的是，《大白鲨》取得了成功，打破了美国国内票房纪录，并使斯皮尔伯格不羁夜。

In 1952, his family moved to Haddon Township, New Jersey, after his father was hired by RCA. Spielberg attended Hebrew school from 1953 to 1957, in classes taught by Rabbi Albert L. Lewis. In early 1957, the family moved to Phoenix, Arizona. Spielberg had a bar mitzvah ceremony when he was thirteen. His family was involved in the synagogue and had many Jewish friends. Of the Holocaust, he said that his parents "talked about it all the time, and so it was always on my mind". His father had lost between sixteen and twenty relatives in the Holocaust. Spielberg found it difficult accepting his heritage; he said: "It isn't something I enjoy admitting ... but when I was seven, eight, nine years old, God forgive me, I was embarrassed because we were Orthodox Jews. I was embarrassed by the outward perception of my parents' Jewish practices. I was never really ashamed to be Jewish, but I was uneasy at times." Spielberg was the target of antisemitism: "In high school, I got smacked and kicked around. Two bloody noses. It was horrible." He gradually followed Judaism less during adolescence, after his family had moved to various neighborhoods and found themselves to be the only Jews. Spielberg's parents took him to see Cecil B. DeMille's The Greatest Show on Earth (1952). He had never seen a movie before, and thought they were taking him to the circus. He was terrified by the movie's train crash, and at age 12, recreated it with his Lionel trains and filmed it.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

《大白鲨》荣获奥斯卡最佳剪辑奖（维娜·菲尔兹）、最佳原创配乐奖（约翰·威廉斯）和最佳音效奖（罗伯特·霍伊特、罗杰·赫曼、厄尔·马德里和约翰·卡特）。斯皮尔伯格表示，机械鲨鱼的故障反而成就了一部更好的电影，因为他必须寻找其他方法来暗示鲨鱼的存在。在看过《大白鲨》中非传统的拍摄技巧后，阿尔弗雷德·希区柯克称赞“年轻的斯皮尔伯格”跳出了剧院的视觉动态：“他是我们当中第一个不把舞台拱门视为局限的人。” 斯皮尔伯格执导了《夺宝奇兵》（1981），剧本由劳伦斯·卡斯丹根据乔治·卢卡斯和菲利普·考夫曼的故事改编。他们认为这部电影是对1930年代和40年代系列电影的致敬。本片由哈里森·福特饰演印地安纳·琼斯，凯伦·艾伦饰演玛莉安·雷文伍德。拍摄地点包括夏威夷拉罗谢尔、突尼斯和英国埃尔斯特里制片厂，拍摄过程十分艰辛，但斯皮尔伯格表示这有助于他磨练商业头脑。《夺宝奇兵》票房大获成功，并荣获奥斯卡金像奖最佳艺术指导（诺曼·雷诺兹、莱斯利·迪利和迈克尔·D·福特）、最佳剪辑（迈克尔·卡恩）、最佳艺术指导（比尔·瓦尼、史蒂夫·马斯洛、格雷格·兰德克和罗伊·查曼）、最佳剪辑（比尔·瓦尼、史蒂夫·马斯洛、格雷格·兰德克和罗伊·查曼）及最佳视觉效果奖（理查德·埃德伦德、基特·韦斯特、布鲁斯·尼科尔森和乔·约翰斯顿）。

Spielberg went to the local theater every Saturday. Formative films included Victor Fleming's Captains Courageous (1937), Disney's Pinocchio and Fantasia (both 1940), Akira Kurosawa's Rashomon (1950) and The Seven Samurai (1954), Ishirō Honda's Godzilla, King of the Monsters! (1956), David Lean's Bridge on the River Kwai (1957) and Lawrence of Arabia (1962) ("the film that set me on my journey"), Alfred Hitchcock's The Birds (1963) and Stanley Kubrick's Dr. Strangelove (1964) and 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968) ("I'm still living off the adrenalin that ... I experienced watching that film for the first time.") He attended Arcadia High School for three years. He wrote and directed a science fiction film, Firelight, funded mainly by his father. It was shown in a local theater for one evening and grossed $501 against its $500 budget. He took a tour at Universal Studios. He jumped off the tour bus and hid in a bathroom until it left. Fortunately, "There happened to be avery nice man there named Chuck Silvers who ran the library at Universal. And when I said I jumped off the bus because I wanted to be a movie director, he thought that was terrific." Silver asked to see Spielberg's shorts, and gave him a three-day pass. On the fourth day, he walked up to the studio gates without a pass, and the security guard waved him in: "I basically spent the next two months at Universal Studios ... that was how I became an unofficial apprentice that summer."
