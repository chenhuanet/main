1984年7月23日：凡妮莎·威廉斯July 23, 1984: Vanessa Williams

凡妮莎·林恩·威廉姆斯（Vanessa Lynn Williams，1963年3月18日—），美国歌手、演员及模特儿。她在1983年成为美国首位有非裔血统的美国小姐，1988年发行首张个人专辑《The Right Stuff》。曾获格莱美奖、艾美奖、托尼奖提名。 凡妮莎·威廉姆斯曾演唱迪士尼电影《风中奇缘》的主题曲“Colors of the Wind”（中文版本为“风之彩”，由辛晓琪演唱）。而她最广为人知，也是唯一的一首冠军单曲是“Save the Best for Last”，此曲在1992年3月拿下公告牌单曲榜五周冠军，在该年的单曲年终总排行也获得了第四名的佳绩。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「1984年7月23日：凡妮莎·威廉斯」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

### 补记

还需要补上一层：历史写作最难的地方，不是把事件说得热闹，而是把它的尺度说准。一个日期之所以能进入“历史上的今天”，往往因为它同时具备可被记录的动作、可被辨认的现场和可被后来者继续追问的后果。照片会褪色，报纸会变黄，建筑会被改建，但这些问题不会自动消失。它们会在城市更新、技术迭代和公共记忆的争论中一次次返回。也正因如此，重读这一天，应当同时保留两种眼光：一种贴近现场，看见具体的人在具体的时间做出具体的选择；另一种拉远距离，看见这些选择如何被制度、市场和偶然性放大。两种眼光互相校正，历史才不至于变成单纯的赞歌或单纯的清算。我们把这些图像放在一起，是希望读者先看见，再判断；先理解，再纪念。

Vanessa Lynn Williams (born March 18, 1963) is an American singer, actress, model, producer and dancer. She gained recognition as the first Black woman to win the Miss America title when she was crowned Miss America 1984. She would later resign her title amid a media controversy surrounding nude photographs published in Penthouse magazine. Thirty-two years later, Williams was offered a public apology during the Miss America 2016 pageant for the events. Williams rebounded from the scandal with a successful career as a singer and actress. In 1988, she released her debut studio album The Right Stuff, whose title single saw moderate success as well as "Dreamin'", which peaked at number 8 on the Billboard Hot 100 in the United States in 1989.

缘起Origins

互联网百老汇数据库（IBDB）上Vanessa Williams的资料（英文）

As an actress, Williams enjoyed success on stage and screen. She made her Broadway debut in 1994 with Kiss of the Spider Woman. In 2002, she starred as The Witch in the revival of Stephen Sondheim's Into the Woods that earned her a nomination for the Tony Award for Best Actress in a Musical at the 56th Tony Awards. She starred in the revival of Horton Foote's The Trip to Bountiful in 2013, and the ensemble political farce POTUS: Or, Behind Every Great Dumbass Are Seven Women Trying to Keep Him Alive in 2022. In television, Williams has played Wilhelmina Slater on Ugly Betty (2006–2010) for which she was nominated three times for the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Comedy Series; and Renee Perry on Desperate Housewives (2010–2012). From 2024 to 2026, she starred as Miranda Priestly in the musical The Devil Wears Prada at the Dominion Theatre, London. Vanessa Lynn Williams was born on March 18, 1963, in Tarrytown, New York, a suburb of New York City. She had a birth announcement that read: "Here she is: Miss America". She was raised nearby in Millwood, New York. A paternal great-great-grandfather was William Feilds, an African-American legislator in the Tennessee House of Representatives. Williams is also of English, Welsh, Irish, Finnish, Italian, and Portuguese descent. Her mother Helen Tinch (1939–2024) met her father Milton Augustine Williams Jr. (1934–2006) while both were music education students at Fredonia State Teachers College in the late 1950s. They became elementary school music teachers in separate districts after marriage.

现场The event

Vanessa Williams在互联网电影资料库（IMDb）上的资料（英文）

Williams and her younger brother Chris Williams, who later became an actor, grew up in Westchester County, a predominantly white middle-to-upper-class suburb of New York City. Williams believes she may have been the first African-American student to go from the first grade to the 12th grade in the Chappaqua Central School District. She attended Robert E. Bell Middle School, as did her children years later. Williams revealed that the shop and home economics teachers, Mr. and Mrs. Fink, were still there when her children attended. A child of music teachers, Williams grew up in a musical household, studying classical and jazz dance, French horn, piano, and violin. She was offered the Presidential Scholarship for Drama to attend Carnegie Mellon University during the college application period, one of 12 students to receive it, but decided instead to attend Syracuse University on a different scholarship. In 1981, Williams joined Syracuse's College of Visual and Performing Arts, Department of Drama as a musical theater major. She stayed at Syracuse through her second year until she was crowned Miss America 1984 in September 1983.

In May 2008, Syracuse granted Williams a Bachelor of Fine Arts degree. According to Syracuse News, "Williams earned the remaining credits for her degree through industry experience and her substantial performances on stage and screen." Williams delivered the 2008 convocation address, telling Syracuse seniors to "treasure this moment. These days are irreplaceable and are the beginning of the rest of your life." Williams is most often publicly recognized simply as "Vanessa Williams." There is occasional confusion with the similarly named actress Vanessa E. Williams. It has been reported that Vanessa L. became aware of Vanessa E. in the 1980s when the New York University registrar told her that another, similarly aged student with the same name and from the same state had applied. When Williams appeared as Miss America in a Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, Vanessa E. accidentally received her cheque for the appearance, which she returned.
