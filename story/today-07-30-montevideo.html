1930年7月30日：蒙得维的亚July 30, 1930: Montevideo

蒙得维的亚（西班牙语：Montevideo，发音：[monteβiˈðeo] ）是乌拉圭的首都和最大城市。Montevideo (, US also ; Spanish: [monteβiˈðeo] ) is the capital and largest city of Uruguay.

蒙得维的亚（西班牙语：Montevideo，发音：[monteβiˈðeo] ）是乌拉圭的首都和最大城市。根据2023年的人口普查，该市人口为1,287,452人，约占全国总人口的36.8%，面积为201平方公里（78平方英里）。蒙得维的亚位于该国南部沿海，拉普拉塔河东北岸。 1723年11月，葡萄牙在今蒙得维的亚一带建立了驻军。1724年2月，西班牙士兵布鲁诺·毛里西奥·德·萨瓦拉（Bruno Mauricio de Zabala）出于战略考虑，驱逐了这支葡萄牙驻军，以应对西班牙和葡萄牙在拉普拉塔河地区的争端。虽然没有官方文件记载这座城市的建立，但布鲁诺·毛里西奥·德·萨瓦拉的日记（Diario）中记载，1726年12月24日是这座城市的建城日期，并得到了在场证人的证实。直到1730年1月1日，蒙得维的亚才正式脱离布宜诺斯艾利斯，成为一座独立的城市。1807年，蒙得维的亚曾短暂被英国统治，但最终被击败英国入侵拉普拉塔河地区的西班牙克里奥尔人夺回 。蒙得维的亚是南方共同市场和拉丁美洲贸易联盟（拉丁美洲和加勒比地区贸易联盟）的行政总部所在地，这两个组织是拉丁美洲的主要贸易集团，其地位使其与欧洲的布鲁塞尔相提并论。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「1930年7月30日：蒙得维的亚」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

Montevideo (, US also ; Spanish: [monteβiˈðeo] ) is the capital and largest city of Uruguay. As of the 2023 census, the city proper has a population of 1,287,452, making up about 36.8% of the country's total population, in an area of 201 square kilometers (78 sq mi). Montevideo is situated on the southern coast of the country, on the northeastern bank of the Río de la Plata. A Portuguese garrison was established at the site of modern-day Montevideo in November 1723. The Portuguese garrison was expelled in February 1724 by a Spanish soldier, Bruno Mauricio de Zabala, as a strategic move amidst the Spanish-Portuguese dispute over the platine region. There is no official document establishing the foundation of the city, but the "Diario" of Bruno Mauricio de Zabala officially mentions the date of 24 December 1726 as the foundation, corroborated by presential witnesses.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

“蒙得维的亚”的名称来源有两个版本。一则出自麦哲伦1520年1月的航海日志，其中记载有一座帽子形状的山，航海家自东向西航行时可以看到。此山随后被命名为“我所见之山”（葡萄牙语：Monte vi eu）。这也是西语文献中可以找到的最早的类似蒙得维的亚（Montevideo）的记载。另一版本则相对缺乏史实支撑，但却更广为人知。航海家沿着达普拉塔河（葡萄牙语：Rio da Prata）自东向西航行时，看到的第六座山，就记录下“自东向西的第六座山”（葡萄牙语：Monte VI de Este a Oeste），其葡萄牙语简写即为Monte VI-D-E-O。 1680年至1683年间，葡萄牙在布宜诺斯艾利斯海湾对岸的地区建立了科洛尼亚·杜·萨克拉门托城。直到1723年，西班牙人才开始在蒙得维的亚湾周围的高地上修建防御工事，这座城市才遭到抵抗。1723年11月22日，葡萄牙陆军元帅曼努埃尔·德·弗雷塔斯·达·丰塞卡修建 了蒙得维的亚 要塞。

The 2019 Mercer report on quality of life rated Montevideo first in Latin America, a rank the city has consistently held since 2005. As of 2010, Montevideo was the 19th largest city economy in the continent and 9th highest income earner among major cities. In 2022, it has a projected GDP of $53.9 billion, with a per capita of $30,148. In 2018, it was classified as a beta global city, ranking eighth in Latin America and 84th in the world. Montevideo hosted every match during the first FIFA World Cup in 1930. It has been described as a "vibrant, eclectic place with a rich cultural life", and "a thriving tech center and entrepreneurial culture". The city features historic European architecture and is considered one of the cities with the most art deco influence. It is the hub of commerce and higher education in Uruguay as well as its chief port and financial hub, anchoring a metropolitan area with a population of around 2 million. There are several explanations for the word Montevideo. All agree that "Monte" refers to the Cerro de Montevideo, the hill situated across the Bay of Montevideo, but there is disagreement about the etymological origin of the "video" part.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

一支西班牙探险队从布宜诺斯艾利斯出发，由该市的西班牙总督布鲁诺·毛里西奥·德·萨瓦拉组织。1724年1月22日，西班牙人迫使葡萄牙人放弃该地，并开始在此定居。最初有六个家庭从布宜诺斯艾利斯迁入，随后不久，来自加那利群岛的家庭也陆续抵达，他们被称为关切人或加那利人。此外，还有一位名叫豪尔赫·布尔格斯的意大利早期居民。 蒙得维的亚根据柯本气候分类法属于副热带湿润气候（Cfa）。该市冬季温和（6月至9月），夏季炎热（12月至3月），河流水位多变（10月和11月）。有许多雷暴，但没有热带气旋。全年定期降雨并均匀分布，达到约950毫米（37英寸）。

Monte vide eu ("I saw a mount") is the most widespread belief but is rejected by the majority of experts, who consider it unlikely because it involves a mix of dialects. The name would come from a Portuguese expression which means "I saw a mount", wrongly pronounced by an anonymous sailor belonging to the expedition of Ferdinand Magellan on catching sight of the Cerro de Montevideo. Montem vídeo ("I see a hill"): This version, a variant of the previous one, suggests that the name comes directly from Latin, stemming from the spontaneous expression of a learned member of Magellan's expedition, who, upon spotting the Cerro de Montevideo, exclaimed: Montem vídeo ("I see a hill"). The rest of the crew, who did not speak Latin, mistakenly registered this as the name of the hill they had just sighted, Monte Vídeo. This theory is supported by numerous maps and documents from the colonial period that refer to the Cerro de Montevideo with the name Monte Vídeo.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

蒙得维的亚是乌拉圭职业运动的重心，也是发展最突出的。世纪球场为国家足球场，为了第一届世界杯于1930年启用，以及为纪念乌拉圭的第一部宪法100周年。在首届世界杯乌拉圭以4比2击败阿根廷夺得冠军。球场可容纳70000人。它被列为国际足联世界足球经典体育场馆之一，和马拉卡纳体育场、温布利球场、圣西罗球场、阿兹特克体育场、伯纳乌球场等球场同列。

Monte Vidi: This hypothesis comes from the "Diario de Navegación" (Navigational Calendar) of boatswain Francisco de Albo, member of the expedition of Magellan, who wrote, "Tuesday of the said [month of January 1520] we were on the straits of Cape Santa María [now Punta del Este], from where the coast runs east to west, and the terrain is sandy, and at the right of the cape there is a mountain like a hat to which we gave the name "Montevidi"." This is the oldest Spanish document that mentions the promontory with a name similar to the one that designates the city, but it does not contain any mention of the alleged cry "Monte vide eu." Monte-VI-D-E-O (Monte VI De Este a Oeste, "Sixth mount from east to west"): According to Rolando Laguarda Trías, professor of history, the Spaniards annotated the geographic location on a map or Portolan chart, so that the mount/hill is the VI (6th) mount observable on the coast, navigating Río de la Plata from east to west. With the passing of time, these words were unified to "Montevideo". No conclusive evidence has been found to confirm this academic hypothesis, nor can it be asserted with certainty which the other five mounts observable before the Cerro were.
