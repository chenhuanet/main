1919年8月18日：俄国内战August 18, 1919: Russian Civil War

俄国内战（俄语：Гражданская война в России，罗马化：Grazhdanskaya voyna v Rossii），或称苏联国内战争、苏俄国内战争和对苏干涉战争，是1917年至1922年前俄罗斯帝国境内发生的大规模内战，交战各方包含前俄罗斯帝国各方派系、同盟国和协约国。战争源自布尔什维克在十月革命推翻俄国临时政府，而其领导的俄罗斯苏维埃联邦社会主义共和国最终击败反对势力，并促成苏联成立。 俄罗斯君主制在俄国二月革命期间沙皇尼古拉二世退位后终结，此后俄罗斯陷入政治动荡，最终导致布尔什维克在十月革命推翻新成立的俄国临时政府。然而，其夺取政权并未获得普遍承认，俄罗斯遂陷入武装冲突，并于1918年5月至6月间演变为全面内战。参战的两大主要势力为红军与白军。红军致力于建立由布尔什维克领导、以弗拉基米尔·列宁为领导核心的社会主义国家；白军则主要由原俄罗斯帝国军队中的右派军官领导，并以亚历山大·高尔察克为主要领袖。此外，其他彼此竞争的激进社会主义势力也参与了战争，其中包括乌克兰的马赫诺运动无政府主义者，以及左翼社会革命党。这些势力与非意识形态性的绿军一样，既反对布尔什维克，也反对白军及外国干涉军。共有13个外国国家出兵对抗红军，其中最重要的是协约国的武装干涉，其主要目的在于重建第一次世界大战的东方战线。此外，同盟国也介入战争，与协约国的干涉相抗衡，其主要目的则是保住它们根据布列斯特-立陶夫斯克条约从苏俄取得的领土。

The Russian Civil War was a multi-party civil war in the former Russian Empire, lasting from 1917 to 1922, sparked by the overthrowing of the Russian Provisional Government in the October Revolution, as many factions vied to determine Russia's political future. It resulted in the formation of the Russian Socialist Federative Soviet Republic and later the Soviet Union in most of its territory. Its finale marked the end of the Russian Revolution, which was one of the key events of the 20th century. The Russian monarchy ended with the abdication of Tsar Nicholas II during the February Revolution, and Russia was in a state of political flux. A tense summer culminated in the October Revolution, where the Bolsheviks overthrew the provisional government of the new Russian Republic.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

布尔什维克最初巩固对前帝国大部分地区的控制，而德军则依布列斯特-立陶夫斯克条约占领俄罗斯大片领土。1918年5月，驻俄罗斯的捷克斯洛伐克军团在西伯利亚发动起义。协约国亦开始干涉俄罗斯北部及西伯利亚。与此同时，临时全俄罗斯政府的成立，也使布尔什维克控制的领土缩减至主要包括欧俄地区以及中亚部分地区。1919年，白军分别于3月从东部、7月从南部、10月从西部发动多次攻势，但至1920年初已在三条主要战线上全部被红军击败。与此同时，许多非俄罗斯民族利用帝国崩溃与内战造成的混乱，推动民族独立。1921年3月，苏俄与波兰签署里加和约，将白俄罗斯与乌克兰的争议领土一分为二：一部分归波兰第二共和国，另一部分则归苏俄与乌克兰苏维埃社会主义共和国。苏俄尝试出兵入侵新独立的诸国，或支持当地的布尔什维克及社会主义势力。其对爱沙尼亚、拉脱维亚与立陶宛的攻势均失利，但占领亚美尼亚、阿塞拜疆与格鲁吉亚。至1921年，布尔什维克已击败乌克兰与高加索地区的民族独立运动，但中亚的反布尔什维克起义仍持续至1920年代末期。 高尔察克麾下的军队最终被迫向东撤退。红军虽在赤塔、雅库特以及蒙古等地遭遇抵抗，仍一路向东推进。欧洲方面，红军击溃顿河军与志愿军，迫使白军分别于1920年3月从新罗西斯克撤离，同年11月撤出克里米亚。此后，战事逐渐转为零星冲突，直到1922年10月红军攻占海参崴，战争才正式结束。然而，反布尔什维克的抵抗并未立即停止；中亚的穆斯林巴斯马奇运动以及哈巴罗夫斯克边疆区的反抗活动，一直持续至1934年。俄罗斯内战共造成约700万至1,200万人伤亡，其中大多数为平民。 俄罗斯帝国从1914年起即与法兰西第三共和国和大不列颠及爱尔兰联合王国（三国协约）一起参加了第一次世界大战，对抗德意志帝国、奥匈帝国和奥斯曼帝国（中央同盟）。

The Bolsheviks initially consolidated control over most of the former empire. The Treaty of Brest-Litovsk was an emergency peace with the German Empire, who had captured vast swathes of the Russian territory during the chaos of the revolution. In May 1918, the Czechoslovak Legion in Russia revolted in Siberia. In reaction, the Allies began their North Russian and Siberian interventions. That, combined with the creation of the Provisional All-Russian Government, saw the reduction of Bolshevik-controlled territory to most of European Russia and parts of Central Asia. In 1919, the White Army launched several offensives from the east in March, the south in July, and west in October. The advances were later checked by the Eastern Front counteroffensive, the Southern Front counteroffensive, and the defeat of the Northwestern Army. By 1919, the White armies were in retreat and by the start of 1920 were defeated on all three fronts. Although the Bolsheviks were victorious, the territorial extent of the Russian state had been reduced, for many non-Russian ethnic groups had used the disarray to push for national independence. In March 1921, during a related war against Poland, the Peace of Riga was signed, splitting disputed territories in Belarus and Ukraine between the Republic of Poland on one side and Soviet Russia and Soviet Ukraine on the other. Soviet Russia invaded all the newly independent nations of the former empire or supported the Bolshevik and socialist forces there, although the success of such invasions was limited.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

1917年3月，二月革命迫使沙皇尼古拉二世退位，导致新成立的俄国临时政府与全国各地组织起来的苏维埃（由工人、士兵和农民组成的民选委员会）形成了双重政权的局面。1917年9月，俄罗斯共和国宣布成立。 1917年11月十月革命胜利之后，旧的俄罗斯帝国军队被解散，志愿者组成的赤卫队是布尔什维克主要的军事力量，由布尔什维克政权的安全机构契卡的武装军事部分扩建而来。在战斗中遭遇重挫后，1918年1月，战时人民委员列夫·托洛茨基领导赤卫队改组为工农红军，以创建更专业的武装力量。政治委员被任命到每个支队以维持士气并保持忠诚。

From World War I to the Russian Revolution The Russian Empire fought in World War I from 1914 alongside France and the United Kingdom (Triple Entente) against Germany, Austria-Hungary and the Ottoman Empire (Central Powers).

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

1918年6月，纯粹的工人组成的革命军将会变得弱小的问题凸显出来，托洛茨基在红军中建立了面向乡村地区农民的强制征兵制。俄国农村对红军征兵部队的反抗被劫取人质及为强制执行而实施的枪杀镇压，同样的手法也被白军军官完全运用。革命家以“军事专家”（voenspetsy）的名义启用前沙皇军官，有时为使他们保持忠诚而将其家属劫为人质。战争开始之际，四分之三的红军军官由前沙俄时期的军官组成。战争结束时，所有红军师级和团级83%的指挥官都是前沙皇时期的将士。 白军对红军的反抗开始于布尔什维克起义随后的时日，和德国、奥匈帝国、保加利亚、奥斯曼帝国签订的布列斯特-立陶夫斯克条约和政治禁令成为俄国内外反布尔什维克团体建立的导火线，把他们推进反抗新政权的行动中。

The February Revolution of 1917 resulted in the abdication of Emperor Nicholas II of Russia. As a result, the Russian Provisional Government formed by a coalition of centrist parties was established, and soviets, elected councils of workers, soldiers, and peasants, were organized throughout the country, leading to a situation of dual power. The Russian Republic was proclaimed in September of the same year. The Provisional Government, led by Socialist Revolutionary Party politician Alexander Kerensky, was unable to solve the most pressing issues of the country, most importantly to end the war with the Central Powers. A failed military coup by General Lavr Kornilov in September 1917 led to a surge in support for the Bolsheviks, who took control of the soviets, which until then had been controlled by the Socialist Revolutionaries. Promising an end to the war and "all power to the Soviets", the Bolsheviks then ended dual power by overthrowing the Provisional Government in late October, on the eve of the Second All-Russian Congress of Soviets of Workers' and Soldiers' Deputies, in what would be the second Revolution of 1917. The initial stage of the October Revolution which involved the assault on Petrograd occurred largely without any human casualties. Despite the Bolsheviks' seizure of power, they lost to the Socialist Revolutionary Party in the 1917 Russian Constituent Assembly election, and the Constituent Assembly was dissolved by the Bolsheviks in retaliation.
