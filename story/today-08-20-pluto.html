冥王星（小行星序号 134340）是位于柯伊伯带中的一颗矮行星，该带是位于海王星轨道之外的天体环。它是已知第九大体积、第十大质量，直接绕太阳运行的天体。它是已知体积最大的海王星外天体，但质量比矮行星阋神星还要小。与其它柯伊伯带天体一样，冥王星主要由冰和岩石组成，体积远小于内行星。冥王星的质量约为月球的六分之一，体积约为月球的三分之一。最初被视为行星，但随着天文学家采用新的行星定义，并采用新标准，使其地位有所改变。 冥王星的轨道属于中等偏心和倾角，范围为距离太阳 30至49天文单位（4.5至7.3 × 109千米；2.8至4.6 × 109英里）。以39.5 AU（5.91 × 109 km；3.67 × 109 mi）的距离，太阳光到达冥王星需耗费5.5小时。冥王星的偏心轨道周期性地使其比海王星更接近太阳，但稳定的轨道共振阻止了它们碰撞。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「1909年8月20日：冥王星」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

Pluto (minor-planet number 134340) is a dwarf planet in the Kuiper belt, a ring of bodies beyond the orbit of Neptune. It is the ninth-largest and tenth-most-massive known object to directly orbit the Sun. It is the largest known trans-Neptunian object by volume by a small margin, but is less massive than Eris, which is denser. Like other Kuiper belt objects, Pluto is made primarily of ice and rock and is much smaller than the inner planets. Pluto has roughly one-sixth the mass of the Moon and one-third of its volume. Originally considered a planet, its status was changed when astronomers adopted a new definition of the word with new criteria. Pluto has a moderately eccentric and inclined orbit, ranging from 30 to 49 astronomical units (4.5 to 7.3 billion kilometres; 2.8 to 4.6 billion miles) from the Sun.

缘起Origins

已知冥王星拥有五颗卫星：卡戎（最大且直径略超过冥王星的一半）、斯堤克斯、尼克斯、科伯罗司，和许德拉。冥王星和卡戎有时被视为双星系统]，因为它们轨道的重心（即质心）不位于任一天体内，且它们潮汐锁定。“新视野号”是首艘造访冥王星及其卫星的航天器，于2015年7月14日进行飞越，并进行了详细的测量与观测。 冥王星于1930年由克莱德·汤博发现，使其成为柯伊伯带中已知的第一个天体。它立即被誉为第九行星，但当发现其体积远小于预期时，其行星地位就受到质疑。自1990年代起，柯伊伯带发现更多天体，特别是2005年质量较大的离散盘天体阋神星，使这疑虑进一步加深。2006年，国际天文学联合会（IAU）正式重新定义“行星”一词，以排除如冥王星等矮行星。然而，许多行星天文学家仍认为冥王星及其他矮行星是行星。 在1840年代，于尔班·勒威耶利用牛顿力学，在分析天王星轨道受到的摄动后，预测当时尚未被发现的行星海王星的位置。19世纪末，对海王星的后续观测使天文学家推测天王星轨道可能受到除海王星外另一颗行星的扰动。

Pluto has five known moons: Charon (the largest and the diameter of which is just over half that of Pluto), Styx, Nix, Kerberos, and Hydra. Pluto and Charon are sometimes considered a binary system because the barycenter of their orbits (that is, their center of mass) does not lie within either body, and they are tidally locked. New Horizons was the first spacecraft to visit Pluto and its moons, making a flyby on July 14, 2015, and taking detailed measurements and observations. Pluto was discovered in 1930 by Clyde W. Tombaugh, making it the first known object in the Kuiper belt. It was immediately hailed as the ninth planet, but its planetary status was questioned when it was found to be much smaller than expected. These doubts increased following the discovery of additional objects in the Kuiper belt starting in the 1990s, particularly the more massive scattered disk object Eris in 2005. In 2006, the International Astronomical Union (IAU) formally redefined the term planet to exclude dwarf planets such as Pluto. Many planetary astronomers, however, continue to consider Pluto and other dwarf planets to be planets. In the 1840s, Urbain Le Verrier used Newtonian mechanics to predict the position of the then-undiscovered planet Neptune after analyzing perturbations in the orbit of Uranus. Subsequent observations of Neptune in the late 19th century led astronomers to speculate that Uranus's orbit was being disturbed by another planet besides Neptune.

现场The event

1906年，富有的波士顿人帕西瓦尔·罗威尔（英语：Percival Lowell）于1894年在亚利桑那州旗杆镇创立了罗威尔天文台（英语：Lowell Observatory）——开始了一项庞大的计划，寻找可能的第九颗行星，他称之为“行星X”。1909年，罗威尔与威廉·H·皮克林提出了数个可能的天体坐标。 罗威尔和他的天文台使用伊丽莎白·威廉士所做的数学计算，持续进行搜寻，直到他于1916年去世，但始终无成果。罗威尔不知道的是，他的巡天曾于1915年3月19日和4月7日捕捉到两幅冥王星的微弱影像，但未被辨识出它们的真实身份。目前已知还有十四次回溯发现观测，最早一次由叶凯士天文台于1909年8月20日进行。 珀西瓦尔的遗孀康斯坦斯·罗威尔与罗威尔天文台就丈夫遗产展开长达十年的法律诉讼，直到1929年才重新展开X行星的搜寻。 天文台台长维斯托·斯莱弗（英语：Vesto Melvin Slipher）将定位行星X的任务交给23岁的克莱德·汤博（英语：Clyde Tombaugh），当时他刚到天文台任职，只因为斯莱弗对他的天文素描图印象深刻。

In 1906, Percival Lowell—a wealthy Bostonian who had founded Lowell Observatory in Flagstaff, Arizona, in 1894—started an extensive project in search of a possible ninth planet, which he termed "Planet X". In 1909, Lowell and William H. Pickering suggested several possible celestial coordinates for such a planet. Lowell and his observatory conducted his search, using mathematical calculations made by Elizabeth Williams, until his death in 1916, but to no avail. Unknown to Lowell, his surveys had captured two faint images of Pluto on March 19 and April 7, 1915, but they were not recognized for what they were. There are fourteen other known precovery observations, with the earliest made by the Yerkes Observatory on August 20, 1909. Percival's widow, Constance Lowell, entered into a ten-year legal battle with the Lowell Observatory over her husband's legacy, and the search for Planet X did not resume until 1929. Vesto Melvin Slipher, the observatory director, gave the job of locating Planet X to 23-year-old Clyde Tombaugh, who had just arrived at the observatory after Slipher had been impressed by a sample of his astronomical drawings.

余波Aftermath

汤博的任务是系统性地拍摄夜空成对的照片，然后检查每对照片，判断是否有位置移动的天体。他利用闪烁比对器，快速在每张底片的视角间切换，营造出任何在照片间位置或外观改变的物体移动的错觉。1930年2月18日，经过近一年的搜寻，汤博在1月23日和29日拍摄的感光底片上发现了一个可能的移动物体。一张1月21日拍摄的较不佳照片有助于确认此运动。天文台取得更多确认照片后，发现的消息于1930年3月13日电报传送达哈佛大学天文台。 该天体立即被称为第九行星。然而，当发现其大小远小于预期时，行星的地位就受到质疑。

Tombaugh's task was to systematically image the night sky in pairs of photographs, then examine each pair and determine whether any objects had shifted position. Using a blink comparator, he rapidly shifted back and forth between views of each of the plates to create the illusion of movement of any objects that had changed position or appearance between photographs. On February 18, 1930, after nearly a year's search, Tombaugh discovered a possible moving object on photographic plates taken on January 23 and 29. A lesser-quality photograph taken on January 21 helped confirm the movement. After the observatory obtained further confirmatory photographs, news of the discovery was telegraphed to the Harvard College Observatory on March 13, 1930. The object was immediately hailed as the ninth planet. However, its planetary status was questioned when it was found to be much smaller than expected.
