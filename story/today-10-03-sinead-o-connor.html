1992年10月3日：希妮德·奥康娜October 3, 1992: Sinéad O'Connor

舒哈达·萨达卡特（阿拉伯语：شهداء صداقة，英语：Shuhada' Sadaqat），旧名西妮德·玛丽·伯纳黛特·奥康纳（英语：Sinéad Marie…Sinéad Marie Bernadette O'Connor ( , shin-AYD; 8 December 1966 – 26 July 2023), also known as Shuhada' Sadaqat…

舒哈达·萨达卡特（阿拉伯语：شهداء صداقة，英语：Shuhada' Sadaqat），旧名西妮德·玛丽·伯纳黛特·奥康纳（英语：Sinéad Marie Bernadette O'Connor，1966年12月8日—2023年7月26日），是一名爱尔兰创作女歌手。代表作为翻唱王子的《Nothing Compares 2 U》。除了音乐以外，她还以反传统的行为和有争议性的观点而闻名。 西妮德·奥康纳出生于爱尔兰都柏林，在五兄弟姐妹中排行第三。她最初的名字是根据爱尔兰总统埃蒙·德·瓦莱拉的妻子西妮德·德·瓦莱拉取的。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「1992年10月3日：希妮德·奥康娜」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

Sinéad Marie Bernadette O'Connor ( , shin-AYD; 8 December 1966 – 26 July 2023), also known as Shuhada' Sadaqat, was an Irish singer, songwriter, and activist. During her musical career, which encompassed several hit records and artist collaborations, O'Connor drew attention to issues such as child abuse, human rights, racism, and women's rights. She was also known for her outspoken public image, openly discussing her spiritual journey, activism, socio-political viewpoints, and struggles with mental health. O'Connor's debut studio album, The Lion and the Cobra, was released in 1987 and achieved international chart success. Her 1990 album, I Do Not Want What I Haven't Got, was her biggest commercial success, selling over seven million copies worldwide.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

她的父亲是约翰·奥康纳（英语：John O'Connor），原任职结构工程师，后来转职为律师；而母亲是玛丽·奥康纳（英语：Marie O'Connor）。她的父母很早已结婚，但当奥康纳8岁时便已离婚，5兄弟姊妹中，最年长的3位（包括奥康纳）与母亲同居。 1992年10月3日，奥康纳在美国电视节目《周六夜现场》亮相。在场献唱了鲍勃·马利的歌曲《战争》（War），抗议罗马天主教的性虐待丑闻，然后拿出教宗若望保禄二世的照片，大喊“Fight the real enemy! (打倒真正的敌人!)”，并当着数百万的电视观众面前撕毁照片（因这个节目是现场直播）。 在媒体的推波助澜之下，奥康纳接着便不断地被嘘下演出舞台并受到观众的言语攻击。如此事件两周后，在麦迪逊广场花园举办的鲍勃·迪伦30周年纪念演唱会，当奥康纳要演唱《I Believe In You》时，因嘘声（也有鼓励声）太大以致于她无法开唱，于是她便直接清唱大声喊出了《War》的歌词后下台崩溃痛哭，活动主持人克里斯·克里斯托佛森则是拥抱了她并给予“不要让那些浑球影响你的心情”等鼓励安慰的话语。

O'Connor's next albums Am I Not Your Girl? (1992) and Universal Mother (1994) were both certified gold in the UK, while Faith and Courage (2000) was certified gold in Australia and Throw Down Your Arms (2005) was certified gold in Ireland. Having converted to Islam in 2018, she adopted the name Shuhada' Sadaqat while continuing to perform and record under her birth name. Her memoir, Rememberings, was published in 2021 and became a bestseller. In 2024, O'Connor was posthumously nominated for induction into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame. Sinéad Marie Bernadette O'Connor was born on 8 December 1966 at the Cascia House Nursing Home on Baggot Street in Dublin. She was named Sinéad after Irish author Sinéad de Valera, the mother of the doctor who presided over her delivery, Éamon de Valera jnr, and Bernadette in honour of Saint Bernadette of Lourdes. She was the third of five children; an older brother is the novelist Joseph O'Connor. Her parents were John Oliver "Seán" O'Connor, a structural engineer who later became a barrister and chairperson of the Divorce Action Group, and Johanna Marie O'Grady (1939–1985), who married in 1960 at the Church of Our Lady of Good Counsel, Drimnagh, Dublin. She attended Dominican College Sion Hill school in Blackrock, Dublin. In her 2021 memoir, Rememberings, O'Connor wrote that she was regularly beaten by her mother, who also taught her to steal from the collection plate at Mass and from charity tins.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

《周六夜现场》声称在事前并不知道奥康纳会这么做，并且拒绝了重播的要求（不过在该节目推出的DVD特集《Saturday Night Live - 25 Years of Music》的第四集中可以看到）。喜剧中心频道在重播这段时，则用奥康纳彩排时拿着一张微笑黑人儿童的照片的画面取代原本画面。周六夜现场为了向观众表示歉意，安排主持人乔·佩西在隔周的演出时拿着被贴回原状的教宗照片。 这不是奥康纳第一次跟周六夜现场不合。此前她拒绝在安德鲁·戴斯·克莱主持的节目中出现（因为他“排斥女性”）。但她同意上另一集由凯尔·麦克拉兰主持的节目。

At the age of 15, following her acts of shoplifting and truancy, O'Connor was placed for 18 months in the Grianán Training Centre in Drumcondra, which was run by the Order of Our Lady of Charity. She thrived in certain aspects, particularly in the development of her writing and music, but she chafed under the imposed conformity of the asylum, despite being given freedoms not granted to the other girls, such as attending an outside school and being allowed to listen to music, write songs, etc. For punishment, O'Connor described how "if you were bad, they sent you upstairs to sleep in the old folks' home. You're in there in the pitch black, you can smell the shit and the puke and everything, and these old women are moaning in their sleep ... I have never—and probably will never—experience such panic and terror and agony over anything." She later attended Maryfield College in Drumcondra, and Newtown School in Waterford for fifth and sixth year as a boarder, but did not sit the Leaving Certificate in 1985. On 10 February 1985, when O'Connor was 18, her mother died in a car accident, aged 45, after losing control of her car on an icy road in Ballybrack and crashing into a bus. In June 1993, O'Connor wrote a public letter in The Irish Times in which she asked people to "stop hurting" her: "If only I can fight off the voices of my parents / and gather a sense of self-esteem / Then I'll be able to REALLY sing..." The letter repeated accusations of child abuse by her parents as a child which O'Connor had made in interviews.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

1997年9月22日，意大利周报Vita采访奥康纳。奥康纳请教宗宽恕她，她说撕毁照片是一件可笑的做法。她还说明她并非叛离天主教，还是信教的，并引用圣奥古斯丁所说的“愤怒是前往勇气的第一步”。但是在2002年，她说对当年撕毁照片这事不会改变主意，同时亦表示已离开梵蒂冈的天主教，加入爱尔兰的天主教和东正教使徒教会（英语：Irish Orthodox Catholic and Apostolic Church）。 2018年10月19日，西妮德·奥康纳在Twitter上表示自己改信伊斯兰教，并改名为舒哈达·萨达卡特（阿拉伯语：شهداء صداقة，英语：Shuhada' Sadaqat），但仍以原名进行音乐活动。据信她在2023去世之前在爱尔兰、罗斯康芒郡和伦敦三地之间生活。

1980s: Beginnings and The Lion and the Cobra One of the volunteers at the Grianán centre was the sister of Paul Byrne, the drummer for the band In Tua Nua, who heard O'Connor singing "Evergreen" by Barbra Streisand. She recorded a song with them called "Take My Hand" but they felt that at 15, she was too young to join the band. Through an ad she placed in Hot Press in mid-1984, she met Colm Farrelly. Together they recruited a few other members and formed a band, Ton Ton Macoute. The band moved to Waterford briefly while O'Connor attended Newtown School, but she soon dropped out of school and followed them to Dublin, where their performances received positive reviews. Their sound was inspired by Farrelly's interest in world music, though most observers thought O'Connor's singing and stage presence were the band's strongest features.
