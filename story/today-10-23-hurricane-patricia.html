2015年10月23日：颶風派翠莎October 23, 2015: Hurricane Patricia

飓风帕特丽夏（英语：Hurricane Patricia，西班牙语发音：[paˈtɾisia]）为2015年太平洋飓风季第24个被命名的风暴，是2015年度全球最强及西半球有记录以来最强的热带气旋，亦是全球有记录以来第二强的热带气旋。帕特丽夏的接近中心最高持续风速为每小时345公里（以1分钟平均风速计算），打破2013年台风海燕之每小时315公里纪录，是全球有记录以来1分钟平均风速最高的热带气旋。帕特丽夏的海平面气压为872毫巴（百帕，25.75英寸汞柱），是全球有记录以来气压第二低的热带气旋，仅次于1979年台风泰培的870毫巴（百帕，25.69英寸汞柱）。 帕特丽夏为中美洲带来了广泛暴雨，数十万人受到风暴影响。帕特丽夏对墨西哥造成严重影响，损失估计超过54亿墨西哥比索（3.25亿美元），金塔纳罗奥州和韦拉克鲁斯州的降雨量超过500毫米，切图马尔的损失达到14亿墨西哥比索（8530万美元）。帕特丽夏造成的损失估计至少为4.628亿美元（2015年美元）。

Hurricane Patricia was the most powerful tropical cyclone on record worldwide in terms of maximum sustained winds. It was also the second-most intense on record worldwide in terms of pressure, with a minimum atmospheric pressure of 872 mbar (25.75 inHg), just behind Typhoon Tip's 870 mbar (25.69 inHg). Originating from a sprawling disturbance near the Gulf of Tehuantepec, south of Mexico, in mid-October 2015, Patricia was first classified as a tropical depression on October 20. Initial development was slow, with only modest strengthening within the first day of its classification. The system later became a tropical storm and was named Patricia, the twenty-fourth named storm of the 2015 Pacific hurricane season. Exceptionally favorable environmental conditions fueled explosive intensification on October 22.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

2015年10月11日，一个扰动天气区横穿中美洲并出现在东太平洋上空。在接下来的几天里，扰动缓慢移动，后来于10月15日与东风波合并。这些系统的合并以及同时发生的特万特佩克间隙风事件的影响刺激了大范围低气压域的形成。该特征在10月20日00:00UTC后不久逐渐巩固并成为热带低气压；此时，低气压位于萨利纳克鲁斯南偏东约205英里（335公里）处， 墨西哥。北面的中层山脊将低气压带向西引导，然后沿着弧形路径向北移动。 最初由于局部较冷的海面温度和干燥的空气而减缓了发展，那天晚些时候，低气压变成了热带风暴帕特丽夏。一旦离开不利区域，帕特丽夏在异常有利于快速增强的环境中穿越异常温暖的水域。10月21日晚开始戏剧性的加强，一直持续到 10月23日。帕特丽夏在10月22日世界标准时间00:00后不久达到飓风强度，具有突出的外围，明确的条带特征，和正在形成的风眼。飓风猎人调查飓风的数据表明，帕特丽夏在UTC时间18:00时已达到萨菲尔-辛普森飓风风力等级的4级标准。 到10月23日凌晨，发生了明显的向北转弯，随后向东北加速。-130°F（-90。C）云顶的实心环包围了飓风的12英里（19公里）宽的风眼，并表明其增强为5级飓风。在24 时的时间跨度内，帕特丽夏的最大持续风速增加了120 英里/小时（195公里/小时）。这是美国国家飓风中心 (NHC) 观测到的任何飓风中强度最快的一次。飓风在10月23日世界标准时间12:00左右达到峰值，估计风速约为215英里/小时（345公里/小时），气压为872毫巴（hPa；25.75英寸汞柱）；这些值是基于飓风猎人任务进入风暴六小时前的持续强化。

The precursor to Patricia produced widespread flooding rains in Central America. Hundreds of thousands of people were directly affected by the storm, mostly in Guatemala. At least six fatalities were attributed to the event: four in El Salvador, one in Guatemala, and one in Nicaragua. Torrential rains extended into southeastern Mexico, with areas of Quintana Roo and Veracruz reporting accumulations in excess of 19.7 in (500 mm). Damage in Chetumal reached MX$1.4 billion (US$85.3 million). As a tropical cyclone, Patricia's effects in Mexico were tremendous; however, the affected areas were predominantly rural, lessening the number of people directly impacted. Violent winds tore roofs from structures and stripped coastal areas of their vegetation. Preliminary assessments indicated hundreds of homes were destroyed; seven fatalities were linked to the hurricane directly or indirectly, including one during evacuations. Total damage from Patricia was estimated to be at least $462.8 million (2015 USD); the damage in Mexico alone was estimated to be in excess of MX$5.4 billion (US$325 million), with agriculture and infrastructure comprising the majority of losses. Flooding partially associated with remnant moisture from Patricia inflicted US$52.5 million in damage across Southern Texas. On October 11, 2015, an area of disturbed weather traversed Central America and emerged over the eastern Pacific Ocean. The disturbance moved slowly over the next few days, later merging with a tropical wave on October 15.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

10月23日晚些时候，随着眼墙置换循环的形成和风切变增加，随后迅速减弱。在墨西哥登陆前的五个小时内，帕特丽夏在仍然在水面上时以前所未有的速度减弱。然而，在UTC时间23:00左右在哈利斯科州奎斯马拉附近上岸后，它仍然是一个强大的4级飓风，持续风速为150英里/小时（240公里/小时），分析压力为 32毫巴（hPa；27.52 inHg）这使帕特丽夏成为有记录以来登陆的最强烈的太平洋飓风。上岸后，马德雷山脉的高地地形加速了帕特丽夏的减弱。热带气旋的低层和中层环流解耦，后者向东北方向加速，帕特丽夏于10月24日在墨西哥中部上空消散，距离上岸不到18小时。 飓风帕特丽夏的接近中心最高持续风速为每小时345公里，气压为872毫巴（百帕，25.75英寸汞柱），其气压仅次于1979年台风泰培的870毫巴（百帕，25.69英寸汞柱），不但是西半球有纪录以来最强的热带气旋，同时亦是全球有纪录以来第二强的热带气旋。先前西半球气压最低的热带气旋为2005年飓风威尔玛的882毫巴（百帕，26.05英寸汞柱），风速最高的热带气旋为1980年飓风艾伦，其接近中心最高持续风速为每小时305公里；而东北太平洋最强的热带气旋为1997年飓风琳达，其接近中心最高持续风速为每小时295公里，气压为902毫巴（百帕，26.64英寸汞柱）。

Development was initially slowed by locally cooler sea surface temperatures and dry air, and the depression became Tropical Storm Patricia later that day. Once clear of the unfavorable region, Patricia traversed anomalously warm waters within an environment exceptionally conducive to rapid intensification. Dramatic strengthening began late on October 21 and continued through October 23. Patricia reached hurricane strength shortly after 00:00 UTC on October 22, featuring prominent outflow, well-defined banding features, and a developing eye. Data from hurricane hunters investigating the cyclone indicated Patricia to have reached Category 4 status on the Saffir–Simpson hurricane wind scale by 18:00 UTC. By the early hours of October 23, a clear northward turn took place followed by acceleration northeast. A solid ring of −130 °F (−90 °C) cloud tops surrounded the hurricane's 12 mi (19 km) wide eye and signaled its intensification into a Category 5 hurricane. In a 24-hour time span, Patricia's maximum sustained winds increased by 120 mph (195 km/h), the fastest such intensification in any hurricane observed by the National Hurricane Center (NHC). The hurricane reached its peak intensity at around 12:00 UTC on October 23, with estimated winds around 215 mph (346 km/h) and a barometric pressure of 872 mbar (hPa; 25.75 inHg); these values are based upon continued intensification after a Hurricane Hunter mission into the storm six hours prior.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

根据实际观测及估算，帕特丽夏的一分钟最高持续风速达185节（每小时345公里），打破了2013年台风海燕、2016年台风莫兰蒂、2020年台风天鹅及2021年台风舒力基之170节（每小时315公里）纪录，成为全球有纪录以来风速最高的热带气旋。但海燕、莫兰蒂、天鹅及舒力基的风速均是透过卫星云图及德沃夏克分析法的估算得出，没有实际观测的数据，因此不适合作直接比较。此外，根据世界气象组织的纪录，1961年台风南施的接近中心最高持续风速同为185节（每小时345公里），但气象学界普遍认为1940至60年代的西太平洋热带气旋强度有高估之嫌，所以南施的纪录并不可靠。另外，除龙卷风所产生的风速外，热带气旋所产生的最强阵风纪录仍然由1996年气旋奥利维亚所保持，该气旋一度在西澳大利亚州巴罗岛产生高达每小时407公里的阵风。 在飓风帕特丽夏到来之前，墨西哥政府向沿海社区发出了多次观察和警告。10月21日世界标准时间09:00首次发出飓风监视，覆盖米却肯州、科利马州和哈利斯科州。热带风暴监视也覆盖了格雷罗的部分地区。随着帕特丽夏的增强，政府向科连特斯角、哈利斯科州和蓬塔圣特尔莫之间的地区发布了飓风预警，热带风暴警告补充了更北部和东部地区的情况。飓风警告于10月23日向北扩展至纳亚里特州圣布拉斯以南的地区。风暴向内陆移动且破坏性大风的威胁减弱后，这些警告于10月24日逐渐停止。

Later on October 23, rapid weakening ensued as an eyewall replacement cycle took shape and wind shear increased. In the five hours up until landfall in Mexico, Patricia weakened at an unprecedented rate while still over water. However, upon moving ashore around 23:00 UTC near Cuixmala, Jalisco, it remained a strong Category 4 hurricane, with sustained winds of 150 mph (240 km/h) and an analyzed pressure of 932 mbar (hPa; 27.52 inHg). This made Patricia the most intense landfalling Pacific hurricane on record, until Hurricane Otis surpassed it in October 2023. Once onshore, the high terrain of the Sierra Madre mountains accelerated Patricia's weakening. The low- and mid-level circulations of the tropical cyclone decoupled, with the latter accelerating northeast, and Patricia dissipated on October 24 over central Mexico, less than 18 hours after moving ashore. Hurricane Patricia set multiple records for maximum strength, rate of intensification, and rate of weakening throughout its relatively short existence. With maximum sustained winds of 215 mph (345 km/h) and a minimum pressure of 872 mbar (hPa; 25.75 inHg), Patricia is the most intense tropical cyclone ever observed in the Western Hemisphere. In terms of central pressure, it is also the second-most intense tropical cyclone ever recorded worldwide, just shy of Typhoon Tip in 1979 which had a minimum pressure of 870 mbar (hPa; 25.69 inHg).
