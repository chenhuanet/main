1965年10月28日：圣路易斯 (密苏里州)October 28, 1965: St. Louis

圣路易斯（英语：St.St.

圣路易斯（英语：St. Louis，/seɪnt ˈluːɪs, sənt ˈluːɪs/）位于密苏里河和密西西比河汇合处，是美国密苏里州唯一的独立市，也是该州第二大城市（第一大城市为堪萨斯城）。市内人口为308,626人，为美国按人口排名第58大城市。 圣路易斯是美国中西部的交通枢纽，由法国探险家兼皮草商人皮埃尔·拉克莱德（Pierre Laclède）和奥古斯特·舒托（Auguste Chouteau）于1764年建立，以中世纪是法王路易九世的封号圣路易斯命名，曾于1904年举办第三届夏季奥林匹克运动会。

### 一、时代条件

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

St. Louis (officially the City of St. Louis) is an independent city in the U.S. state of Missouri. It lies near the confluence of the Mississippi and the Missouri rivers. In 2020, the city proper had a population of 301,578, while its metropolitan area, which extends into Illinois, had an estimated population of over 2.8 million. It is the largest metropolitan area in Missouri and the second-largest in Illinois. The city's metropolitan statistical area is the 23rd-largest in the United States. The land that became St. Louis had been occupied by Native American cultures for thousands of years before European settlement. The city was founded on February 14, 1764, by French fur traders Gilbert Antoine de St. Maxent, Pierre Laclède, and Auguste Chouteau. They named it for King Louis IX of France, and it quickly became the regional center of the French Illinois Country.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

圣路易斯有多所大学，如圣路易斯华盛顿大学、圣路易斯大学和密苏里大学圣路易斯分校。位于中西区的华盛顿大学医学中心拥有医疗和制药机构的聚集地，包括巴恩斯犹太医院。 圣路易斯早于大航海时代以前，已经有人聚居，当时主要是些被称为筑丘人（Mound Builder）的美洲原住民。欧洲人最早于1673年踏足圣路易斯，其时法国探险家Louis Jolliet乘船穿越密西西比河时，正好途经此地。而在1700年代，就有一些欧洲移民来此定居。 至于正式建城则是1764年的事：两位法国籍皮草商人皮埃尔·拉克利德（Pierre Laclède）和奥古斯特·舒托（Auguste Chouteau），就在圣路易斯建城，专门与美国中西部原住民进行皮草贸易。同时以中世纪是法王路易九世的封号圣路易斯命名这座城市，故该城的末尾s是否发音仍有争议（相比之下，美国另一座以波旁王朝法王路易命名的城市路易维尔公认s不发音）。早年居民为法国殖民者，18世纪中叶七年战争后西班牙人开始增多。由于法国在七年战争中失败，密西西比河以东的殖民地由大英帝国占领。如今圣路易斯市所在的区被称为西班牙路易斯安那，仍然保留在法国势力范围内

St. Louis is designated as one of 173 global cities by the Globalization and World Cities Research Network. The GDP of Greater St. Louis was $226.6 billion in 2023. St. Louis has a diverse economy with strengths in the service, manufacturing, trade, transportation, and aviation industries. It is home to fourteen Fortune 1000 companies, seven of which are also Fortune 500 companies. Federal agencies headquartered in the city or with significant operations there include the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, the U.S. Department of Agriculture, and the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency. The city's attractions include the 630-foot (192 m) Gateway Arch, the Saint Louis Zoo, Forest Park, the City Museum, the Missouri Botanical Garden, the Saint Louis Science Center, and the Saint Louis Art Museum. Major research universities in Greater St. Louis include Washington University in St. Louis, Saint Louis University, and the University of Missouri–St. Louis. The Washington University Medical Center hosts an agglomeration of medical and pharmaceutical institutions, including Barnes-Jewish Hospital. St. Louis has four professional sports teams: the St. Louis Cardinals of Major League Baseball, the St. Louis Blues of the National Hockey League, St. Louis City SC of Major League Soccer, and the St. Louis Battlehawks of the United Football League.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

美国宣布独立期间，圣路易斯仍是法国属土。至1803年路易斯安那购地，圣路易斯与其他中西部地区一样，被卖给了美国。 1804年，受美国总统托马斯·杰斐逊派遣，美国陆军军官梅里韦瑟·刘易斯和威廉·克拉克率队从圣路易斯出发，远征西部地区。探险队沿密苏里河逆流而上向西，并于此年5月抵达太平洋海岸。1806年9月23日探险队胜利返回圣路易斯，探险队两位领导刘易斯和克拉克均居住于圣路易斯。此后圣路易斯成为各种探险家，西进垦荒者和毛皮生意人的集散地。

Mississippian culture and European exploration The area that became St. Louis was a center of the Native American Mississippian culture, which built numerous temple and residential earthwork mounds on both sides of the Mississippi River. Their major regional center was at Cahokia Mounds, active from 900 to 1500. Due to numerous major earthworks within St. Louis boundaries, the city was nicknamed "Mound City". These mounds were mostly demolished during the city's development. Historic Native American tribes in the area encountered by early Europeans included the Siouan-speaking Osage people, whose territory extended west, and the Illiniwek. Sugarloaf Mound in South St. Louis was repatriated to the Osage Nation in 2025.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

1808年，圣路易斯居民首次进行公投选举市政府。1818年，蒸汽船正式通航，大宗货物可直接通航至新奥尔良并海运至东海岸和欧洲国家，商业开始蓬勃发展。 1821年密苏里州成立，圣路易斯也于1822年被划做美国城市之一。这一时期主要支柱产业为港口、贸易和运输业。上述行业大量使用黑奴作为苦力。由于城市紧邻伊利诺伊州（自由州），每年有大量黑奴逃亡。某些逃亡失败的黑奴（多数为妇女儿童）则被法院起诉。圣路易斯深受自由州影响，每年都有许多正义律师义务为黑人争取自由。自美国内战前，有数百位黑人通过法庭合法的争取到自由。1840年起，德国、爱尔兰裔移民大量涌入，人口从1840年的20,000，升至1850年的77,860；1860年则高达16万。从18世纪中叶，圣路易斯人口超过新奥尔良，为密西西比河沿岸最大城市。

European exploration of the area was first recorded in 1673, when French explorers Louis Jolliet and Jacques Marquette traveled through the Mississippi River valley. Five years later, La Salle claimed the region for France as part of La Louisiane, also known as Louisiana. The earliest European settlements in the Illinois Country (also known as Upper Louisiana) were built by the French during the 1690s and early 1700s at Cahokia, Kaskaskia, and Fort de Chartres. Migrants from the French villages on the east side of the Mississippi River, such as Kaskaskia, also founded Ste. Genevieve in the 1730s. In 1764, after France lost the Seven Years' War, Pierre Laclède and his stepson Auguste Chouteau founded what was to become the city of St. Louis. (French lands east of the Mississippi had been ceded to Great Britain and the lands west of the Mississippi to Spain; Catholic France and Spain were 18th-century allies. Louis XV of France and Charles III of Spain were cousins, both from the House of Bourbon.) The French families built the city's economy on the fur trade with the Osage, and with more distant tribes along the Missouri River. The Chouteau brothers gained a monopoly from Spain on the fur trade with Santa Fe. French colonists used African slaves as domestic servants and workers in the city.
