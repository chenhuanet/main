2012年10月29日：颶風桑迪October 29, 2012: Hurricane Sandy

飓风桑迪（英语：Hurricane Sandy，美国海军研究实验室：18L）是2012年大西洋飓风季）第18个被命名的风暴。源于10月22日由加勒比海西部产生的扰动天气，因其吹袭美国东海岸，造成严重损害而受到关注。由于飓风桑迪对美国地区造成巨大的灾难，已遭除名。也是当年北大西洋第10个飓风、第2个大型飓风。这场风暴成为有记录以来覆盖范围最广的热带气旋之一（按直径测量，热带风暴的风速范围超过1,150英里（1,850公里））。 10月20日一个热带扰动于加勒比海海域形成，10月22日国家飓风中心发布热带气旋形成警报，稍晚升格为一热带风暴，并命名为“桑迪”（Sandy）。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「2012年10月29日：颶風桑迪」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

Hurricane Sandy (unofficially referred to as Superstorm Sandy) was an extremely large and devastating tropical cyclone which ravaged the Caribbean and the coastal Mid-Atlantic region of the United States in late October 2012. It was the largest Atlantic hurricane on record as measured by diameter, with tropical storm-force winds spanning 1,150 miles (1,850 km). The storm inflicted nearly US$70 billion in damage (equivalent to $98 billion in 2025), and killed 254 people in eight countries, from the Caribbean to Canada. The eighteenth named storm, tenth hurricane, and second major hurricane of the 2012 Atlantic hurricane season, Sandy was a Category 3 storm at its peak intensity when it made landfall in Cuba, though most of the damage it caused was after it became a Category 1–equivalent extratropical cyclone off the coast of the Northeastern United States.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

随后路径朝北增强为一级飓风，10月25日凌晨登陆牙买加东部，出海后再度增强为二级飓风（最大持续风速每小时175公里），不久后再从古巴东南部沿海登陆，出海后减弱为一级飓风，在10月26日凌晨经过巴哈马，总计造成69人死亡，10月27日凌晨减弱为热带风暴，28日再度增强为一级飓风，路径往美国东海岸前进。 至少来自巴哈马、加勒比海地区、加拿大和美国的108人已被证实因此次飓风死亡。此次飓风在美国地区造成的财物损失高达750亿美元，仅次于2005年由飓风卡特里娜所造成的1080亿美元损失。时值美国总统大选，奥巴马与罗姆尼两位美国总统候选人都小心翼翼地应对飓风灾情，但对于谁收获了更多“政治红利”，并非所有媒体都像《图片报》那么肯定。10月31日，多家机构公布的最新民调结果各不相同，唯一的共同点是，奥巴马和罗姆尼的支持率“极其接近”。 因2010年地震和2010年-2011年海地霍乱爆发受灾的海地仍在重建。截止10月29日，此次飓风造成的连续四日的降雨中，造成至少52人死亡，约20万人无家可归。该国首都太子港的所有街道都已被淹，“整个国家的南部都陷入洪水之中”。绵延在市区的难民帐篷或住房以及Cité Soleil街区或已被淹、或不断漏水，而此情此景不过是今年早些时候飓风艾萨克来袭的重现。联合国警告持续的洪涝灾害以及不卫生的生活环境可能导致两年前的霍乱再次袭来，在2010年的霍乱中至少七千四百余人死亡，60万人感染。此外精疲力竭的军队可能让海地无法即时应付各种紧急需求。

In Jamaica, winds left 70% of residents without electricity, blew roofs off buildings, killed one person, and caused about $100 million (equivalent to $140 million in 2025) in damage. Sandy's outer bands brought flooding to Haiti, killing a total of 75 people, causing food shortages, and leaving about 200,000 homeless; the hurricane also caused two deaths in the Dominican Republic. In Puerto Rico, one man was swept away by a swollen river. In Cuba, there was extensive coastal flooding and wind damage inland, destroying some 15,000 homes, killing 11, and causing $2 billion (equivalent to $2.8 billion in 2025) in damage. Sandy caused two deaths and an estimated $700 million (equivalent to $982 million in 2025) in damage in the Bahamas. In the United States, Hurricane Sandy affected 24 states, including the entire eastern seaboard from Florida to Maine and west across the Appalachian Mountains to Michigan and Wisconsin, with particularly severe damage in New Jersey and New York. Its storm surge hit New York City on October 29, flooding streets, tunnels and subway lines and cutting power in and around the city. Damage in the United States amounted to $65 billion (equivalent to $91 billion in 2025). In Canada, two were killed in Ontario, and the storm caused an estimated C$100 million (equivalent to C$135 million in 2025) in damage throughout Ontario and Quebec. Hurricane Sandy began as a low-pressure system which developed sufficient organized convection to be classified as Tropical Depression Eighteen on October 22 south of Kingston, Jamaica.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

在多米尼加共和国，飓风导致2人死亡。应政府表示持续的降雨会持续至10月27日，已有8,755人撤离。由于部分路段积水严重，交通严重受阻。一名CNN的雇员估计圣多明各70%的街道出现积水。一些车辆被淹，一些卡车司机借此收取车主5美元以协助拖出被淹车辆，但也不乏免费帮助他人的司机。 由于桑迪所造成的严重破坏及人命伤亡，世界气象组织通过将“桑迪”除名，由“莎拉”（Sara）取代。

After Sandy exited Cuba, the structure of the storm became disorganized, and it turned to the north-northwest over the Bahamas. By October 27, Sandy was no longer fully tropical, as evidenced by the development of frontal structures in its outer circulation. Despite strong shear, Sandy maintained its convection due to influence from an approaching trough; the same trough eventually turned the hurricane to the northeast. After briefly weakening to a tropical storm, Sandy re-intensified into a Category 1 hurricane, and on October 28 an eye began redeveloping. The storm moved around an upper-level low over the eastern United States and also to the southwest of a ridge over Atlantic Canada, turning it to the northwest. Sandy briefly re-intensified to Category 2 intensity on the morning of October 29, by which time it had become an extremely large hurricane, with a record gale-force wind diameter of over 1,150 miles (1,850 km), and an unusually low central barometric pressure of 940 mbar, possibly due to the very large size of the system. This pressure set records as the lowest ever observed in many cities across the Northeastern United States. The convection diminished while the hurricane accelerated toward the New Jersey coast, and the cyclone was no longer tropical by 2100 UTC on October 29. About 2½ hours later, Sandy made landfall near Brigantine, New Jersey, with sustained winds of 80 mph (130 km/h).

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

On October 23, 2012, the path of Hurricane Sandy was correctly predicted by the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) headquartered in Reading, England, nearly eight days in advance of its striking the American East Coast. The computer model noted that the storm would turn west towards land and strike the New York/New Jersey region on October 29, rather than turn east and head out to the open Atlantic as most hurricanes in this position do. By October 27, four days after the ECMWF made its prediction, the National Weather Service and National Hurricane Center confirmed the path of the hurricane predicted by the European model. The National Weather Service was criticized for not employing its higher-resolution forecast models the way that its European counterpart did. A subsequent hardware and software upgrade completed at the end of 2013 enabled the NWS to make predictions more accurately and farther in advance than the technology in 2012 had allowed. According to NCAR senior climatologist Kevin E. Trenberth, "The answer to the oft-asked question of whether an event is caused by climate change is that it is the wrong question. All weather events are affected by climate change because the environment in which they occur is warmer and moister than it used to be." Although NOAA meteorologist Martin Hoerling attributes Sandy to "little more than the coincidental alignment of a tropical storm with an extratropical storm", Trenberth does agree that the storm was caused by "natural variability" but adds that it was "enhanced by global warming".
