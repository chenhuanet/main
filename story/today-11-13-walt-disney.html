1940年11月13日：華特·迪士尼November 13, 1940: Walt Disney

沃尔特·伊利亚斯·“华特”·迪士尼（英语：Walter Elias "Walt" Disney、/ˈdɪzni/，1901年12月5日—1966年12月15日）是一位美国动画师、电影制片人、配音员及企业家。他是美国动画黄金时代的重要先驱之一，为卡通制作引入多项创新技术。作为电影制片人，他保持着个人获得最多奥斯卡金像奖（22座）及提名（59次）的纪录。他亦曾获颁两项金球奖特别成就奖及一项艾美奖等多项荣誉。他的多部作品被美国国会图书馆列入国家影片登记表，亦被美国电影学会评选为影史最伟大电影之一。 迪士尼于1901年出生于芝加哥，主要在密苏里州成长，自幼即对绘画产生浓厚兴趣。他童年时曾接受艺术课程训练，18岁即投入商业插画工作。1920年代初，他迁居加利福尼亚州，与兄长洛伊·O·迪士尼共同创立迪士尼兄弟工作室（后发展为华特迪士尼公司）。1928年，他与乌布·伊沃克斯共同创造角色米老鼠，该角色亦成为其首个广受欢迎的代表作；在早期制作中，迪士尼本人亦曾为米老鼠配音。随着工作室逐步壮大，他在创作与技术上持续突破，推动同步声音、三色特艺彩色全彩技术、长篇动画电影制作以及摄影技术的革新。相关成果体现在《白雪公主》（1937）、《木偶奇遇记》与《幻想曲》（均为1940）、《小飞象》（1941）及《小鹿斑比》（1942）等作品中，对动画电影的发展产生深远影响。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「1940年11月13日：華特·迪士尼」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

Walter Elias Disney ( DIZ-nee; December 5, 1901 – December 15, 1966) was an American animator, film producer, voice actor, and entrepreneur. A pioneer of the American animation industry, he introduced several developments in the production of cartoons. As a film producer, he holds the record for most Academy Awards won by (22) and nominated for (59) an individual. He was presented with two Golden Globe Special Achievement Awards and an Emmy Award, among other honors. Several of his films are included in the National Film Registry by the Library of Congress and have also been named as some of the greatest films ever by the American Film Institute. Born in Chicago in 1901 and raised largely in Missouri, Disney developed an early interest in drawing. He took art classes as a boy and took a job as a commercial illustrator at the age of 18.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

1950年代，迪士尼进军主题乐园产业，并于1955年在加利福尼亚州安那罕开设迪士尼乐园。为筹措资金，他同时拓展电视节目业务，如《华特迪士尼的迪士尼乐园》与《米老鼠俱乐部》。此外，他亦参与规划美国国家展览会（1959年莫斯科展）、1960年冬季奥林匹克运动会以及1964年纽约世界博览会。1965年，他开始开发另一座主题乐园迪士尼世界，其核心构想为一座新型城市——“EPCOT”。 迪士尼一生吸烟甚重，并于1966年因肺癌去世，当时主题乐园与EPCOT计划尚未完成。私下里，他性格较为害羞，常有自我贬抑与缺乏安全感的一面；但在公众场合则展现出温暖外向的形象。他对合作对象要求严格且标准甚高。尽管曾有指控称其具有美国种族主义或美国反犹太主义倾向，但此类说法亦遭许多熟识他的人反驳。关于迪士尼的史学史呈现多元观点，既有将其视为传播美国主义价值的代表，也有观点认为其体现美国文化帝国主义的象征。迪士尼普遍被视为20世纪最具影响力的文化人物之一，在动画史与美国文化史中占有重要地位，并被视为国家级文化象征。他的电影作品至今仍持续放映与改编，迪士尼主题乐园遍布全球且规模持续扩大，其公司亦发展为全球最大的媒体与娱乐企业集团之一。 迪士尼于1901年12月5日出生于芝加哥赫莫萨区特里普大道1249号。他是伊莱亚斯·迪士尼之第四子，父亲生于加拿大省，具有盎格鲁-爱尔兰人血统；母亲弗洛拉（本姓 Call）则为具有德国与英国血统的美国人。除华特外，家中尚有兄长赫伯特、雷蒙与洛伊·O·迪士尼，以及1903年12月出生的妹妹露丝。

In the 1950s, Disney expanded into the theme park industry, and in July 1955 he opened Disneyland in Anaheim, California. To fund the project he diversified into television programs, such as Walt Disney's Disneyland and The Mickey Mouse Club. He was also involved in planning the 1959 Moscow Fair, the 1960 Winter Olympics, and the 1964 New York World's Fair. In 1965, he began development of another theme park, Disney World, the heart of which was to be a new type of city, the "Experimental Prototype Community of Tomorrow" (EPCOT). Disney was a heavy smoker throughout his life and died of lung cancer in 1966 before either the park or the EPCOT project were completed. Disney was a shy, self-deprecating and insecure man in private but adopted a warm and outgoing public persona. He had high standards and high expectations of those with whom he worked. Although there have been accusations that he was racist or antisemitic, they have been contradicted by many who knew him. The historiography of Disney has taken a variety of perspectives, ranging from views of him as a purveyor of homely patriotic values to being a representative of American cultural imperialism. Widely considered to be one of the most influential cultural figures of the 20th century, Disney remains an important presence in the history of animation and in the cultural history of the United States, where he is acknowledged as a national cultural icon.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

1906年，迪士尼四岁时，全家迁至密苏里州马赛林的一座农场。在当地，他曾替一位退休医生绘制马匹图像并获得报酬，逐渐培养出对绘画的兴趣。其父订阅《理性呼声报》，迪士尼则临摹漫画家莱恩·沃克的头版漫画作为练习。他亦开始学习使用水彩与蜡笔。此外，他居住在阿奇森、托皮卡与圣菲铁路沿线，因而对火车产生浓厚兴趣。 1909年末，他与妹妹露丝一同进入马塞林的帕克学校就读。迪士尼一家为公理会的活跃成员。迪士尼后来回忆，马塞林时期的童年是其一生中最幸福且最具塑造性的阶段之一，并成为其日后多项创作的重要灵感来源。

In 1911, the Disneys moved to Kansas City, Missouri. There, Disney attended the Benton Grammar School, where he met fellow-student Walter Pfeiffer, who came from a family of theatre fans and introduced him to the world of vaudeville and motion pictures. Before long, Disney was spending more time at the Pfeiffers' house than at home. Elias had purchased a newspaper delivery route for The Kansas City Star and Kansas City Times. Disney and Roy woke up at 4:30 every morning to deliver the Times before school, and repeated the round for the evening Star after school. The schedule was exhausting, and Disney often received poor grades after falling asleep in class, but he continued his paper route for more than six years. He attended Saturday courses at the Kansas City Art Institute and also took a correspondence course in cartooning. In 1917, Elias bought stock in a Chicago jelly producer, the O-Zell Company, and moved back to Chicago with his family. Disney enrolled at McKinley High School and became the cartoonist of the school newspaper, drawing patriotic pictures about World War I; he also took night courses at the Chicago Academy of Fine Arts.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

1911年，迪士尼一家迁往密苏里州堪萨斯城。他在本顿文法学校就读，并结识同学华特·菲佛（Walter Pfeiffer）。菲佛来自戏剧爱好家庭，将他带入杂技表演与电影的世界。不久后，迪士尼在菲佛家的时间甚至多于自己家中。 其父购得《堪萨斯城星报》与《堪萨斯城时报》的送报路线。迪士尼与兄长罗伊每日清晨4时30分起床，在上学前派送《时报》，放学后再派送晚间版《星报》。繁重的工作使他经常在课堂上睡着，成绩不佳，但他仍持续此工作超过六年。他亦于周六在堪萨斯城艺术学院修习课程，并透过函授学习漫画绘制。

In mid-1918, Disney attempted to join the United States Army to fight the Germans, but he was rejected as too young. After forging the date of birth on his birth certificate, he joined the Red Cross in September 1918 as an ambulance driver. Disney was shipped to France but arrived in November, after the armistice. He drew cartoons on the side of his ambulance for decoration and had some of his work published in the army newspaper Stars and Stripes. He returned to Kansas City in October 1919, where he worked as an apprentice artist at the Pesmen-Rubin Commercial Art Studio, where he drew commercial illustrations for advertising, theater programs and catalogs, and befriended fellow artist Ub Iwerks. In January 1920, as Pesmen-Rubin's revenue declined after Christmas, Disney, aged 18, and Iwerks were laid off.
