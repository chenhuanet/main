1842年11月26日：聖母大學November 26, 1842: University of Notre Dame

湖畔圣母大学（英语：University of Notre Dame du Lac），通称圣母大学（英语：University of Notre Dame），简称圣母（英语：Notre Dame），是一所位于美国印地安纳州南本德东北市郊圣母镇的私立天主教研究型大学，创建于1842年，是美国大学协会、大西洋沿岸联盟和大学研究协会成员。 圣母大学在1842年由法国神父爱德华·索林的圣十字会所创立，是一所不受于教廷管辖的天主教大学。乃为一所文理兼顾的著名私立大学。“Notre Dame”是圣母之意。顾名思义，是一所罗马天主教学校，其中大约七成的学生信奉天主教。爱德华索林于1842年创立了这所学校。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「1842年11月26日：聖母大學」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

### 补记

还需要补上一层：历史写作最难的地方，不是把事件说得热闹，而是把它的尺度说准。一个日期之所以能进入“历史上的今天”，往往因为它同时具备可被记录的动作、可被辨认的现场和可被后来者继续追问的后果。照片会褪色，报纸会变黄，建筑会被改建，但这些问题不会自动消失。它们会在城市更新、技术迭代和公共记忆的争论中一次次返回。也正因如此，重读这一天，应当同时保留两种眼光：一种贴近现场，看见具体的人在具体的时间做出具体的选择；另一种拉远距离，看见这些选择如何被制度、市场和偶然性放大。两种眼光互相校正，历史才不至于变成单纯的赞歌或单纯的清算。我们把这些图像放在一起，是希望读者先看见，再判断；先理解，再纪念。

The University of Notre Dame du Lac (known simply as Notre Dame; NOH-tər-DAYM; ND) is a private Catholic research university in Notre Dame, Indiana, United States. The university was founded in 1842 by members of the Congregation of Holy Cross, a Catholic religious order of priests and brothers. Its main campus covers 1,261 acres (510 ha) in a suburban setting and has landmarks such as the Golden Dome main building, Sacred Heart Basilica, the Grotto of Our Lady of Lourdes, the Word of Life mosaic mural, and Notre Dame Stadium. Notre Dame is classified among "R1: Doctoral Universities – Very high research spending and doctorate production". In the 2025–2026 academic year 13,042 students attended Notre Dame, making it the eighth largest Catholic university in terms of enrollment in the U.S.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

1952年至1987年间，西奥多·赫斯堡担任校长期间对大学进行了重大改进，增加了大学的资源、学术项目和声誉。 该大学于1972年首次招收女本科生。此后，该大学稳步发展，在接下来的两位校长爱德华·马洛（Edward Malloy）和约翰·杰金斯（John Jenkins）的领导下，完成了许多基础设施和研究扩展。 2015年，大学与爱尔兰的凯尔莫尔修道院建立合作，次年开始大学的部分学生会在该修道院上课。2021财年末，其捐赠基金价值203亿美元，是美国大学中最大的捐赠基金之一。

The university's athletic teams are members of the NCAA Division I and are known collectively as the Fighting Irish. Notre Dame is noted for its football team, which contributed to its rise to prominence on the national stage in the early 20th century. Notre Dame teams in other sports, chiefly in the Atlantic Coast Conference, have won 17 national championships. Between 1952 and 1987, Theodore Hesburgh's administration increased the university's resources and improved its academic programs and its reputation. Its network of alumni consists of 151,000 members. Stephen Badin, the first priest ordained in the United States, had come to northern Indiana after an invitation extended by Potawatomi chief Leopold Pokagon to minister to his tribe, and Badin bought the 524 acres (212 ha) of land in 1830 that became the university's campus. In 1842, bishop Célestin Guynemer de la Hailandière of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Vincennes offered the land to Edward Sorin of the Congregation of Holy Cross on the condition that he build a college in two years. Sorin arrived on the site with eight Holy Cross brothers from France and Ireland on November 26, 1842 and began the school using Badin's old log chapel. After enrolling two students, Sorin erected more buildings, including the Old College, the first church, and the first main building. Notre Dame began as a primary and secondary school; in 1844 it received its official college charter from the Indiana General Assembly, under the name of the University of Notre Dame du Lac (University of Our Lady of the Lake).

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

2023年，圣母大学加入美国大学协会，成为印第安纳州继印第安纳大学（1909年）、普渡大学（1958年）之后的第三所成员院校。 圣母大学的一万多名学生来自美国各地和世界100多个国家。该大学由七所学院（文学院、全球事务学院、法学院、商学院、工学院、建筑学院、理学院）和研究生院组成，研究生课程包括50多个硕士、博士和专业学位，包括圣母法学院和与印第安纳大学医学院联合提供的医学士哲学博士双学位课程。该大学提供超过50年的海外学习课程和超过15个暑期课程。

The college awarded its first degrees in 1849. As it grew under the presidency of Sorin and his successors, new academic programs were offered and new buildings built to accommodate the growing student and faculty population. The brief presidency of Patrick Dillon (1865–1866) saw the original main building replaced with a larger one that housed the university's administration, classrooms, and dormitories. Under William Corby's first administration, enrollment at Notre Dame increased to over 500 students. In 1869, he opened the law school, which offered a two-year course of study, and in 1871 he began construction of Sacred Heart Church, today the Basilica of the Sacred Heart. Two years later, the president Auguste Lemonnier started a library in the Main Building that had 10,000 volumes by 1879. However, fire destroyed the Main Building and the library collection in April 1879; the school closed immediately and students were sent home. Rebuilding began on May 17, and the third and current Main Building was completed before the fall semester of 1879. The library collection was likewise redeveloped.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

一些学科具有很强的科研学术实力，尤以其大学部见长，政治学、社会学、政治经济学、经济伦理学、逻辑学、商学、会计学、历史、哲学、英语和医学等在相应领域有较大的影响力。圣母大学神学系是美国神学研究的重要场所之一，有100余名教职人员，主要针对罗马天主教进行教学及研究。 法学院和商学院研究所都位于国内前列。商学院规模较大，有2300名学生，包括会计学、金融、管理学、信息系统、会计学硕士、非营利组织管理学以及市场学，EMBA世界排名第20名。建筑学院以教授新古典主义建筑和年度颁发的德里豪斯建筑奖而闻名。

The presidency of Thomas E. Walsh (1881–1893) focused on improving Notre Dame's scholastic reputation and standards. At the time, many students came to Notre Dame only for its business courses and did not graduate. Walsh started a "Belles Lettres" program and invited notable lay intellectuals like writer Maurice Francis Egan to campus. Washington Hall was built in 1881 as a theater, and the Science Hall (today the LaFortune Student Center) was built in 1883 to house the science program (established in 1880) and multiple classrooms and science labs. The construction of Sorin Hall saw the first freestanding residence hall on campus and one of the first in the country to have private rooms for students, a project championed by Sorin and John Zahm. During Walsh's tenure, Notre Dame started its football program and awarded its first Laetare Medal, one the earlist such honors bequesthed by a Catholic university in the United States. The Law School was reorganized under the leadership of William J. Hoynes (dean from 1883 to 1919), and when its new building was opened shortly after his death, it was renamed in his honor. John Zahm was the Holy Cross Provincial for the United States from 1898 to 1906, with overall supervision of the university. He sought to modernize and expand Notre Dame by erecting buildings and adding to the campus art gallery and library, amassing what became a famous Dante collection, and pushing Notre Dame toward becoming a research university dedicated to scholarship.
