1925年11月28日：大奥普里November 28, 1925: Grand Ole Opry

大奥普里（英语：Grand Ole Opry）是美国一档乡村音乐电台广播实况节目，每周在晚间演出2至5次（随季节调整）。该节目由乔治·D·海于1925年11月28日创立，初名“WSM谷仓舞”（WSM Barn Dance），在田纳西州纳什维尔WSM电台上播放。1927年改为现名。现由奥普里娱乐公司（Opry Entertainment，NBC环球、Atairos和大股东莱曼酒店物业的合资企业）运营，是美国广播史上持续运营时间最长的节目之一。节目以传承乡村音乐及其历史为宗旨，汇聚著名歌手与当红新秀，呈现乡村音乐、蓝草音乐、美式根源音乐、民谣和福音音乐，有时还会播出喜剧和喜剧小品。每年吸引全球数十万现场观众及数百万广播与网络听众。 1930年代，该节目开始聘请专业人士，并将时长延长至4小时。当时，WSM的广播功率已达5万瓦，该节目也在近30个州成为周六晚间音乐传统节目。1939年，该节目在NBC广播电台首次全国播出。1943年，大奥普里搬到了其最著名的故址——赖曼礼堂。随着大奥普里重要性的提升，纳什维尔市也随之发展成为美国的“乡村音乐之都”。大奥普里对纳什维尔意义非凡，在进入纳什维尔大都会区/戴维森县境时，驾车者所看到的欢迎标语上，都标明了这是大奥普里的“故乡”。

The Grand Ole Opry is a regular live country-music radio broadcast originating from Nashville, Tennessee, on WSM, held between two and five nights per week, depending on the time of year. It was founded on November 28, 1925, by George D. Hay as the WSM Barn Dance, taking its current name in 1927. Currently owned and operated by Opry Entertainment (a joint venture between NBCUniversal, Atairos and majority shareholder Ryman Hospitality Properties), it is the longest-running radio broadcast in U.S. history. Dedicated to honoring country music and its history, the Opry showcases a mix of famous singers and contemporary chart-toppers performing country, bluegrass, Americana, folk, and gospel music as well as comedic performances and skits. It attracts hundreds of thousands of visitors from around the world and millions of radio and internet listeners.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

在大奥普里播出，至今仍是乡村音乐的最高成就之一。在乡村音乐史的数千场演出中，只有超过225场在大奥普里播出。在2024年，大约有75场。正如蒂娜·贝尼特斯-埃文斯（Tina Benitez-Evans）在《美国歌曲作家》（American Songwriter）中所写：“在大奥普里播出，是乡村音乐界的最高成就之一。” 自1974年以来，该节目一直在纳什维尔市中心东边的大奥普里剧院播出；1999至2020年间，曾每年冬季回到赖曼礼堂演出三个月；并从2023年起，再次进行冬季短期驻场演出。多年来，除了广播节目外，演出还不定期地在电视上播出，最初是在纳什维尔电视网上，后来是在CMT、大美家庭和环绕乡村。截至2025年，以前大奥普里演出的视频合集（称为Opry Live，意为奥普里现场），每周六晚上在免费广告支持网络电视环绕乡村，以及大奥普里的YouTube和Facebook频道以数字方式播出，并同步在北美多家电视台播出。 大奥普里最初称为“WSM谷仓舞”（WSM Barn Dance），于1925年11月28日，在纳什维尔市中心国家人寿和意外保险公司新建的五楼广播演播室开播。1925年10月17日，管理层开始播出一档以“汉弗莱·贝特（Humphrey Bate）博士和他的老派弦乐四重奏”为标志的节目。11月2日，WSM聘请了长期担任播音员和节目总监的乔治·D·海，他是芝加哥WLS电台“国家谷仓舞”节目的进取先锋，并因在WLS和田纳西州孟菲斯的WMC电台工作出色，而被誉为美国最受欢迎的电台播音员。尽管WSM聘用乔治·D·海时，他还差一周满30岁，但他（人称“Solemn Old Judge”，意为“庄严的老法官”）于1925年11月28日，与77岁的小提琴手吉米·汤普森叔叔一起创办了WSM谷仓舞，这一天也被定为大奥普里的诞生日。

Membership in the Opry remains one of country music's crowning achievements. Just over 225 acts have been members of the Grand Ole Opry out of the thousands of acts that have existed during the history of country music. At present (2024), about 75 acts are members. As Tina Benitez-Evans wrote in American Songwriter, "Membership in the Grand Ole Opry is one of the highest achievements within the country music community." Since 1974, the show has been broadcast from the Grand Ole Opry House east of downtown Nashville, with an annual three-month winter foray back to the Ryman from 1999 to 2020, and again for shorter winter residencies beginning in 2023. In addition to the radio programs, performances have been sporadically televised over the years, originally on The Nashville Network, and later CMT, GAC, and Circle. As of 2025, video compilations of previous Opry performances (dubbed "Opry Live") are distributed digitally every Saturday evening on FAST network Circle Country as well as the Opry's YouTube and Facebook outlets, and syndicated to a number of television stations across North America. The Grand Ole Opry started as the WSM Barn Dance in the new fifth-floor radio studio of the National Life & Accident Insurance Company in downtown Nashville on November 28, 1925. On October 17, 1925, management began a program featuring "Dr. Humphrey Bate and his string quartet of old-time musicians." 第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

该节目早期经常邀请的乐队包括比尔·门罗、汉弗莱·贝特领衔的负鼠猎人（Possum Hunters）、戴夫·梅肯叔叔领衔的果罐饮者（Fruit Jar Drinkers）、克鲁克兄弟（Crook Brothers）、宾克利兄弟的迪克西·克洛德霍普斯、锡德·哈克雷德、德福德·贝利、小提琴亚瑟·史密斯和峡谷跳跃者。 “海法官”很喜欢果罐饮者，每次演出都让他们最后出场，因为他总想用“热情洋溢的小提琴演奏”来结束每个环节。他们是第二支被谷仓舞接纳的乐队，而克鲁克兄弟是第一支。当大奥普里开始邀请广场舞者上场时，果罐饮者总会为他们演出。1926年，田纳西班卓琴演奏家戴夫·梅肯叔叔，成为了该节目第一位真正的明星。他曾录制过多首歌曲，并曾在杂耍剧团巡回演出。

Some of the bands regularly on the show during its early days included Bill Monroe, the Possum Hunters (with Humphrey Bate), the Fruit Jar Drinkers with Uncle Dave Macon, the Crook Brothers, the Binkley Brothers' Dixie Clodhoppers, Sid Harkreader, DeFord Bailey, Fiddlin' Arthur Smith, and the Gully Jumpers. Judge Hay liked the Fruit Jar Drinkers and asked them to appear last on each show because he wanted to always close each segment with "red hot fiddle playing". They were the second band accepted on Barn Dance, with the Crook Brothers being the first. When the Opry began having square dancers on the show, the Fruit Jar Drinkers always played for them. In 1926, Uncle Dave Macon, a Tennessee banjo player who had recorded several songs and toured on the vaudeville circuit, became its first real star.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

“大奥普里”（Grand Ole Opry）这个词最早出现在广播中是在1927年12月10日。当天，在乔治·D·海的谷仓舞之前，是NBC红网的《音乐欣赏时间》节目，包含古典音乐和大歌剧选段。当晚在介绍节目的第一位表演者德福德·贝利时，乔治·D·海说：“过去一个小时，我们主要听大歌剧，但从现在开始，我们将呈现‘大奥普里’。” 随着现场演出的观众人数不断增加，国家人寿和意外保险公司的广播场地已显得太小，无法容纳大批粉丝。他们建了一个更大的演播室，但仍然不够大。前几个月没有观众，其后国家人寿决定允许将演出转移至其总部之外。1934年10月，大奥普里迁入当时还位于郊区的希尔斯伯勒剧院（今贝尔考特剧院），又于1936年6月13日迁至东纳什维尔的迪克西礼拜堂（Dixie Tabernacle）。之后，大奥普里再迁至战争纪念礼堂，该演出场所位于市中心、毗邻州议会大厦。为控制人流，向每位观众收取25美分，但收效甚微。1943年6月，大奥普里迁至赖曼礼堂。

The phrase "Grand Ole Opry" was first uttered on radio on December 10, 1927. At the time, the NBC Red Network's Music Appreciation Hour, a program with classical music and selections from grand opera, was followed by Hay's Barn Dance. That evening, as he was introducing DeFord Bailey, the show's first performer of the night, George Hay said the following words: "For the past hour, we have been listening to music largely from Grand Opera, but from now on, we will present 'The Grand Ole Opry'." As audiences for the live show increased, National Life & Accident Insurance's radio venue became too small to accommodate the hordes of fans. They built a larger studio, but it was still not large enough. After several months broadcasting with no live audience, National Life decided to allow the show to move outside its home offices. In October 1934, the Opry moved into then-suburban Hillsboro Theatre (now the Belcourt) before moving to the Dixie Tabernacle in East Nashville on June 13, 1936. The Opry then moved to the War Memorial Auditorium, a downtown venue adjacent to the State Capitol, and a 25-cent admission fee was charged to try to curb the large crowds, but to no avail. In June 1943, the Opry moved to the Ryman Auditorium.
