1963年11月29日：蒙特婁-杜魯道國際機場November 29, 1963: Montréal–Trudeau International Airport

蒙特利尔皮埃尔·埃利奥特·特鲁多国际机场（法语：Aéroport international Pierre-Elliott-Trudeau de Montréal，英语：Montréal-Pierre Elliott Trudeau International Airport；IATA代码：YUL；ICAO代码：CYUL简称蒙特利尔特鲁多机场或特鲁多机场），三跑道，4F级。前称多佛尔国际机场（英语：Dorval International Airport；法语：Aéroport international de Montréal-Dorval）。它位于加拿大魁北克省蒙特利尔市以西，多佛尔市境内，作为一所国际机场服务于蒙特利尔与周边地区。机场由“蒙特利尔机场”（ADM）经营与管理。它是魁北克省最繁忙的机场，也是加拿大第三繁忙的机场（按旅客），排在多伦多皮尔逊国际机场与温哥华国际机场之后。机场提供直飞非洲、中美洲、南美洲、加勒比海地区、欧洲、美国、墨西哥和加拿大其他城市的直飞航班。它也拥有北美最大的免税商店。 蒙特利尔特鲁多机场同时是加拿大最大的航空公司加拿大航空与Air Transat的总部所在地。它刚刚完成了7亿多加元的扩建工程。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「1963年11月29日：蒙特婁-杜魯道國際機場」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

### 补记

还需要补上一层：历史写作最难的地方，不是把事件说得热闹，而是把它的尺度说准。一个日期之所以能进入“历史上的今天”，往往因为它同时具备可被记录的动作、可被辨认的现场和可被后来者继续追问的后果。照片会褪色，报纸会变黄，建筑会被改建，但这些问题不会自动消失。它们会在城市更新、技术迭代和公共记忆的争论中一次次返回。也正因如此，重读这一天，应当同时保留两种眼光：一种贴近现场，看见具体的人在具体的时间做出具体的选择；另一种拉远距离，看见这些选择如何被制度、市场和偶然性放大。两种眼光互相校正，历史才不至于变成单纯的赞歌或单纯的清算。我们把这些图像放在一起，是希望读者先看见，再判断；先理解，再纪念。

Montréal–Trudeau International Airport (IATA: YUL, ICAO: CYUL) (French: Aéroport International Montréal-Trudeau) or Montréal–Trudeau, formerly known and still commonly referred to as Montréal–Dorval International Airport (Aéroport international Montréal-Dorval), is an international airport straddling the border of Dorval and Montreal, Quebec, Canada. It is the only Transport Canada designated international airport serving Montreal and is situated 15 km (9.3 mi) west of Downtown Montreal. The airport terminals are located entirely in the suburb of Dorval, while one runway is located in the Montreal borough of Saint-Laurent. Air Canada, the country's flag carrier, also has its corporate headquarters complex on the Saint-Laurent side of the airport.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

蒙特利尔特鲁多机场的前身是蒙特利尔多佛尔国际机场。多佛尔机场在二战的时候便由于它的天气条件优秀而开始应用。它曾经成为过全北美第三繁忙的机场。之后由于多伦多的兴起，许多航班开始转向多伦多。之后加拿大政府开始大力推广与建设蒙特利尔新机场——蒙特利尔米拉贝尔国际机场，可由于飞往蒙特利尔航线的逐渐减少和多佛尔机场距离市区更近的缘故，新机场被慢慢废弃。加拿大联邦政府于2004年1月1日正式更名蒙特利尔多佛尔国际机场为蒙特利尔皮埃尔·爱略特·特鲁多国际机场，以纪念前总理皮埃尔·特鲁多。 蒙特利尔特鲁多机场共有3条跑道：两条东北-西南向的平行跑道和一条东西向的跑道。

Trudeau is the busiest airport in the province of Quebec and the third-busiest airport in Canada by passenger traffic, with 22.4 million passengers in 2025. It is one of nine Canadian airports with United States border preclearance and is one of the main gateways into Canada with 15.79 million or 71% of its passengers being on non-domestic flights, the highest proportion amongst Canada's airports. YUL has also risen to become the 5th busiest transatlantic hub in North America. The route between Montreal and Paris is Canada's busiest international route and the 6th busiest international route from North America, with more than 1.5 million passengers a year. It is one of three Air Canada hubs and, in that capacity, serves mainly Quebec, the Atlantic Provinces and Eastern Ontario. On an average day, 58,000 passengers transit through Montréal-Trudeau. Airlines servicing Trudeau offer year-round non-stop flights to five continents, namely Africa, Asia, Europe, North and South America. It is one of only two airports in Canada with non-stop flights to five continents, the other being Toronto Pearson International Airport. Trudeau airport is the headquarters of and a large hub for Air Canada, the country's largest airline. It is also the headquarters of Air Inuit and Air Transat, and an operation base for Porter Airlines. It also plays a role in general aviation as home to the headquarters of Innotech-Execair, Starlink, ACASS and Maintenance Repair & Overhaul (MRO) facilities of Air Transat and Air Inuit.

蒙特利尔特鲁多机场设有一座两层航站楼，被分为四个区域：公共区（陆侧出发和到达）、国内指廊（jetty）、国际指廊和美加过境指廊。机场的公共部分（出发层）有两个不同的区域：加拿大国内或国际（美国除外）航班的值机区，及美国方向航班值机区。

The opening and closing of Mirabel Airport The Trudeau government had developed Mirabel Airport to handle an expected growth in international traffic and eventually, to replace Dorval. On November 29, 1975, Mirabel International Airport went into service. With an operations zone of 70 km2 (27 sq mi) and a buffer zone of 290 km2 (110 sq mi), it became the largest airport in the world. Many connecting flights to Canadian centres were transferred to Mirabel and 23 international airlines moved their overseas activities there. As a consequence, Montréal–Dorval was repurposed to serve domestic flights and transborder flights to the United States, to eventually shift these activities to Mirabel in the future and lead to Dorval's closure for redevelopment.

However, the extra traffic never materialized. Mirabel's traffic decreased due to the advent in the 1980s of longer-range jets that did not need to refuel in Montreal before crossing the Atlantic Ocean from the major Western North American cities. Montreal's economic decline in the late 1970s and 1980s also had a significant effect on both airports' traffic, which made Mirabel's additional capacity redundant. Although this redundancy would have been resolved if Dorval was decommissioned as originally intended, public pressure supported Dorval's continued operation due to its closer proximity to downtown Montreal at 20 km (12 mi) instead of 58 km (36 mi) for Mirabel. Another obstacle of the planned transfer from Dorval to Mirabel was Air Canada's desire to keep flights in Dorval with its proximity to AVEOS workshops. In particular, the simultaneous operation of Mirabel (international flights) and Dorval (continental flights) made Montreal less attractive to international airlines. Passengers who used Montreal in transit had to take long bus rides for connections from domestic to international flights (exacerbated by the partially-completed road links and non-existent rail connecting Mirabel to Montreal), unnecessarily complicating their journeys, while Montrealers grew to resent Mirabel as they were forced to travel far out of town for international flights.
