《女王密使》（英语：On Her Majesty's Secret Service）是一部1969年的谍报电影，同时也是第6部詹姆斯·邦德系列电影。为米高梅首次以肖恩·康纳利以外的演员饰演詹姆斯·邦德的电影，是该系列惟一以悲剧剧情作结局的一集，也是乔治·拉兹比首次及惟一一次饰演詹姆斯·邦德的电影。 贝洛福在瑞士山区的疗养中心，将病毒注入女病患的身上，借以散播病毒，企图以毁灭所有农作物为要胁，以掌握世界的主权，詹姆斯·邦德（乔治·拉兹比饰）发现内情后，是否能及时摧毁实验室，挽救一场人间浩劫？

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「1969年12月18日：鐵金剛勇破雪山堡」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

### 补记

还需要补上一层：历史写作最难的地方，不是把事件说得热闹，而是把它的尺度说准。一个日期之所以能进入“历史上的今天”，往往因为它同时具备可被记录的动作、可被辨认的现场和可被后来者继续追问的后果。照片会褪色，报纸会变黄，建筑会被改建，但这些问题不会自动消失。它们会在城市更新、技术迭代和公共记忆的争论中一次次返回。也正因如此，重读这一天，应当同时保留两种眼光：一种贴近现场，看见具体的人在具体的时间做出具体的选择；另一种拉远距离，看见这些选择如何被制度、市场和偶然性放大。两种眼光互相校正，历史才不至于变成单纯的赞歌或单纯的清算。我们把这些图像放在一起，是希望读者先看见，再判断；先理解，再纪念。

On Her Majesty's Secret Service is a 1969 epic spy film and the sixth in the James Bond series produced by Eon Productions. It is based on the 1963 novel by Ian Fleming, whose title is a derivative of the term "On Her Majesty's Service". Following Sean Connery's decision to retire from the role after You Only Live Twice, Eon selected George Lazenby, a model with no prior acting credits, to play the part of James Bond. During filming, Lazenby announced that he would play the role of Bond only once. Connery returned to portray Bond in 1971's Diamonds Are Forever. In the film, Bond faces Blofeld (Telly Savalas), who is planning to hold the world to ransom by threatening to render all food plants and livestock infertile through the actions of a group of brainwashed "angels of death". Along the way Bond meets, falls in love with, and eventually marries Contessa Teresa di Vicenzo (Diana Rigg).

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

男主角詹姆斯·邦德罕有地在该片里跟女主角正式结了婚，随后幽灵党向邦德乱枪扫射做为报复，女主角在影片结束前即死去。 由于演员肖恩·康纳利的辞演，使得制片人艾伯特·柏考利与哈利·萨斯门举行全世界最大的试镜活动，前来试镜的有高达400多位演员，最后录用了来自澳洲的素人演员乔治·拉兹比，1968年10月21日正式开机，场景大部分在瑞士阿尔卑斯山等地方拍摄。

The film marked the directorial debut of Peter R. Hunt, who had served as a film editor and second unit director on previous films in the series: it would be his only Bond film as director. Hunt, along with producers Albert R. Broccoli and Harry Saltzman, decided to produce a more realistic film that would follow the novel closely. It was shot in Switzerland, England, and Portugal from October 1968 to May 1969. Although its cinema release was not as lucrative as its predecessor You Only Live Twice, On Her Majesty's Secret Service was still one of the top-performing films of the year. Critical reviews upon release were mixed, but the film's reputation has improved greatly over time and it is now regarded as one of the strongest entries in the series as well as one of the most faithful adaptations of a Fleming novel. James Bond saves a woman on the beach from an attempted suicide by drowning, and later meets her again in a casino. The woman, Contessa Teresa "Tracy" di Vicenzo, invites Bond to her hotel room to thank him, but when he arrives he is attacked by an unidentified man. After subduing him, Bond returns to his own room and finds Tracy there; she claims that she was unaware of the attacker's presence. The next morning, Bond is kidnapped by several men, including the one he fought, who take him to meet Marc-Ange Draco, the head of the European crime syndicate Unione Corse. Draco reveals that Tracy is his only daughter and tells Bond of her troubled past, offering him one million pounds if he will marry her.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

由于拉兹比没有任何关于电影的演出经历，所以特别因着他灵敏的身手而加重他动作戏的部分，他都演得很好，但是在拍摄前后一直传出拉兹比与剧组闹不和的消息，使得拉兹比的媒体形象被大打折扣，最后直到1969年11月传出拉兹比辞演的消息。 然而本片在美国上映表现没有前一部《雷霆谷》来的好，后来剧组在下一部《永远的钻石》试图请肖恩·康纳利回归。

Posing as Bray, Bond goes to meet Blofeld, who has established a clinical allergy research institute atop Piz Gloria in the Swiss Alps. Bond meets twelve young women, later referred to by Blofeld as his "angels of death", who are patients at the institute's clinic, apparently cured of various allergies. After dinner, Bond goes to the room of one patient, Ruby, who wrote her room number on his bare leg. At midnight, while still with Ruby, Bond discovers that the women go into a sleep-induced hypnotic state while Blofeld implants subliminal audio instructions. In fact, they are being brainwashed to distribute bacteriological warfare agents throughout the world. Bond tries to trick Blofeld into leaving Switzerland so that MI6 can arrest him without violating Swiss sovereignty. Blofeld refuses and Bond is eventually caught by henchwoman Irma Bunt. Blofeld reveals that he identified Bond due to an error regarding the Bleuchamp estate, and has him imprisoned after revealing that he has killed his colleague Shaun Campbell. Bond eventually makes his escape by skiing down from Piz Gloria while Blofeld and his men give chase. Simultaneously, the "angels of death" depart Piz Gloria, escorted by Bunt. Tracy finds Bond in the village of Lauterbrunnen and helps him escape in her car. Pursued by Bunt and her men, they drive into a stock-car race to shake them off. A blizzard forces them off the road and they shelter in a remote barn. Bond professes his love to Tracy and proposes marriage to her, which she happily accepts.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

由于饰演该片女反派尔玛的德国演员伊尔斯·史蒂佩特于电影上映前过世，因此尔玛也成为007电影系列中唯一没有描绘其下场的反派。 由路易斯·阿姆斯特朗（Louis Armstrong）演唱的《We Have All the Time in the World》。

Back in London at M's office, Bond is informed that Blofeld intends to hold the world to ransom by threatening to destroy its agriculture using his brainwashed women. Blofeld demands amnesty for all past crimes and to be recognised as the current Count de Bleuchamp. M tells 007 that the ransom will be paid and forbids him to mount a rescue mission. Bond instead enlists Draco and his forces to attack Blofeld's headquarters, while also rescuing Tracy from Blofeld's captivity. The facility is destroyed, and Blofeld escapes the destruction alone in a bobsleigh, with Bond pursuing him. The chase ends when Blofeld is trapped in a collision with the branch of a tree. Bond and Tracy marry in Portugal, then drive away in Bond's Aston Martin DBS decorated with flowers. When Bond pulls over to the roadside to remove them, Blofeld and Bunt drive past and machine-gun their car. Tracy is killed and Bond cradles her dead body.
