1958年12月23日：东京铁塔December 23, 1958: Tokyo Tower

东京铁塔（日语：東京タワー／とうきょうタワー，罗马化：Tōkyō tawā，中文亦译为东京塔），正式名称为日本电波塔（日本電波塔／にっぽんでんぱとう，Nippon denpatō），是位于日本东京芝公园的一座电波塔。建于1958年。高332.9米（1,092 英尺），是日本第二高的结构物，仅次于东京晴空塔。该塔结构以巴黎埃菲尔铁塔为范本而建造，漆成白色和国际橙色，以符合航空安全法规。 东京铁塔自完工以来即成为东京著名地标与观光景点，并经常出现在和东京有关的大量流行作品及文化等，其设施的主要收入来源是旅游业和天线租赁。共有超过1.5亿人参观了这座塔。FootTown是一座位于塔楼正下方的四层建筑，并设有博物馆、餐厅和商店设施，旅客可从此处搭乘电梯参观两个观景台。两层的大展示场（以前称为主观景台）高150米（490 英尺），而较小的顶层展示场（以前称为“特别观景台”）则高达249.6米（819 英尺）。东京铁塔每五年重新粉刷一次，需要一年的时间才能完成。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「1958年12月23日：东京铁塔」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

Tokyo Tower (東京タワー, Tōkyō Tawā; pronounced [toːkʲoː taꜜɰᵝaː] ), also known by its official name Japan Radio Tower (日本電波塔, Nippon denpatō) is a communications and observation tower in the district of Shiba-koen in Minato, Tokyo, Japan, completed in 1958. At 332.9 meters (1,092 feet), it was the tallest tower in Japan until the construction of Tokyo Skytree in 2012. It is a lattice tower inspired by the Eiffel Tower, and is painted white and international orange to comply with air safety regulations. The tower's main sources of income are antenna leasing and tourism, and its five-story base building FootTown houses a number of restaurants, gift shops, and other attractions. Departing from it, guests can visit two observation decks: the two-story Main Deck at 150 m (490 ft), and the smaller Top Deck at 249.6 m (819 ft).

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

1961年，发射天线被添加到塔铁塔上用于广播和电视广播，现在依然为包括日本广播协会、TBS电视台和富士电视台等日本媒体播放信号。 东京铁塔由建筑师内藤多仲与日建设计株式会社共同设计。高332.6米，较艾菲尔铁塔的324米高出8.6米，较东京天空树矮301.4米，比广州塔矮267.4米，是世界第三高的自立式铁塔，重4,000吨，与重7,300吨的埃菲尔铁塔相比重量大幅减轻，开工于1957年6月29日，1958年10月14日竣工，12月7日正式公开，12月23日举行竣工仪式，开始了正式营业。三分之一用于建塔的钢铁，来自朝鲜战争时美军的坦克废铁。 东京铁塔在150米处设有大瞭望台，249.9米处设有特别瞭望台，可一览东京都内景色，晴朗之日可远眺富士山。铁塔正下方建有四层的东京铁塔楼，除设置了通往展望台的出入口外，还设有东京铁塔水族馆和各种纪念品小卖铺。

In 1961, transmission antennas were added. They are used for radio and television broadcasting and now broadcast signals for media outlets such as NHK, TBS Television, and Fuji Television. The height of the tower was not suitable for Japan's planned terrestrial digital broadcasting planned for July 2011, and for the Tokyo area. A taller digital broadcasting tower, known as Tokyo Skytree, was completed on 29 February 2012. Tokyo Tower has become a prominent landmark and frequently appears in media set in Tokyo. A large broadcasting tower was needed in the Kantō region after NHK, Japan's public broadcasting station, began television broadcasting in 1953. Private broadcasting companies began operating in the months following the construction of NHK's own transmission tower. This communications boom led the Japanese government to believe that transmission towers would soon be built all over Tokyo, eventually overrunning the city. The proposed solution was the construction of one large tower capable of transmitting to the entire region. Furthermore, because of the country's postwar boom in the 1950s, Japan was searching for a monument to symbolize its national recovery from World War II, as one of the countries most ravaged by the war. Hisakichi Maeda, founder and president of Nippon Denpatō, the tower's owner and operator, originally planned for the tower to be taller than the Empire State Building, which at 381 m (1,250 ft) was the highest structure in the world at the time. However, the plan fell through because of the lack of both funds and materials.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

东京铁塔的颜色为红白相间，并不是因为日本国旗，而是因为航空交通管制规定以利识别的缘故。近年来大众的景观要求提升，铁塔不再有颜色限制，但原有的涂装极具特色，就这样保留下来。灯光照明则由世界著名照明设计师，石井干子设计主持，照明时间为日落到午夜。灯光颜色随季节变化，夏季为白色，春、秋、冬季为橙色。 该塔除主要用于发送电视、电台等各种广播信号外、还在大地震发生时发送JR列车停止信号，兼有航标、风向风速测量、温度测量等功能。由于日本的模拟电视播送于2011年7月24日终止，由数字电视取而代之，为改善收讯质量，从2013年5月31日起改由位于东京墨田区、标高634米的东京晴空塔取代东京铁塔承担的广播信号发射功能，东京铁塔则成为备援之信号发射站点。

Tachū Naitō, designer of tall buildings in Japan, was chosen to design the newly proposed tower. Looking to the Western world for inspiration, Naitō based his design on the Eiffel Tower in Paris, France. With the help of engineering company Nikken Sekkei Ltd., Naitō claimed his design could withstand earthquakes with twice the intensity of the 1923 Great Kantō earthquake or typhoons with wind speeds of up to 220 km/h (140 mph). The new construction project attracted hundreds of tobi (鳶), traditional Japanese construction workers who specialized in the construction of high-rise structures. The Takenaka Corporation broke ground in June 1957 and each day at least 400 laborers worked on the tower. It was constructed of steel, a third of which was scrap metal taken from US tanks damaged in the Korean War. When the 90-meter (300 ft)-long antenna was bolted into place on 14 October 1958, Tokyo Tower was the tallest freestanding tower in the world, taking the title from the Eiffel Tower by 9 m (30 ft).

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

塔顶的天线曾于2011年3月11日受东日本大震灾影响而弯曲，修复工程于2012年2月1日开始，在修复期间东京铁塔的高度曾被暂时截短为315米，最终于2012年8月3日修复完成，并恢复成原来的高度。2012年7月10日，东京铁塔在修复塔顶时，在301米处发现一颗破旧的棒球，但是在非常高的地方出现球很怪异，所以将其称为“谜之球”，后续该球被放在大展示场展示。2013年5月15日宣布，设置在东京铁塔下长达半个世纪之久的“南极桦太犬群像雕塑”的搬迁工作正式开始。 东京铁塔的所有及营运机构为“株式会社TOKYO TOWER”（株式会社TOKYO TOWER），1957年成立时名为“日本电波塔株式会社”（日本電波塔株式会社），在2018年12月23日适逢东京铁塔开业60周年之际更名为“株式会社东京铁塔”（株式会社東京タワー），至2019年10月1日又以罗马字改为现名。

Though physically taller than the Eiffel Tower, Tokyo Tower weighs about 4,000 tons, 3,300 less than the Eiffel Tower as it is significantly thinner and simpler in construction. It was opened to the public on 23 December 1958 at a final cost of ¥2.8 billion ($8.4 million in 1958). Tokyo Tower was mortgaged for ¥10 billion in 2000. It was the tallest artificial structure in Japan until April 2012, when it was surpassed by the Tokyo Skytree. Planned as an antenna for telecommunications and brightly colored in accordance with the time's Aviation Law, the tower's two panoramic observatories are mostly frequented by tourists today. The tower constitutes a clear reference point in the city's skyline, forming a strong landmark, both night and day. The tower is periodically repainted in a process that takes about a year to complete. This traditionally occurred every five years, but the latest painting in 2019 utilized a new, more durable coating that extended the official timetable to seven years, projecting the next painting for 2026.
