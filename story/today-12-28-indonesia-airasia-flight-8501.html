印尼亚洲航空8501号班机是一趟从印度尼西亚泗水飞往新加坡樟宜机场的国际线客运航班，2014年12月28日，一架执飞该航班的空中客车A320-216客机（注册编号：PK-AXC），在飞行一段时间后于爪哇海上空与空中管制人员失去联系。尽管在失去联系前机组人员曾经要求更改路线，但被印尼空中交通控制中心拒绝，最终造成机上155名乘客和7名机组成员全数罹难。同年12月30日，搜救人员在卡里马塔海峡找到失踪航班的碎片以及一些乘客的遗体。 当地时间2014年12月28日清晨05:35，亚洲航空公司8501号班机于印尼朱安达国际机场起飞，启程前住新加坡樟宜机场。据印尼飞航当局AirNav表示，航班起飞前，飞行员曾提升飞行高度以避开暴风雨，但因航道交通壅塞而被拒绝。期后于航班飞行期间，飞行员提出要求向左转以避过雷暴，并即时获得批准，于是飞机改变航道向左转了约11公里。接着，飞行员再次重申原本的请求，要求准许飞机爬升高度以避开恶劣天气。及后于06:14，控制塔人员通知机长因上方有其他飞机，只可飞行到3万4千呎时，飞机方面就已经没有回应，与雅加达空中管制失去联系。在失去联系之时，航机仍处于印尼管制范围内，估计正在飞越爪哇海加里曼丹和爪哇岛之间的风暴群，并有着正常的巡航高度及速度。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「2014年12月28日：印尼亚洲航空8501号班机空难」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

Indonesia AirAsia Flight 8501 was a scheduled international passenger flight operated by Indonesia AirAsia from Surabaya, Indonesia, to Singapore. On 28 December 2014, the Airbus A320 flying the route crashed into the Java Sea, killing all 162 of the people on board. When search operations ended in March 2015, only 116 bodies had been recovered. As of 2026, this is the only fatal accident involving AirAsia and its affiliates. In December 2015, the Indonesian National Transportation Safety Committee (KNKT or NTSC) released a report concluding that a non-critical malfunction in the rudder control system prompted the captain to perform a non-standard reset of the on-board flight control computers. Control of the aircraft was subsequently lost, resulting in a stall and uncontrolled descent into the sea. Miscommunication between the two pilots was cited as a contributing factor.

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

失事客机机型为空中客车A320-216，生产编号3648，注册编号为PK-AXC，由德国Doric租赁公司拥有。客机的首航于2008年9月25日，并于同年10月15日租予印尼亚洲航空。直至事故发生前客机已执行超过13,600航班中积累约23,000飞行时数，并曾于2014年11月16日接受定期维修。此客机带有两个CFM56发动机，设计载客量为180。 机组包括正、副机长，4名机舱服务员及1名工程师。机长(53岁)总共有20,537小时的飞行经验，其中6,100小时在亚航飞行，副机长(46岁)在亚航共有2,275小时的飞行经验。 印尼亚洲航空公布乘客及机组人员的国籍，包括144名成人，17名儿童和1名婴儿，总计162人，其中有7名机组人员。

Indonesia AirAsia Flight 8501 was a scheduled flight from Surabaya, East Java, Indonesia, to Singapore on Sunday, 28 December 2014. It was scheduled to depart Juanda International Airport at 05:20 Western Indonesian Time (WIB, UTC+7) and arrive at Singapore Changi Airport at 08:30 Singapore Standard Time (SST, UTC+8). The aircraft took off at 05:35 WIB and reached its cruising altitude at flight level (FL) 320 at 9,800 metres (32,000 ft) fourteen minutes later. It joined air route M635, heading north-west out over the western Java Sea. The plane was in contact with Jakarta air traffic control (ATC). The flight was normal until 06:00 when an electronic centralised aircraft monitor (ECAM) memo was displayed, along with a master caution light, to indicate a fault with the rudder limiter system. Captain Iriyanto read the actions for fixing this failure, and rebooted the aircraft's two Flight Augmentation Computers (FACs). The same fault recurred at 06:09, and the captain fixed it in the same way. At 06:11, the pilots turned fifteen degrees to the left to avoid inclement weather, and contacted Jakarta ATC to request a climb to FL 380 at 12,000 metres (38,000 ft) for the same reason. The controller could not give immediate permission for this due to other aircraft in the vicinity, and instructed them to wait.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

失去联系后，搜索及救援任务由印度尼西亚民航局主导。派遣队伍主要集中于爪哇海勿里洞岛附近海域进行搜索，但由于天黑及天气恶劣，搜救工作于当地时间12月28日下午07:45暂停，并于早上6时再次恢复。 印尼国家救灾机构和海军派出6艘船和1架飞机赶往飞机失联海域搜寻。新加坡共和国空军部队和海军也派出两架C-130运输机参与搜索。马来西亚成立了一个救援协调中心，部署三艘船舰和一架C-130运输机参与搜救行动。

While the pilots were waiting for permission to climb, the rudder limiter problem occurred for a third time, and for the third time the captain reset the FAC computers. When the memo displayed for the fourth time, Captain Iriyanto decided to reset the FAC circuit breakers (CB). He had previously seen this action being performed by a ground engineer, and believed that it was acceptable to do so in flight. The FAC circuit breakers were reset at 06:16:45, with immediate consequences, as this action not only reset the FAC computers but also disconnected the autopilot and autothrottle, and the flight control law changed from Normal to Alternate. It allowed the aircraft to roll to the left, and by the time First Officer Plesel reacted to this it was banked at 54 degrees.

第 3 章Chapter 3

余波Aftermath

搜救工作第二日早上6时再次展开，并转向勿里洞岛方向进行搜索。印尼方面动用12双船只、5架飞机、3架直升机和多艘军舰进行搜救；新加坡和马来西亚亦继续派出船只和飞机帮忙；澳洲皇家空军亦加入搜救工作，派出一架配有海面搜索雷达的AP-3C猎户星座式飞机。 美国在收到印尼方面向其寻求协助后，其第七舰队将搜索一个未被进行搜索的海域。

First Officer Plesel, possibly spatially disoriented due to the roll sensation, over-corrected twice: first by making a sharp right bank input and then a sharp left bank input. After that, at 06:17, Plesel made a nose-up input on his side-stick, causing the aircraft to enter a steep climb at a 24-degree nose-up pitch. The captain gave a confusing direction to "pull down", which "bears an internal contradiction as 'pull' suggests up, while 'down' means down." In just 54 seconds, the aircraft climbed from 32,000 feet to 38,500 ft (11,700 m), exceeding a climb rate of 10,000 ft/min (50 m/s). It then entered a stall, at around 06:17:40, descending at a rate of up to 20,000 ft/min (100 m/s). The aircraft also began a turn to the left, forming at least one complete circle before disappearing from radar at 06:18:44. At 06:20:35, the flight data recorder stopped recording. The CVR stopped recording one second later, at 06:20:36. The aircraft crashed into the Java Sea and was destroyed. All 162 people on board were killed instantly upon impact. Its last recorded position was over the Java Sea, Karimata Strait between the islands of Belitung and Kalimantan (3.3708°S 109.6911°E﻿ / -3.3708; 109.6911﻿ (AirAsia Flight 8501 last transponder signal)). The aircraft crashed in the Java Sea, Karimata Strait between the islands of Belitung and Borneo (3.623°S 109.712°E﻿ / -3.623; 109.712﻿ (AirAsia Flight 8501 crash site)). The cockpit voice recorder captured multiple warnings, including a stall warning, sounding in the cockpit during the final minutes of the flight.
