爱尔兰电视电台（爱尔兰语：Radio Telefís Éireann，简写为RTÉ，英文读作“RTE”）是爱尔兰共和国的公共广播公司，为其电视台、电台和网上业务制作节目。 爱尔兰电视电台的前身为1926年1月1日开播，总部设于都柏林的2RN电台。1960年6月1日，依据《1960年版广播法》（Broadcasting Authority Act 1960），2RN改名为爱尔兰电台（Radio Éireann），总部仍设于都柏林。1961年12月31日，爱尔兰电台附设的爱尔兰电视台（Télifis Éireann）开播。之后，依据《1966年版广播法》（Broadcasting Authority (Amendment) Act 1966），爱尔兰电台改名为爱尔兰广播电视，爱尔兰语名称为“Radio Telefís Éireann”。1978年，爱尔兰电视电台开播了其第二个电视频道。之后，依据《2009年版广播法》第113条（Broadcasting Act 2009 (Section 113)），爱尔兰广播电视爱尔兰语名称改为“Raidió Teilifís Éireann”。

## 史料补记

### 一、时代条件

把「1961年12月31日：愛爾蘭廣播電視」放回它发生的年代，会发现这一天并不是孤立的新闻标题，而是一连串技术条件、城市生活与个人选择交汇后的结果。历史照片之所以重要，不在于它替我们给出结论，而在于它把当时的光线、材料、人物距离和现场秩序保留下来。档案里的每一张底片、每一次曝光和每一行手写说明，都在提醒后来者：事件发生的那一刻，世界并不知道自己将被如何讲述。对于研究者来说，首先要把事件还原到它自己的时间尺度里，理解当时可用的工具、可以动员的人力和尚未被命名的风险，然后再讨论它在更长历史中的位置。

### 二、影像现场

从影像史的角度看，这类事件往往同时留下两种痕迹。一种是正式的记录：报纸头版、机构公报、工程图纸和庆典合影，它们把事件整理成可供引用的事实；另一种是边缘的痕迹：临时拍下的街景、未及刊出的照片、参与者留下的笔记和后来被反复翻印的明信片。两者之间常有缝隙，而缝隙正是历史研究最有价值的地方。我们在这一天重看这些图像，不是为了把过去变成简单的纪念，而是为了理解当时的人如何在有限的信息中做出判断：哪些细节被保留，哪些声音被剪掉，哪些面孔被安排在画面的中央。

### 三、后续回声

对今天的读者而言，这个日期的意义还在于它与更长的历史脉络相连。城市在事件之后改变了自己的道路与天际线，技术在被使用之后才逐渐显出后果，普通人的日常也在新闻退潮后继续。把故事讲到这一层，图片就不再只是插图，而是证据、情绪和时间的容器。它让我们看到：所谓“历史上的今天”，既是某一天的回声，也是今天仍在继续的问题。重访这一天，不是为了替过去盖棺定论，而是为了把那些仍然影响当下的选择、代价与记忆，重新放到可以讨论的位置。

### 补记

还需要补上一层：历史写作最难的地方，不是把事件说得热闹，而是把它的尺度说准。一个日期之所以能进入“历史上的今天”，往往因为它同时具备可被记录的动作、可被辨认的现场和可被后来者继续追问的后果。照片会褪色，报纸会变黄，建筑会被改建，但这些问题不会自动消失。它们会在城市更新、技术迭代和公共记忆的争论中一次次返回。也正因如此，重读这一天，应当同时保留两种眼光：一种贴近现场，看见具体的人在具体的时间做出具体的选择；另一种拉远距离，看见这些选择如何被制度、市场和偶然性放大。两种眼光互相校正，历史才不至于变成单纯的赞歌或单纯的清算。我们把这些图像放在一起，是希望读者先看见，再判断；先理解，再纪念。

Raidió Teilifís Éireann (pronounced [ˈɾˠadʲiːoː ˈtʲɛlʲəfʲiːʃ ˈeːɾʲən̪ˠ] ; Irish for 'Radio [and] Television of Ireland'; RTÉ) is an Irish public service broadcaster. It produces and broadcasts programmes on television, radio and online. The radio service began on 1 January 1926, and regular television broadcasts began on 31 December 1961, making RTÉ one of the oldest continuously operating public service broadcasters in the world. It is headquartered in the Donnybrook district of Dublin, with additional news offices and studios across Ireland. RTÉ is a statutory body, overseen by a board appointed by the Irish government, with general management in the hands of a committee of senior managers – currently an interim leadership team – headed by the director general. RTÉ is regulated by Coimisiún na Meán (lit. 'Media Commission').

第 1 章Chapter 1

缘起Origins

爱尔兰电视电台（爱尔兰语：Radio Telefís Éireann，简写为RTÉ，英文读作“RTE”）是爱尔兰共和国的公共广播公司，为其电视台、电台和网上业务制作节目。

The current network consists of four main television channels (RTÉ One, RTÉ2, RTÉ KIDSjr and RTÉ News) and four FM radio stations (RTÉ Radio 1, RTÉ 2fm, RTÉ Lyric FM and RTÉ Raidió na Gaeltachta). RTÉ also has a number of digital radio services; RTÉ Gold is its only online station with a full schedule. The broadcaster operates a number of online services, including a news website and an app, as well as the streaming service RTÉ Player. RTÉ previously owned 50% of the sports broadcaster GAAGO, which in turn operated LOI TV. Radio Éireann, RTÉ's predecessor and at the time a section of the Department of Posts and Telegraphs, was one of 23 founding organisations of the European Broadcasting Union in 1950. RTÉ also publishes weekly listings and a lifestyle magazine, the RTÉ Guide. Broadcasting in Ireland began in 1926 with the radio station 2RN in Dublin. From that date until June 1960, this broadcasting service (later Radio Éireann) operated as a section of the Department of Posts and Telegraphs. Those working for the service were directly employed by the Irish government and regarded as civil servants.

第 2 章Chapter 2

现场The event

爱尔兰电视电台的前身为1926年1月1日开播，总部设于都柏林的2RN电台。1960年6月1日，依据《1960年版广播法》（Broadcasting Authority Act 1960），2RN改名为爱尔兰电台（Radio Éireann），总部仍设于都柏林。1961年12月31日，爱尔兰电台附设的爱尔兰电视台（Télifis Éireann）开播。之后，依据《1966年版广播法》（Broadcasting Authority (Amendment) Act 1966），爱尔兰电台改名为爱尔兰广播电视，爱尔兰语名称为“Radio Telefís Éireann”。1978年，爱尔兰电视电台开播了其第二个电视频道。之后，依据《2009年版广播法》第113条（Broadcasting Act 2009 (Section 113)），爱尔兰广播电视爱尔兰语名称改为“Raidió Teilifís Éireann”。

RTÉ was established on 1 June 1960 (as the Radio Éireann Authority) under the Broadcasting Authority Act 1960, the principal legislation under which it still operates. The existing Radio Éireann service was transferred to the new Authority, which was also made responsible for the new television service (Telefís Éireann). The television service started broadcasting on 31 December 1961, from the Kippure transmitter site near Dublin. Eamonn Andrews was the first chairman of Radio Éireann; the first director general was Edward Roth. At the suggestion of Áine Ní Cheanainn, the Broadcasting Authority (Amendment) Act 1966 changed the name of the Authority to Radio Telefís Éireann; both the radio and television services became known as RTÉ that year. Section 113 of the Broadcasting Act 2009 changed the name of the organisation from "Radio Telefís Éireann" to "Raidió Teilifís Éireann", to reflect the current standard spelling of the name in Irish.

余波Aftermath

爱尔兰电视电台的前身为1926年1月1日开播，总部设于都柏林的2RN电台。1960年6月1日，依据《1960年版广播法》（Broadcasting Authority Act 1960），2RN改名为爱尔兰电台（Radio Éireann），总部仍设于都柏林。1961年12月31日，爱尔兰电台附设的爱尔兰电视台（Télifis Éireann）开播。之后，依据《1966年版广播法》（Broadcasting Authority (Amendment) Act 1966），爱尔兰电台改名为爱尔兰广播电视，爱尔兰语名称为“Radio Telefís Éireann”。1978年，爱尔兰电视电台开播了其第二个电视频道。之后，依据《2009年版广播法》第113条（Broadcasting Act 2009 (Section 113)），爱尔兰广播电视爱尔兰语名称改为“Raidió Teilifís Éireann”。

The letter É in RTÉ is often pronounced as the English letter E. However, in the Irish language, É is pronounced [eː]. Under Section 31 of the Broadcasting Authority Act 1960, the Minister for Posts and Telegraphs of the day could direct RTÉ "not to broadcast any matter, or any matter of any particular class". In 1971, the first such directive was issued by Gerry Collins, directing RTÉ not to broadcast "any matter that could be calculated to promote the aims or activities of any organisation which engages in, promotes, encourages or advocates the attaining of any particular objective by violent means". A year later, Collins dismissed the entire RTÉ Authority over a report of an interview with Seán Mac Stíofáin, the chief of staff of the Provisional Irish Republican Army (Provisional IRA). RTÉ reporter Kevin O'Kelly had reported, not broadcast, his taped interview with Mac Stíofáin. He was briefly jailed for contempt in a court case arising out of the interview when Mac Stíofáin was charged with IRA membership. O'Kelly refused to identify Mac Stíofáin as the voice in his unbroadcast interview. The interview tape was seized from his house by the Garda Síochána (the police).
