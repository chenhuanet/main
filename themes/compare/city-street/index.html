城市与街道City and Street

城市可以从高处被画成整体，街道只能从人群内部被拍成片段。把全景与街拍放在一起，会看到同一个城市如何同时需要秩序和混乱：一边让建筑说话，一边让路人、车辆和招牌争夺画面。The city can be painted as a whole from above; the street can only be photographed as fragments from inside the crowd. Putting panorama and street picture together shows how one city needs both order and disorder: one lets buildings speak, the other lets passer-by, vehicle and sign compete for the frame.

01把《《僧侣向弟子布道，有俗家听众在场，来自巴瓦德瓦·苏里（Bhavadeva-suri）的帕什瓦那塔·查里特拉（帕什瓦尊者的生平和故事）的第2页（正面）》》（1270）与《1932年11月8日：澳大利亚国防军》（1932）放在一起：相隔约 662 年，一边用蛋彩处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Monk preaching to a disciple with a lay audience, folio 2 (recto) from a Parshvanatha Charitra (Life and Stories of Lord Parshva) of Bhavadeva-suri” (1270) beside “November 8, 1932: Australian Defence Force” (1932): about 662 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 蛋彩, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
