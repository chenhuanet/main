灾难与崇高Disaster and the Sublime

灾难图像总在崇高与责任之间摇摆：烟柱、火焰和废墟让自然显得巨大，也让人显得渺小。把预兆、战争、火灾和火山放在一起，问题不是“美不美”，而是谁被允许从远处观看。Disaster images oscillate between the sublime and responsibility: smoke, fire and ruin make nature huge and people small. Bringing omen, war, fire and eruption together asks not whether it is beautiful, but who is allowed to watch from afar.

01把《《马努奇赫尔与图尔之战（《列王纪》）》》（1605）与《1933年2月25日：遊騎兵號航空母艦 (CV-4)》（1933）放在一起：相隔约 328 年，一边用蛋彩处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Battle between Manuchihr and Tur, from a Shah-nama (Book of Kings) of Firdausi (Persian, c. 934–1020)” (1605) beside “February 25, 1933: USS Ranger (CV-4)” (1933): about 328 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 蛋彩, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
