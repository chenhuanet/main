灾难图像The Disaster Image

灾难可以画成神意、历史或道德教训，也可以拍成废墟、救援和伤亡的现场。两种图像都在替不在场的人安排观看位置：一个让你从远处理解，一个让你误以为自己就在现场。Disaster can be painted as providence, history or moral lesson, or photographed as ruin, rescue and casualty. Both arrange a viewing position for those absent: one lets you understand from afar, the other makes you mistake yourself for a witness.

并置Pairings

01把《《占星师预言，新生的王子将在十三岁时遭遇一场灾难，但他将能够克服这场灾难，出自图提·纳玛（鹦鹉记）》》（1555）与《1915年5月3日：英國皇家空軍》（1915）放在一起：相隔约 360 年，一边用蛋彩处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “The astrologer predicts a calamity for the newly born prince in his thirteenth year, but one which he would be able to overcome, from a Tuti-nama (Tales of a Parrot): Eighth Night” (1555) beside “May 3, 1915: Royal Air Force” (1915): about 360 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 蛋彩, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
