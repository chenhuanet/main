劳动身体The Labouring Body

劳动身体既可以被画成健康的、有力的、属于土地的身体，也可以被拍成疲惫的、受伤的、被机器包围的身体。两种形象都真实，但选择哪一种，取决于谁在委托、谁在观看、谁需要证明劳动的价值。The labouring body can be painted as healthy, strong and rooted in land, or photographed as tired, injured and surrounded by machinery. Both images are real, but the choice depends on who commissions, who looks and who needs to prove the value of work.

并置Pairings

01把《《月下山景》》（1200）与《1893：芝加哥的白色城市》（1893）放在一起：相隔约 693 年，一边用绢本水墨处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Mountain Landscape in Moonlight” (1200) beside “1893: Chicago's White City” (1893): about 693 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 绢本水墨, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
