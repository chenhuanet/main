夜与光Night and Light

夜不是没有光，而是光变得有选择。绘画用月光、烛火和阴影制造神秘，摄影用路灯、霓虹、闪光和长曝光制造证据。把两种夜放在一起，会看到光如何同时是技术、情绪和权力。Night is not the absence of light but light becoming selective. Painting makes mystery from moonlight, candle and shadow; photography makes evidence from street lamp, neon, flash and long exposure. Putting two nights together shows light as technique, mood and power at once.

01把《《月光下小酌》》（1189）与《1933年2月25日：遊騎兵號航空母艦 (CV-4)》（1933）放在一起：相隔约 744 年，一边用绢本水墨处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Drinking in the Moonlight” (1189) beside “February 25, 1933: USS Ranger (CV-4)” (1933): about 744 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 绢本水墨, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
