肖像与面具Portrait and Mask

一边是画出来的身份，一边是拍下来的在场。肖像与面具不是“画得像不像、照片真不真”的问题，而是两种媒介如何用姿态、光线、服装和裁切，把一个人变成可被社会识别的形象。One side is painted identity, the other photographed presence. Portrait and mask is not a question of likeness versus truth, but of how two media turn a person into a socially recognisable image through posture, light, dress and crop.

并置Pairings

01把《《主教圣人与供养人》》（1400）与《1917年12月6日：三硝基甲苯》（1917）放在一起：相隔约 517 年，一边用蛋彩处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “A Bishop Saint with a Donor (Saint Louis of Toulouse?)” (1400) beside “December 6, 1917: TNT” (1917): about 517 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 蛋彩, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
