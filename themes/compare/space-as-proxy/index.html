太空作为代理Space as Proxy

太空图像很少直接呈现天体，它呈现的是代理物：塔罗牌上的行星、计算出的圆点、探测器传回的像素、宇航员的脚印。每一种代理都把距离、风险和权力重新分配。Space images rarely present a body directly; they present proxies: a planet on a tarot card, a calculated dot, pixels returned by a probe, an astronaut's footprint. Every proxy redistributes distance, risk and power.

01把《《朱庇特（木星），天球系列第46号》》（1460）与《1949年1月26日：海爾望遠鏡》（1949）放在一起：相隔约 489 年，一边用铜版画处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Jupiter (from the Tarocchi, series A: Firmaments of the Universe, #46)” (1460) beside “January 26, 1949: Hale Telescope” (1949): about 489 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 铜版画, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
