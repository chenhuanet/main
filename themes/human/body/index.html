身体Body

身体被画成美的、劳动的、受难的、被观看的。绘画用线条和衣褶写身体，摄影用快门和姿势抓住身体。The body is painted as beautiful, labouring, suffering and looked at. Painting writes the body in line and drapery; photography catches it in shutter and pose.

论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

身体在图像里从来不是中性的。绘画用线条、衣褶、肤色和解剖把身体变成理想、欲望或受难的容器；摄影用快门、姿势和光线把身体变成可辨认的个体，也把劳动、疾病、运动和衰老留在画面里。两种媒介都训练观众怎样看身体：什么可以露，什么必须遮，谁的疼痛可以被展示，谁的身体只配做背景。把绘画侧与摄影侧并置，不是比较谁更“自然”，而是看不同材料如何把身体写成社会规范，也看被画、被拍的人如何在镜头前争取一点自己的姿态。The body is never neutral in an image. Painting turns it into a vessel of ideal, desire or suffering through line, drapery, skin and anatomy; photography turns it into an identifiable individual through shutter, pose and light, and leaves labour, illness, sport and ageing in the frame. Both media train viewers how to look at bodies: what may be exposed, what must be covered, whose pain can be shown and whose body serves only as background. Pairing the two sides is not a contest over which is more natural, but a study of how materials write social norms onto the body, and how the painted or photographed person negotiates a posture of their own.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《圣母子与诸圣》》（1225）与《1907年7月7日：女神遊樂廳》（1907）放在一起：相隔约 682 年，一边用蛋彩处理“谁有权决定身体怎样被看见？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Virgin and Child with Saints” (1225) beside “July 7, 1907: Folies Bergère” (1907): about 682 years apart, one works through “Who decides how a body may be seen?” in 蛋彩, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
