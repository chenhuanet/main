肖像Portrait

面孔不是“像不像”，而是身份如何被衣服、姿态、光线和目光做出来。绘画里的肖像与摄影里的肖像，一边在造人，一边在证明人曾经在场。A portrait is not likeness but identity made by dress, posture, light and gaze. Painted portraits construct a person; photographic portraits prove that a person was there.

论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

肖像从来不只是“像不像”。绘画用姿态、衣褶、道具和背景把一个人放进身份关系里，观众看到的是被安排好的角色；摄影则用曝光、裁切和“他确实在场”的证据，把面孔变成档案。两者都在造人，但一个更像在绘制一个人应当如何被记住，另一个更像在证明一个人曾经站在镜头前。把肖像放回六个时间带，会看到身份的标准怎样从宫廷、宗教和家族，转向职业、城市与大众媒体。真正要问的不是哪边更真，而是谁有权决定一张脸的意义，以及被拍的人能否控制自己的形象。A portrait is never only a likeness. Painting places a person inside a web of status through posture, drapery, attribute and setting; photography turns the face into an archive through exposure, cropping and the evidence that someone really stood there. Both construct a person, but one shows how a person should be remembered while the other proves that a person once faced a lens. Across six periods, the standard of identity moves from court, faith and family toward profession, city and mass media. The question is not which medium is truer, but who has the power to define a face and whether the sitter can control the image.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《大日如来（金刚萨埵相）》》（1150）与《1913年5月13日：伊戈爾·西科斯基》（1913）放在一起：相隔约 763 年，一边用蛋彩处理“一张脸如何成为身份的证据？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Tantric Buddha Vairochana as Vajrasattva” (1150) beside “May 13, 1913: Igor Sikorsky” (1913): about 763 years apart, one works through “How does a face become evidence of identity?” in 蛋彩, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
