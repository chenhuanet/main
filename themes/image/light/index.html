光Light

画家不画光本身，他们画光落在什么上面。摄影则把光直接留在化学层和传感器上。Painters do not paint light itself; they paint what light falls on. Photography writes light directly onto chemistry and sensors.

论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

画家不画光本身，他们画光落在什么上面；摄影则把光直接留在化学层和传感器上。两种媒介都让光成为材料，但一个通过颜料和笔触模拟反射，一个通过曝光把时间写进亮度。把绘画与摄影并置，会看到光不只是技术条件，也是等级：圣光、烛光、舞台光、街灯和闪光灯，照亮的对象不同，权力关系也不同。要问的是，谁被光照亮，谁被留在阴影里，以及光是在制造真实感还是在制造信仰。Painters do not paint light itself; they paint what light falls on. Photography writes light directly onto chemistry and sensor. Both make light a material, but one simulates reflection through pigment and brushstroke while the other writes time into brightness through exposure. Pairing the two shows light as more than a technical condition: sacred light, candle, stage light, street lamp and flash illuminate different subjects and different power relations. Ask who is lit, who stays in shadow, and whether light produces realism or belief.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《描绘贵族男子和龙舌兰叶的壁画残片》》（500）与《1878年5月25日：吉爾伯特與薩利文》（1878）放在一起：相隔约 1378 年，一边用壁画处理“谁被光照亮，谁被留在阴影里？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Mural Fragment with Elite Male and Maguey Cactus Leaves” (500) beside “May 25, 1878: Gilbert and Sullivan” (1878): about 1378 years apart, one works through “Who is lit, and who is left in shadow?” in 壁画, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
