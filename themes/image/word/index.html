文字Word

手抄本、书法、版画、题跋、报纸与海报。文字不是说明，它和图像争同一张纸，也决定阅读的顺序。Manuscripts, calligraphy, prints, inscriptions, newspapers and posters. The word is not a caption: it competes for the same sheet and decides the order of reading.

论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

文字不是图像的说明，它和图像争同一张纸。绘画把书法、题跋、经文、印刷和海报放进画面，让阅读顺序参与构图；摄影把报纸、广告、标语、地图和屏幕拍进现场，让照片里同时出现第二层文本。两种媒介都在处理“看”和“读”的冲突。并置时要看，文字是在解释图像、控制图像，还是被图像吞掉；谁有权写字，谁只能被写，往往比画面本身更清楚。The word is not the image's caption; it competes for the same sheet. Painting puts calligraphy, inscription, scripture, print and poster inside the picture, letting reading order enter composition; photography photographs newspaper, advertisement, slogan, map and screen, so a second text appears inside the frame. Both media handle the conflict between looking and reading. The pairing asks whether the word explains, controls or is swallowed by the image; who may write and who can only be written about is often clearer than the picture itself.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《诗篇集圣母诞生细密画》》（1265）与《1909年8月20日：冥王星》（1909）放在一起：相隔约 644 年，一边用蛋彩处理“文字解释图像，还是控制图像？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Miniature Excised from a Psalter: The Nativity” (1265) beside “August 20, 1909: Pluto” (1909): about 644 years apart, one works through “Does the word explain the image or control it?” in 蛋彩, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
