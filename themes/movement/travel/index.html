旅行Travel

山路、航海、朝圣、迁徙与铁路。旅行图像不只是风景，它记录谁离开了哪里，又带着什么回来。Mountain roads, sea routes, pilgrimage, migration and rail. Travel images are not only scenery; they record who left, and what they brought back.

论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

旅行图像不只是风景，它记录谁离开了哪里，又带着什么回来。绘画可以把山路、航海、朝圣和异国写成可收藏的见闻，也可以把旅行的路线变成帝国和贸易的地图；摄影把行李箱、车站、旅馆、海滩和“值得拍”的地标变成可运输的记忆。两种媒介都在把远方变成可接近的商品，也都在选择哪些地方值得被看。并置时要问，旅行者是谁，被看的人是谁，谁有权力把别人的家变成风景。Travel images are not only scenery; they record who left, where and what they brought back. Painting can turn mountain road, sea route, pilgrimage and foreign land into collectable sight, and can turn the route into a map of empire and trade; photography turns suitcase, station, hotel, beach and photographable landmark into transportable memory. Both make distance into an approachable commodity and choose which places deserve to be seen. The pairing asks who travels, who is looked at, and who has the power to turn someone else's home into scenery.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《溪山行旅图》》（1025）与《1878年6月15日：埃德沃德·迈布里奇》（1878）放在一起：相隔约 853 年，一边用绢本水墨处理“谁有权力把别人的家变成风景？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Travelers Among Mountains and Streams” (1025) beside “June 15, 1878: Eadweard Muybridge” (1878): about 853 years apart, one works through “Who has the power to turn another's home into scenery?” in 绢本水墨, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
