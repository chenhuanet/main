夜Night

夜晚不是白天的剩余，而是另一种可见性。绘画用月光、烛火和阴影造出夜，摄影用长曝光、人工光和感光材料把夜留下来；两边都在回答同一个问题：当光不够时，图像还相信什么。Night is not the remainder of day but another visibility. Painting builds night from moonlight, candle and shadow; photography keeps it through long exposure, artificial light and sensitive material. Both answer one question: when light is scarce, what does the image still trust?

论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

夜晚不是白天的剩余，而是另一种可见性。绘画用月光、烛火、阴影和黑色制造夜，让看不清的部分成为想象和恐惧的入口；摄影用长曝光、人工光、闪光和感光材料把夜留下来，让城市、天空和身体在白天看不见的状态下显影。两种媒介都在处理“光不够时还相信什么”。并置时要看，夜是被浪漫化的孤独，还是被灯光照亮的权力地图；谁能在夜里被看见，谁又必须留在黑暗里。Night is not the remainder of day but another visibility. Painting makes night from moonlight, candle, shadow and black, turning what cannot be seen into an entry for imagination and fear; photography keeps night through long exposure, artificial light, flash and sensitive material, developing city, sky and body in states invisible by day. Both ask what can still be trusted when light is scarce. The pairing asks whether night is romantic solitude or a power map lit by lamps, and who may be seen at night while others must remain in darkness.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《渔庄秋霁图》》（1355）与《1893：芝加哥的白色城市》（1893）放在一起：相隔约 538 年，一边用纸本水墨处理“光不够时，图像还相信什么？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Woods and Valleys of Mount Yu” (1355) beside “1893: Chicago's White City” (1893): about 538 years apart, one works through “When light is scarce, what does the image still trust?” in 纸本水墨, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
