海洋Sea

河流、港湾、海难与渡口。水在画面里既是分隔，也是运输、贸易与迁徙的通道。Rivers, harbours, shipwrecks and ferries. Water separates, and also carries trade, migration and passage.

论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

水在画面里既是分隔，也是通道。绘画可以把海画成神怒、贸易、帝国航道或浪漫的无限；摄影把港口、船坞、海难、移民和渡口变成具体的现场。两种媒介都处理水的双重性：它可以连接远方，也可以吞掉船、货物和人。并置时要看，谁被画成航海者，谁被拍成乘客或遇难者；海平线的位置、船的尺度和岸上人群的排列，往往比文字更能说明一个社会怎样想象海洋。Water separates and connects. Painting can turn the sea into divine wrath, trade, imperial route or romantic infinity; photography turns harbour, dock, shipwreck, migration and ferry into specific sites. Both media handle water's double nature: it can link distant places and swallow ship, cargo and people. The pairing asks who is painted as navigator and who is photographed as passenger or casualty; the position of the horizon, the scale of the ship and the arrangement of people on shore often explain a society's imagination of the sea better than any caption.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《城门口西湖泊船》》（1150）与《十二秒：莱特兄弟的第一次动力飞行》（1903）放在一起：相隔约 753 年，一边用绢本设色处理“水连接远方，还是吞掉船上的人？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Boats at Anchor on West Lake at the City Gate” (1150) beside “Twelve seconds: the Wright brothers' first powered flight” (1903): about 753 years apart, one works through “Does water connect distances or swallow those on board?” in 绢本设色, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
