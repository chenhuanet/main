建筑Architecture

宫殿、桥梁、塔、隧道、城市与展馆。建筑把权力、工程和日常空间压进同一个轮廓。Palaces, bridges, towers, tunnels, cities and pavilions. Architecture presses power, engineering and everyday space into one outline.

论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

建筑把权力、工程和日常空间压进同一个轮廓。绘画可以把宫殿、教堂、桥、塔和园林画成秩序的肖像，让透视和比例替赞助人说话；摄影把建筑拍成可测量、可施工、可宣传的对象，也让脚手架、裂缝、住户和拆迁进入画面。两种媒介都在把空间变成可读的结构，但一个常常从外部看整体，一个必须处理内部、使用和失败。并置时要看，建筑图像是在展示谁的秩序，又把谁的生活藏到立面和平面图后面。Architecture presses power, engineering and everyday space into one outline. Painting can turn palace, church, bridge, tower and garden into a portrait of order, letting perspective and proportion speak for the patron; photography makes building measurable, buildable and publicisable, and also brings scaffold, crack, resident and demolition into the frame. Both turn space into readable structure, but one often looks at the whole from outside while the other must deal with interior, use and failure. The pairing asks whose order is displayed and whose life is hidden behind facade and plan.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《杏仁形光环中的基督，带有受难的刑具，下方有圣斯德望和圣老楞佐》》（1225）与《1869：苏伊士运河通航》（1869）放在一起：相隔约 644 年，一边用蛋彩处理“建筑图像展示谁的秩序，藏起谁的生活？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Christ in a Mandorla, with the instruments of the Passion, with St. Stephen and St. Lawrence below” (1225) beside “1869: the Suez Canal opens” (1869): about 644 years apart, one works through “Whose order does architecture display, and whose life does it hide?” in 蛋彩, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
