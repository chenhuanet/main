中南美Latin America

中南美的图像长期被“发现”“开发”和“异国情调”的语言包围。本页把绘画中的殖民地城市、宗教空间和地形，与摄影中的革命、城市、劳工和日常生活并置，看图像如何参与命名和占有。Images of Latin America have long been surrounded by the language of discovery, development and exoticism. This page puts painted colonial city, religious space and terrain beside photographed revolution, city, labour and everyday life, to see how images take part in naming and possession.
