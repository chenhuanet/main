城市City

市场、街巷、港口、宫廷与天际线。城市不是建筑列表，而是交换、相遇和冲突同时发生的地方。Markets, streets, ports, courts and skylines. The city is not a list of buildings but a place where exchange, encounter and conflict happen at once.
