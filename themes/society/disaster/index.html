灾难Disaster

地震、洪水、火灾、沉船与爆炸之后，图像先于语言抵达现场。灾难图像同时是证据、募捐工具、新闻商品和幸存者的记忆；它让远方看见，也可能把痛苦变成可消费的奇观。After earthquake, flood, fire, shipwreck and explosion, images reach the site before language. Disaster images are evidence, fundraising tool, news commodity and survivor memory at once; they show the distant and can turn pain into spectacle.

论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

灾难图像总在事件之后赶到，却常常被当成事件本身。绘画可以把地震、洪水、火灾和沉船放进神意、历史或道德的叙事，让灾难成为可解释的图景；摄影则把废墟、救援和伤亡直接推到观看者面前，让远方的人产生“我在现场”的错觉。两种媒介都可能在灾难中制造意义：谁被拍成受害者，谁被拍成救援者，谁只出现在数字里。并置时要问，图像是在帮助幸存者，还是把他们的痛苦变成新闻和募捐的材料。Disaster images always arrive after the event and are often taken for the event itself. Painting can place earthquake, flood, fire and shipwreck inside providence, history or moral narrative, making catastrophe interpretable; photography pushes ruin, rescue and casualty directly before the viewer and creates the illusion of presence. Both media can produce meaning in disaster: who appears as victim, who as rescuer and who only as a number. The pairing must ask whether the image helps survivors or turns their pain into news and fundraising material.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《占星师预言，新生的王子将在十三岁时遭遇一场灾难，但他将能够克服这场灾难，出自图提·纳玛（鹦鹉记）》》（1555）与《1871年10月8日：芝加哥大火》（1871）放在一起：相隔约 316 年，一边用蛋彩处理“灾难图像帮助幸存者，还是消费痛苦？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “The astrologer predicts a calamity for the newly born prince in his thirteenth year, but one which he would be able to overcome, from a Tuti-nama (Tales of a Parrot): Eighth Night” (1555) beside “October 8, 1871: Great Chicago Fire” (1871): about 316 years apart, one works through “Does a disaster image help survivors or consume pain?” in 蛋彩, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
