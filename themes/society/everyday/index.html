日常Everyday

清晨、正午、黄昏与夜晚；劳动、吃饭、祈祷与睡眠。日常不是背景，它决定人物何时出现、做什么。Dawn, noon, dusk and night; labour, meals, prayer and sleep. The everyday is not background: it decides when figures appear and what they do.
