信仰与仪式Faith and Ritual

宗教图像、仪式空间与神圣叙事。不是教义图解，而是看不同信仰如何造脸、造空间、造光。Religious images, ritual space and sacred narrative: how different faiths make a face, a room and a light.

论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

信仰不可见，图像只能拍它的物质痕迹。绘画用光、金色、空间和身体姿势把神圣叙事变成可接近的视觉秩序；摄影拍教堂、仪式、朝圣和祈祷，把信仰留在建筑、服装、动作和人群里。两种媒介都不是教义的透明图解：它们选择谁可以靠近圣物，谁只能在外围观看，也选择神圣感应当由光、尺度还是人群来制造。并置时要问，图像是在帮助人进入信仰，还是把信仰变成可消费的视觉经验。Faith is invisible; images can only show its material traces. Painting turns sacred narrative into an approachable visual order through light, gold, space and posture; photography records church, ritual, pilgrimage and prayer, leaving faith in building, dress, gesture and crowd. Neither medium is a transparent diagram of doctrine: both choose who may approach the relic and who must watch from outside, and decide whether the sacred is made by light, scale or crowd. The pairing asks whether the image helps people enter faith or turns faith into a consumable visual experience.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《瀑布上方的树上栖息着鸟儿》》（1127）与《1858 年：三座圣所被照进底片》（1858）放在一起：相隔约 731 年，一边用绢本设色处理“图像帮助人进入信仰，还是把信仰变成经验？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Birds on a Tree above a Cataract” (1127) beside “1858 年：三座圣所被照进底片” (1858): about 731 years apart, one works through “Does an image open faith or turn it into experience?” in 绢本设色, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
