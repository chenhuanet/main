家庭Family

谁站在中间、谁抱着孩子、谁在镜头外。家庭照是最私人的档案，也是最规范的形象：它记录亲情，也训练人如何成为“合格”的家庭成员。Who stands at the centre, who holds the child, who stays outside the frame. The family photograph is the most private archive and the most regulated image: it records affection and trains people to be 'proper' family members.

论点：两种媒介怎样回答同一个问题Argument: how two media answer one question

家庭照是最私人的档案，也是最规范的形象。绘画把家庭放进宗教、王朝或市民生活的秩序，用站位、服饰和手势说明谁属于谁；摄影让家庭成员在镜头前重新排队，处理谁抱孩子、谁站中间、谁被裁掉。两种媒介都把亲密关系变成可展示的社会单位。并置时要看，家庭图像在记录亲情的同时，也在训练人怎样成为“合格”的父母、子女和配偶；那些没有进入画面的家庭成员，往往比站在中间的人更能说明问题。The family photograph is the most private archive and the most regulated image. Painting places family inside religious, dynastic or bourgeois order, using position, dress and gesture to show who belongs to whom; photography makes family members queue again before the lens, deciding who holds the child, who stands at the centre and who is cropped out. Both turn intimacy into a displayable social unit. The pairing asks how family images record affection while training people to be proper parents, children and spouses; those left outside the frame often explain more than those standing at the centre.

并置练习Pairing exercises4

不是“画得像照片”，而是同一时期两种观看方式如何互相解释；每组都写明为什么放在一起。Not 'painting looks like photography', but how two ways of looking explain each other in the same period; each pairing states why it is together.

01把《《百子嬉戏图》》（1100）与《1849年10月7日：艾德加·愛倫·坡》（1849）放在一起：相隔约 749 年，一边用绢本设色处理“家庭照记录亲情，还是训练角色？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “One Hundred Children at Play” (1100) beside “October 7, 1849: Edgar Allan Poe” (1849): about 749 years apart, one works through “Does the family photograph record affection or train roles?” in 绢本设色, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
