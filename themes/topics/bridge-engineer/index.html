桥梁与工程Bridges and Engineering

桥梁是最容易被当成“进步”的建筑。绘画可以把桥画成连接、征服或城市全景的一部分；摄影必须处理脚手架、铆钉、工人、河水和通车后的交通。两种媒介都让工程显得宏伟，也都能把事故、劳动和土地被切开的事实留在边缘。The bridge is the building most easily turned into progress. Painting can make it part of connection, conquest or city panorama; photography must deal with scaffold, rivet, worker, river and traffic after opening. Both media make engineering look monumental and both can leave accident, labour and land cut open at the edge of the frame.
