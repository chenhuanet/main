童年与游戏Childhood and Play

儿童图像总被用来象征纯真，也总被成人安排。绘画里的孩子可以是圣婴、继承人或百子图里的吉祥符号；摄影里的孩子必须在镜头前游戏、上学、穿衣和表演童年。两种媒介都在定义“孩子应当是什么样”，也都在暴露成人对未来的期待。Images of children are always asked to symbolise innocence and are always arranged by adults. A painted child can be holy infant, heir or auspicious figure in a hundred-children scroll; a photographed child must play, go to school, dress and perform childhood before the lens. Both media define what a child should be and expose adult expectations of the future.
