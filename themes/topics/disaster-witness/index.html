灾难见证Disaster Witness

灾难图像总在事件之后赶到。绘画可以把地震、洪水、火灾和沉船放进神意、历史或道德叙事，让灾难成为可解释的图景；摄影把废墟、救援和伤亡直接推到观看者面前，让远方的人产生“我在现场”的错觉。本页要问，图像是在帮助幸存者，还是把他们的痛苦变成新闻和募捐的材料。Disaster images always arrive after the event. Painting can place earthquake, flood, fire and shipwreck inside providence, history or moral narrative, making catastrophe interpretable; photography pushes ruin, rescue and casualty before the viewer and creates the illusion of presence. This page asks whether the image helps survivors or turns their pain into news and fundraising material.
