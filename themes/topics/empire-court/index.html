帝国与庆典Empire and Spectacle

帝国需要庆典来证明自己仍然有秩序。绘画把加冕、朝贡、凯旋和宫廷仪式画成可读的等级空间；摄影把博览会、阅兵、殖民展和群众集会拍成可复制的现代奇观。两种媒介都在制造“所有人都在看”的感觉，也都在隐藏谁被排除在庆典之外。Empires need spectacle to prove that order still holds. Painting turns coronation, tribute, triumph and court ritual into a readable hierarchy of space; photography turns exhibition, parade, colonial display and mass rally into a reproducible modern spectacle. Both manufacture the feeling that everyone is watching and hide who is excluded from the celebration.
