家庭相册The Family Album

家庭照看起来最私人，其实最规范。谁站中间、谁抱孩子、谁被裁掉，说明的不只是亲情，也说明一个社会承认什么样的家庭。本页把绘画里的家庭群像与摄影里的家庭相册放在一起，看亲密关系怎样被站位、服装和镜头训练成可展示的单位。The family photograph looks most private and is in fact most regulated. Who stands at the centre, who holds the child and who is cropped out speaks not only of affection but of which family a society recognises. This page puts painted family groups beside photographic family albums to see how intimacy is trained into a displayable unit by position, dress and lens.

怎么看这一页How to read this page

先看两侧选件是否回答同一个问题，再看并置里哪一件承担“证据”、哪一件承担“想象”。专题不是把两站内容拼在一起，而是让两种媒介互相校正。First ask whether both sides answer the same question; then ask which work carries evidence and which carries imagination. A topic is not two sites stitched together but two media correcting each other.
