劳动的手Hands of Labour

手比脸更能说明一个人做什么。绘画可以把劳动的手画成祈祷、耕作、纺织或工匠技艺，让动作进入秩序；摄影可以记录污渍、伤口、工具和疲劳，让劳动变成具体的证据。两种媒介都让劳动被看见，也都可能把劳动者变成类型、符号或宣传形象。Hands say more than faces about what a person does. Painting can turn the hand of labour into prayer, ploughing, weaving or craft, making action part of order; photography can record grime, wound, tool and fatigue, making labour specific evidence. Both media make work visible and both can turn the worker into type, symbol or propaganda image.

Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
