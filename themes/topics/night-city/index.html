夜城Night City

夜城不是白天的城市关了灯，而是另一套可见性。绘画用月光、烛火、窗影和黑色把街道变成想象与恐惧的空间；摄影用长曝光、路灯、霓虹和闪光把夜间的人群、建筑和身体显影。谁能在夜里被看见，谁必须留在黑暗里，是这两条图像传统共同的问题。The night city is not the day city with lights off but another regime of visibility. Painting turns the street into a space of imagination and fear through moonlight, candle, window shadow and black; photography develops crowd, building and body at night through long exposure, street lamp, neon and flash. Who can be seen at night and who must stay in darkness is the shared question of both traditions.
