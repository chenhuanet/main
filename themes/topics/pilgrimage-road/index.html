朝圣与迁徙Pilgrimage and Migration

路不只是风景，它记录谁离开、谁到达、谁被迫上路。绘画可以把朝圣画成信仰的旅程，把迁徙画成队伍、道路或远方；摄影把车票、行李、边境、难民营和到达时的脸变成现场。两种媒介都在把移动变成故事，也都在决定谁有资格被看成“旅行者”。A road is not only scenery; it records who leaves, who arrives and who is forced to move. Painting can turn pilgrimage into a journey of faith and migration into procession, road or distance; photography turns ticket, luggage, border, refugee camp and the face of arrival into site. Both turn movement into story and decide who qualifies as a traveller.
