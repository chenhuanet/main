铁路与速度Rail and Speed

铁路把距离压成时刻表，也把工业的速度带进画面。绘画用蒸汽、铁桥、车站和烟囱表现现代性的崇高与不安；摄影把轨道、机车、隧道、工程和乘客变成可验证的记录。两种媒介都在处理同一个新经验：世界突然变快，风景突然可以被精确测量。Rail compresses distance into a timetable and brings industrial speed into the picture. Painting uses steam, iron bridge, station and chimney to express the sublime and anxiety of modernity; photography turns rail, locomotive, tunnel, engineering and passenger into verifiable record. Both handle one new experience: the world suddenly accelerates and landscape becomes precisely measurable.
