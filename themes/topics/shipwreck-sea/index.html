海难与救援Shipwreck and Rescue

海难图像同时是灾难、英雄主义和商业新闻。绘画可以把沉船画成神罚、浪漫的崇高或帝国航海的失败；摄影把救生艇、残骸、幸存者和失踪名单变成可验证的现场。两种媒介都在处理海的不可控，也都在选择谁被救、谁被记住、谁只留在数字里。Shipwreck images are disaster, heroism and commercial news at once. Painting can turn the wreck into divine punishment, romantic sublime or the failure of imperial navigation; photography turns lifeboat, debris, survivor and missing list into verifiable site. Both handle the sea's uncontrollability and choose who is rescued, who is remembered and who remains only a number.
