体育与身体Sport and the Body

体育图像把身体变成可比较、可训练、可欢呼的对象。绘画可以画角力、赛马、舞蹈和竞技，让身体进入英雄或节庆的叙事；摄影用快门、连拍和电视画面把肌肉、汗水、胜负和伤病变成可验证的瞬间。两种媒介都在规定什么样的身体值得被看见。Sport turns the body into something comparable, trainable and cheerable. Painting can show wrestling, horse racing, dance and contest, placing the body inside heroic or festive narrative; photography uses shutter, burst and television to make muscle, sweat, victory and injury verifiable instants. Both prescribe which bodies deserve to be seen.
