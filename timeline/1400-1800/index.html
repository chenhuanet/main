世界与图像World and Image

透视、印刷、宫廷和山水同时扩张，图像开始组织一个更大的世界。这一期看绘画如何把空间变成可测量、可拥有、可展示的秩序，也看版画和书籍怎样让图像离开原来的墙面。摄影仍未出现，但“可复制的图像”已经改变了权力和知识流通。Perspective, print, court and landscape expand together, and images begin to organise a larger world. This period looks at how painting turned space into measurable, ownable and displayable order, and how print and book let images leave the wall. Photography has not appeared, but the reproducible image has already changed the circulation of power and knowledge.
