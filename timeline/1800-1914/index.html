时代问题Period question

工业与城市Industry and the City

铁路、蒸汽、街道和摄影术同时出现，速度第一次被写进画面。绘画还在处理工业的崇高与城市的陌生感，摄影已经把工程、殖民地、肖像和日常生活变成可复制的证据。两种媒介都在学习同一件事：世界变快了，观看也必须变快。Rail, steam, street and photography appear together, and speed enters the picture for the first time. Painting is still handling the sublime of industry and the strangeness of the city; photography has already turned engineering, colony, portrait and everyday life into reproducible evidence. Both media learn one thing: the world has accelerated, and looking must accelerate too.

并置Pairings2

01把《《1808年5月3日》》（1814）与《1858 年：三座圣所被照进底片》（1858）放在一起：相隔约 44 年，一边用油画（布面）处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “The Third of May 1808” (1814) beside “1858 年：三座圣所被照进底片” (1858): about 44 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 油画（布面）, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
