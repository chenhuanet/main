时代问题Period question

太空与消费Space and Consumption

太空、电视、彩色印刷和消费社会让图像离开地面，也进入每个家庭的屏幕。绘画在抽象、波普和观念之间重新定义作品，摄影把冷战、航天、广告、街头和彩色底片变成日常视觉经验。两种媒介都面对一个新问题：当图像太多，什么还值得被相信。Space, television, colour print and consumer society lift images off the ground and into every household screen. Painting redefines the work between abstraction, pop and concept; photography turns Cold War, spaceflight, advertisement, street and colour film into everyday visual experience. Both face a new question: when there are too many images, what still deserves belief.

01把《《威茅斯湾，胜利日庆典》》（1946）与《1950年6月26日：道格拉斯DC-4》（1950）放在一起：相隔约 4 年，一边用水彩、石墨、钢笔与墨水处理“同一时期，两种媒介如何回答同一个问题？”，另一边用摄影给出另一种回答。先看两幅画面怎样安排人、空间和光，再问谁有权拍摄、谁被安排进画面；差异不在像不像，而在证据与想象的分工。Place “Weymouth Bay, Victory Day Celebrations” (1946) beside “June 26, 1950: Douglas DC-4” (1950): about 4 years apart, one works through “How do two media answer one question in the same period?” in 水彩、石墨、钢笔与墨水, while the photograph answers otherwise. Read how each arranges people, space and light, then ask who had the right to photograph and who was placed in the frame. The difference is not likeness but the division between evidence and imagination.
