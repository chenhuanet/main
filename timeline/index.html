六个时期，每期先问一个时代问题Six periods, one question each

时间线不铺全量作品。1839 年以后的时期按 4 件绘画、4 件摄影、2 组并置组织；1839 年前只放绘画 4–6 件，摄影侧明确写“摄影术尚未出现”，不配图。The timeline does not lay out every work. After 1839 each period is organised as 4 paintings, 4 photographs and 2 pairings; before 1839 it shows 4–6 paintings and marks the photo side as 'photography has not yet appeared', with no image.

400–1400
6 画 / 0 影 / 0 并置6 paintings / 0 photos / 0 pairs

信仰与手抄本Faith and the Manuscript

在摄影出现之前，图像如何把不可见的世界放进可携带的页面？这一期从宗教图像、写本、壁画和朝圣物出发，看绘画怎样用金色、光线和身体姿势制造神圣感。这里没有摄影，也不硬造摄影；要问的是，当图像是信仰的主要载体时，谁有权生产它、复制它、携带它。Before photography, how did images put the invisible world onto a portable page? This period starts from religious image, manuscript, mural and pilgrimage object, and asks how painting made the sacred through gold, light and posture. There is no photography here and none is invented; the question is who had the right to produce, copy and carry images when they were the main vehicle of faith.

1400–1800

世界与图像World and Image

透视、印刷、宫廷和山水同时扩张，图像开始组织一个更大的世界。这一期看绘画如何把空间变成可测量、可拥有、可展示的秩序，也看版画和书籍怎样让图像离开原来的墙面。摄影仍未出现，但“可复制的图像”已经改变了权力和知识流通。Perspective, print, court and landscape expand together, and images begin to organise a larger world. This period looks at how painting turned space into measurable, ownable and displayable order, and how print and book let images leave the wall. Photography has not appeared, but the reproducible image has already changed the circulation of power and knowledge.

1800–1914
4 画 / 4 影 / 2 并置4 paintings / 4 photos / 2 pairs

工业与城市Industry and the City

铁路、蒸汽、街道和摄影术同时出现，速度第一次被写进画面。绘画还在处理工业的崇高与城市的陌生感，摄影已经把工程、殖民地、肖像和日常生活变成可复制的证据。两种媒介都在学习同一件事：世界变快了，观看也必须变快。Rail, steam, street and photography appear together, and speed enters the picture for the first time. Painting is still handling the sublime of industry and the strangeness of the city; photography has already turned engineering, colony, portrait and everyday life into reproducible evidence. Both media learn one thing: the world has accelerated, and looking must accelerate too.

1914–1945
4 画 / 4 影 / 2 并置4 paintings / 4 photos / 2 pairs

战争与大众War and the Masses

战争、宣传、新闻和大众印刷把图像变成动员与见证的工具。绘画用碎裂、抽象和表现主义处理创伤，摄影把前线、废墟、阅兵和难民放进报纸与档案。两种媒介都必须在国家宣传和个人见证之间选择位置，也都可能被审查、摆拍和重新配文。War, propaganda, news and mass print turn images into tools of mobilisation and witness. Painting handles trauma through fracture, abstraction and expressionism; photography puts frontline, ruin, parade and refugee into newspaper and archive. Both media must choose a position between national propaganda and personal witness, and both can be censored, staged and recaptioned.

1945–1990
4 画 / 4 影 / 2 并置4 paintings / 4 photos / 2 pairs

太空与消费Space and Consumption

太空、电视、彩色印刷和消费社会让图像离开地面，也进入每个家庭的屏幕。绘画在抽象、波普和观念之间重新定义作品，摄影把冷战、航天、广告、街头和彩色底片变成日常视觉经验。两种媒介都面对一个新问题：当图像太多，什么还值得被相信。Space, television, colour print and consumer society lift images off the ground and into every household screen. Painting redefines the work between abstraction, pop and concept; photography turns Cold War, spaceflight, advertisement, street and colour film into everyday visual experience. Both face a new question: when there are too many images, what still deserves belief.

1990–2026
4 画 / 4 影 / 2 并置4 paintings / 4 photos / 2 pairs

数字与全球Digital and Global

数字文件、卫星、网络和全球展览让图像可以复制、传输、再编辑。绘画面对扫描、打印、屏幕和算法，摄影面对手机、社交平台、卫星和生成式图像。两种媒介都不再能靠材料边界定义自己；真正的问题是，谁控制文件、谁拥有平台、谁还能确认一张图像来自哪里。Digital file, satellite, network and global exhibition let images be copied, transmitted and re-edited. Painting faces scan, print, screen and algorithm; photography faces phone, social platform, satellite and generated image. Neither medium can define itself by material boundary any more; the real question is who controls the file, who owns the platform and who can still confirm where an image came from.
