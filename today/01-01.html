《女史箴图》Admonitions of the Instructress to the Court Ladies

《女史箴图》是现存最早的中国绢本人物长卷之一，通常被认为是东晋顾恺之的原作、唐代摹本。画卷根据张华《女史箴》的十二段箴文绘制，现存九段：有冯媛挡熊、班姬辞辇，也有宫中女子对镜梳妆、围坐交谈的日常场景。人物衣带飘举，线条细而不断，像春蚕吐丝。Admonitions of the Instructress to the Court Ladies is among the earliest surviving Chinese handscrolls of figure painting, generally regarded as a Tang-dynasty copy after Gu Kaizhi. It illustrates twelve passages from Zhang Hua's Admonitions in the form of nine surviving scenes: Lady Feng shielding the emperor from a bear, Lady Ban declining the imperial carriage, and court ladies at their toilet or seated in conversation. Draperies lift and flow; lines are fine and unbroken, like silk spun by a silkworm.
