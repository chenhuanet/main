《马拉之死》The Death of Marat

《马拉之死》把一位被刺杀的政治人物画成了殉道者。马拉死在浴缸里，身体沿对角线滑下，右手还握着羽毛笔，左手拿着一张写着求助者名字的纸；木箱上放着墨水瓶、纸和一把刀。背景是一片空白的暗色，没有街道，没有凶手。The Death of Marat turns an assassinated politician into a martyr. Marat lies dead in his bath, body sliding along the diagonal, quill still in his right hand, a petition with a supplicant's name in his left; on the wooden block sit inkwell, papers and a knife. The background is a blank dark field, with no street and no assassin.
