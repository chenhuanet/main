《黄山三十六峰图》Thirty-six Peaks of Mount Huang Recollected

《黄山三十六峰图》是石涛的一幅晚年挂轴作品，凭记忆创作完成。画面层次向上堆叠：前景有松树和茅屋，中景有巨石、溪流和云雾，高峰几乎延伸至画面顶端。干湿笔交替使用，墨色由浓黑到浅灰，如同被雾气冲洗；右上方有一段长款，记载了作画的缘由。Thirty-six Peaks of Mount Huang Recollected is a late hanging scroll by Shitao, painted from memory. The composition stacks upward: pines and a thatched hut in the foreground, boulders, stream and cloud in the middle, peak heads receding almost to the top edge. Wet and dry brush alternate, ink running from dense black to pale grey as if washed by mist; a long inscription at the upper right records the occasion.
