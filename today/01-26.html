《自画像（二十八岁）》Self-Portrait at the Age of 28

《自画像（二十八岁）》，阿尔布雷希特·丢勒，1500，北方文艺复兴，慕尼黑·老绘画陈列馆。In 1500 Dürer painted himself frontally, symmetrically, staring directly at the viewer: long hair and beard falling to either side like a Christ figure, his right hand laid across his chest. The black ground makes face and golden hair luminous, and the fur collar is painted hair by hair. The Latin inscription and his "AD" monogram appear at the right, dated at the threshold of a new century.
