《下十字架》The Descent from the Cross

《下十字架》，彼得·保罗·鲁本斯，1612–1614，巴洛克，安特卫普圣母大教堂。Rubens's Descent from the Cross, the central panel of a triptych for Antwerp Cathedral, shows Christ's body sliding diagonally from the cross, caught in a white sheet. Joseph of Arimathea and Nicodemus haul the cloth from above, John receives the feet below, and the Virgin, Mary Magdalene and other women kneel at the left. The figures form a rotating diamond, light running along the sheet and across muscle.
