《草地上的午餐》Le Déjeuner sur l'herbe

《草地上的午餐》把一个裸体女人和两个完全穿着的男人放在巴黎郊外的草地上。女人坐在左边，膝盖弯起，目光直视画外；男人们在聊天，似乎毫不在意。另一名女子在后面的溪边弯腰。光透过树叶洒下，人物看起来像是贴在同一个平面上。Le Déjeuner sur l'herbe places a nude woman and two fully dressed men on grass outside Paris. The woman sits at the left, knees drawn up, looking straight out of the picture; the men talk, apparently unconcerned. Another woman bends by the stream behind. Light falls through leaves, and the figures seem pasted onto a single plane.
