《朱迪斯斩首荷罗孚尼》Judith Beheading Holofernes

《朱迪斯斩首荷罗孚尼》，卡拉瓦乔，约1598–1599，巴洛克，罗马·巴贝里尼宫。Caravaggio turns the biblical assassination into a murder at the bedside. Judith stands on the right, one hand gripping Holofernes' hair, the other holding the sword; her face recoils, brows knitted, disgust and fear together. The general's body twists on the bed, mouth open, blood jetting from the neck onto white sheets. The maid Abra waits at the left, bag in hand, tense.
