《画室》The Painter's Studio

《画室》的副标题是"概括我七年艺术与道德生活的真实寓言"。画面中央，库尔贝坐在画架前画一幅风景，身边站着裸体模特和一个看画的男孩；右侧是他的朋友与支持者，波德莱尔、普鲁东、尚弗勒里等人被一一安排；左侧是来自日常生活的普通人——工人、农民、乞丐、寡妇。The Painter's Studio carries the subtitle "A real allegory summing up seven years of my artistic and moral life." At the centre, Courbet sits at his easel painting a landscape, a nude model and a boy beside him. On the right are his friends and supporters — Baudelaire, Proudhon, Champfleury and others; on the left, ordinary people from daily life: workers, peasants, beggars, a widow.
