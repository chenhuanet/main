《夜巡》The Night Watch

《夜巡》实际上并不是夜晚的场景。一支民兵队伍正从阿姆斯特丹的拱门里出发。队长弗朗斯·班宁克·考克穿着黑色外套，系着红色腰带，正伸手去碰他的中尉威廉·范·鲁伊滕堡的手套，中尉穿着黄色，手指着画框之外的长矛。一些人在打鼓，有些人在升旗，有些人在操纵武器；人群中间被光照到的小女孩格外显眼。The Night Watch is not actually a night scene. A militia company marches out of an Amsterdam archway. Captain Frans Banninck Cocq, in black with a red sash, reaches towards the glove of his lieutenant, Willem van Ruytenburch, who wears yellow and points a lance beyond the frame. Some men drum, some raise a flag, some handle weapons; a small girl is picked out by light in the middle of the crowd.
