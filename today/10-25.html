《清明上河图》Along the River During the Qingming Festival

《清明上河图》画的是北宋都城汴京清明时节的市井全景。长卷从郊外的柳林与驮队开始，经过汴河上的虹桥，进入城门与街市：船夫、脚夫、商贩、说书人、轿子、骆驼、酒楼、药铺，数百个人物各有动作，连桥上摊贩的遮阳伞都被一一交代。Along the River During the Qingming Festival presents a panoramic view of the Northern Song capital Bianjing at the Qingming season. The scroll begins in willows and pack teams outside the city, passes the Rainbow Bridge over the Bian River, and enters gates and markets: boatmen, porters, vendors, storytellers, sedan chairs, camels, taverns, pharmacies — hundreds of figures, each with an action, down to the parasols of bridge-side hawkers.
