《溪山行旅图》Travelers Among Mountains and Streams

《溪山行旅图》是北宋山水画的巅峰之作。一座巨大的中央山峰向前凸出，山顶隐没在雾中；右侧有瀑布垂下，山脚下有一条小路，驮驴队和两个旅人正沿路前行。人物几乎看不见，被放在画面下方，以衬托山的高大。Travelers Among Mountains and Streams is the summit of Northern Song landscape. A vast central peak presses forward, its summit lost in mist; a waterfall falls at the right, and at the foot a path carries a train of pack donkeys and two travelers. The figures are almost invisible, placed low in the picture to measure the mountain's height.
