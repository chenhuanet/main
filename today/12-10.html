《逐出伊甸园》The Expulsion from the Garden of Eden

马萨乔在佛罗伦萨布兰卡奇礼拜堂的《逐出伊甸园》里，把亚当与夏娃的羞耻画成了身体的重量。两人赤裸着从右侧被天使驱出，夏娃仰头张口，双手捂住下体；亚当低头以臂遮面，脚掌踏在真实的阴影里。画面几乎没有风景，只有一道拱门和一小片地面，却因人体结构、透视缩短和强烈明暗而显得异常具体。In the Brancacci Chapel in Florence, Masaccio made shame a matter of bodily weight. Adam and Eve, naked, are driven out from the right; Eve lifts her head and opens her mouth, her hands covering her sex, while Adam bows and hides his face behind his arm, his feet planted in real shadow. There is almost no landscape, only an archway and a strip of ground, yet anatomy, foreshortening and strong chiaroscuro make the scene unusually concrete.
